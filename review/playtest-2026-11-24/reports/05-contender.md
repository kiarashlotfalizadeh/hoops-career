# 05 — The Win-Now Contender
## Who I am and what I played
Oklahoma City Twisters, real league, GM + Head Coach, job security on. 390x844 phone viewport, 2D court (3D off). I played 4 full seasons (2026-27 to 2029-30), about 330 games. I coached 12 playoff games live: all of R1 vs PHX (4-0), 3 of 4 in R2 vs SAS, and the last 7 games of the Finals vs BOS (4-3). Regular-season live games: 1 (Nov 20 vs NOP). Roughly 4 hours of wall time. Most of it went to driving the headless browser, not to the game.

Caveat: after season 1 I auto-piloted seasons 2-4 with big sims and staff defaults. Season 3 is partly the result of my own neglect.

## Play log
1. **Day one (Oct 2026).** Owner: win about 64, reach the Finals, payroll under $232.7M. Forecast 71 wins. The "first games" checklist is clear. Start to 13-2 took 1 sim month and about 40 s.
2. **Nov 20, first live game vs NOP.** I lost 120-124 as a 78% favourite. The halftime report was the best screen I saw: "looks +22.5, shooting luck -13.5, free throws -13 = -4". I changed help defense to Stay home. The report logged it as "Your moves -1.4 pts, OKC 61-65 since". That honest accounting is the game's signature. NOP shot 37-40 from the line against our 24-28, and SGA had 0 rebounds in 40 minutes, which looked odd.
3. **Feb 2027 deadline.** I tried to trade for Jalen Johnson (88, 25) and then for Alexander-Walker (81, 28). Four 1sts, one of them projected top-6, for Johnson drew "Not even close". Two firsts for an 81 drew "They want a lot more". The GM is "Shark: wins the trade or walks". My picks felt worthless, so I stood pat.
4. **2026-27 regular season: 64-18, 1st seed West.** It matched the owner's 64 almost exactly. Hartenstein got a stress fracture (out 51 games), then Jalen Williams a groin strain.
5. **Playoffs 2027.**
   - R1 PHX: swept 4-0.
   - R2 SAS: won 4-0 in the quick sims.
   - WCF LAL: 4-2.
   - Finals BOS: 4-3. I lost G1 by 1 and G4 by 10, won G5 by 10, lost G6 111-117 (the box score called it "about a 1-in-220 shooting night"), then won G7 115-114.
   - It felt like a Finals. I adjusted at halftime (Ice, Crash glass) and the report told me whether it worked.
6. **Title aftermath.** I got no full-screen champion moment on my path. Titles arrived as inbox mail ("Your first title", "Champions") and a history-page badge ("3 titles", counting the real 2025 title before this league). Owner: season +50 (64 wins +5, title +60, payroll +27). Job security went 59 to 75. The owner then cut the next ask to "win a playoff round" with the payroll limit up to $269.6M.
7. **Summer 2027, the tax shock.** Payroll was $253M against a $213M tax line. The tax bill showed as $117.7M. A $12.8M LaVine signing (80 OVR, with the mid-level) would add $65.4M of tax, so I skipped him. Letting Wallace, K. Williams and Tilly walk was the only sane move. The cap and tax screens explain themselves well, but the marginal rate (about 5x) is brutal.
8. **2027-28: 59-23, 2nd seed.** Jalen Williams missed 24 games, Mitchell 38. I won R1 vs HOU in 7 (G7 116-115), then lost R2 to DEN in 6 (they won 138-120 and 149-133). Owner season +11 (62 wins asked, 59 won, -15), standing +34.
9. **2028-29: 41-41, 7th seed.** Holmgren, Hartenstein and SGA all missed time, and I left the roster at 12/15 and ignored "Sign cover" cards. An 8-game losing streak followed, and Dee Okafor wrote "go get a star". I still won the play-in, beat DAL in 6 as the 7 seed and lost R2 to LAL. Owner -10 (54 wins asked, 41 won, -65). Job security 58, "steady". I was not close to fired.
10. **2029-30: 56-26, 2nd, lost the conference finals.** SGA (95, 31) scored 62 in a playoff game, a "league playoff record since 1996-97", which was a nice moment. Owner +38, security 63. Two job offers elsewhere were waiting.

## What hooked me
1. **The post-game accounting.** "How the margin adds up: looks +19.3 · shooting luck -21.3 · free throws -4" after the G6 loss let me lose a Finals game without feeling robbed.
2. **A Finals that went 7 and was decided by a point.** Game 1 was lost by 1, Game 6 was a 1-in-220 shooting night, and Game 7 was 115-114. No script was visible. I felt the stakes because the same engine that beat me also saved me.
3. **Coaching the series changes the screen.** Halftime moves show up as "Your moves... OKC 61-65 since", so I could check whether I was right.
4. **Cap and tax honesty.** The re-sign screen shows "Keep everyone $271.0M, tax ≈ $211.1M" before you commit, and each FA sheet shows "27-28 tax +$65.4M".
5. **A world that moves.** Players rise and fall (Chambers 81/92 at 20), the league has trades graded in the news, and the owner's ask resets after a title.

## What pushed me away
1. **Trades are nearly impossible for a contender (major).** Four 1sts for an 88 and two 1sts for an 81 both got a flat no. Picks in this game buy nothing, so "trade picks for help" is a dead lever. Evidence: ATL replies "Not even close" and "They want a lot more" in the Feb 2027 trade UI.
2. **The tax is a hard cap in disguise (major).** A $12.8M signing costs $65M of tax, and keeping everyone would cost $211M. Good tension, but there is no lever between "keep a 253M roster" and "let your depth walk". I lost Wallace (77/80, 23) for nothing because of the 5x marginal rate.
3. **Scoring is inflated, especially in the playoffs (major for a hardcore fan).** The sample is 118-133 in R1 G2 and 149-133 in the conference semis. Combined scores of 250 in playoff games do not feel like playoff basketball. The Finals games (97-121, 114-119) read better.
4. **Owner pressure is soft even when you collapse (minor to major).** A 41-41 season graded -10 with security 58 "steady", and the owner then asked for only "a playoff round" after a title. A contender should fear the hot seat. It never came.
5. **Auto-pilot punishes silently (minor).** Staff calls ("Sign cover", "ride it out") are prompts, but unanswered they still sim on. The 41-41 season came from stars missing games and a thin roster. The game was honest, but it never stopped me to say "you are 12/15 with three starters hurt".

## Against the genre
- **vs Football Manager:** the "why" reports are closer to FM's match analysis than anything in basketball. Tactics are less deep than FM's (no set-piece-like library), but the series game plan, "Double a star" and "Hack-a-player" plans are the right shape.
- **vs Basketball GM:** BBGM has a far more forgiving trade AI and a cleaner dynasty loop. Hoops Career has better live coaching and far better explanations, but worse trade freedom.
- **vs NBA 2K MyGM:** the owner and cap screens are cleaner than 2K's, but there is no pageantry (no title parade, no ring ceremony).
- **vs Hoop Land / Draft Day:** it reads more like a real front office. Cap and tax math is honest. It lacks their roster-churn fun.

## Would I pay and keep playing
$7.99 on a phone: yes, easily. The title run alone justified it. $14.99 on Steam: maybe, only if trades and the title moment improve. Hours I'd put in: about 30 to 40. Years 3-4 pulled me through injuries and aging stars, but nothing in the front office gave me a goal beyond "keep the roster legal". Recommend to a friend: 7.5/10.

## The one change that would most raise my score
Make a contender's trade market real: let late picks and young depth buy a real rotation player at the deadline, or give the tax a smoother slope. Right now the only way to improve a champion is to let it decay, and years 3-4 felt like managing decline rather than chasing rings.
