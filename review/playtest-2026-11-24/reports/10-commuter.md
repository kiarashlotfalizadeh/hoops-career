# 10 — The Phone Commuter

## Who I am and what I played
Oklahoma City Twisters (real league, West), GM + Head Coach. 1 full season (82 games, play-in skipped as 1 seed, playoffs lost in conf finals) plus the 2027 offseason and the first 16 games of 2027-28 (14-2). About 40 separate launches ("sessions"): 375x667 for most, 390x844 for a few, one tablet (820x1180), one desktop (1280x800). One live game coached in four sittings (OKC 139, IND 116, two closes mid-game). Real time spent: about 2 hours of tapping; the game itself is fast, since a full season sims in about 30 seconds in total.

## Play log
- **First launch (Oct 2026).** Career creation took 3.6 s. Today greets me with a hero card (owner goals: "Win about 64 games, Reach the Finals, payroll under $232.7M") and a 4-step first-games checklist. One tap per step (rotation, playbook, plan, live game). Learnable in 2 minutes. Good hook.
- **Playbook sheet.** It rates each playbook by fit ("Pace & Space: Yours", "Grit & Grind: Worse fit"). A real choice in 2 taps, no jargon wall.
- **Offline.** `setOffline(true)` after load, then Team, Free agents and Start season all worked. A cold start with the browser already offline loaded in 218 ms and played a game plus a week. Full marks. No other sim I have played does this.
- **Close mid-sim.** I closed 4 days into "Sim 1 month". Reopening put me back on Oct 27, before the sim, with no corruption and no half-state. The progress is lost, but it is safe. A 3-second sim makes that painless.
- **While you were away (Oct 27).** After a week sim, a sheet showed the week's results, a "Needs you" card (Hartenstein out 27 games: Ride it out / Look for a trade) and the staff's call. This is the best thing for a commuter. I could be back in the game in 10 seconds.
- **Trade offers (Nov 16).** Three offers: McCain for Wells, Wallace (torn meniscus, out 30) for Wiggins, and Hartenstein+Caruso for Trae Young ($49.5M to 2030, tax +$19.8M, "In your favor"). Each came with payroll-after and tax figures, a staff read and "For later, you give up: McCain, 22, still growing". I turned all three down in 3 taps. Real decisions with real numbers.
- **Live game (Nov 23, @IND).** The game asks "Watch sideways / Keep upright" (broadcast layout). Upright shows half the court on a phone. Real-time pace is about 2x game speed, so a full game would be about 20 minutes. Pause reveals Resume / 2x / Key plays / Skip, which is the commuter's lifeline. Skip ahead offers End of Q2, Clutch time and Sim to final, and it stopped itself when SGA got hurt. I closed the app at 9:41 Q1 and reopened at 9:20 Q1, in the same game. I finished by "Sim to final" (139-116). The report is excellent: a "how the margin adds up" line (looks +10.4, luck +6.6, FT +6).
- **Backup.** Export gave a .json.gz of 1.1 MB. Import gave "Restore this backup? Restore over it / Keep both". Restored to Nov 24, 15-3, no errors. Done well.
- **Deadline (Feb 6-11).** A staff call: "Trade for Stephen Curry (87, age 38, $178M over 3 years) for J. Williams + Caruso. The forecast barely moves your chances." Two taps to a confirm. I stood pat. I liked that the staff argued against itself with numbers.
- **Season end.** 67-15, a 14-game win streak, Coach of the Year. Whole run from the deadline to Apr 12 took 4 s. League stats look believable (Doncic 32.5 ppg, SGA 30.1, Jokic MVP, Wemby 3.8 blk).
- **Playoffs (Apr 18).** "Sim to end of playoffs" ran the whole postseason in 2.7 s and never stopped. SGA got hurt in the West Finals and I lost 4-2 to SAS without a single input. The summary said "The staff put in Hedge coverage for Game 6". Settings promise a stop for "a playoff series" and "a starter hurt". I never got either. Biggest miss of my run.
- **Offseason.** Lottery, then Draft (pick #6 via LAC: "Take the staff's pick", then "Auto-draft the rest", 1.4 s). Then Re-sign: payroll $253.0M, tax line $213.1M, tax bill $115.7M, with a clear cap/tax bar. Wallace's ask was $13.7M x 5 and "27-28 tax +$70.4M". I let him walk. I opened FA with a confirm sheet, skipped the 30 days, and started 2027-28. FA and the re-sign tax are properly bleak.
- **2027-28.** The preseason forced "Release 1 player" (Jace Banks, $9.2M dead money). The Start button is disabled until I do it, and the bar says why. Season 2: 14-2 after a month, with one 31-point loss at CHA.

## What hooked me (top 5)
1. **Offline and resume are real.** Cold-start offline loaded in 218 ms. A close mid-sim and a close mid-live-game both resumed cleanly (Oct 27 and Q1 9:20).
2. **"While you were away" sheet.** Results, the stretch's moment, Needs you, trade offers and injuries in one scroll. A 10-minute session always starts with a decision.
3. **Honest decision cards.** Every trade shows payroll-after and tax (Young: +$19.8M tax). The staff argues against its own pick (Curry), with age and $178M.
4. **Fast sims.** Week about 1.5 s, month 3 s, to deadline 5.7 s, to season end 4 s, playoffs 2.7 s.
5. **Backup and restore.** One screen, "Restore over it / Keep both". It made me trust the save.

## What pushed me away (top 5)
1. **Major: "Sim to end of playoffs" ignores stops.** It ran 15 playoff games, lost a star (SGA) in the conference finals and I could not act. Settings say sims stop for "a playoff series" and "a starter hurt".
2. **Major: a live game is long.** About 2x game speed, so a full game is about 20 real minutes at 1x. Fine with Skip, but the default asks for more than a commute stop.
3. **Major: bottom chrome is heavy at 375x667.** Continue bar (62 px) plus the nav (54 px) is 17% of the screen. On Today at 667 high, "Ride it out" / "Look for a trade" sat at y=561, under the bar until I scrolled.
4. **Minor: small taps.** "?" explain icons 22x22, slider -/+ 38x38, coverage buttons 36 px high, Decline/Exercise 34 px, Negotiate 34 px. Every one is under 44 px.
5. **Minor: tablet and desktop are a stretched phone.** 820x1180 is the phone layout with a huge empty gap above the bar. Desktop gets a centred column, but Tactics does use two columns. Nothing I would choose a bigger screen for.

## Bugs (repro steps, screenshot path)
- **Playoff sim does not stop for a hurt star or a series.** Repro: Playoffs, Play options, "Sim to end of playoffs", with SGA hurt in the West Finals. Result: no stop, series lost 2-4. See `shots/s32-sheet0.png`.
- **Guide step state.** Tactics step 1 "Set your rotation" never ticked even after I tapped "Keep these five". Today still says "1 of 4 done" with rotation "(next)" while the playbook is "(done)". See `shots/s08-a.png`.
- **Counters stuck on "Staff still looking…"** on the game-plan screen (Drop, Switch, 2-3 zone, Pack the paint) from preseason through week 1. See `shots/s05-b.png`.
- **Score count-up read as the final.** The Game report page text read "138 | 123 | 115 | 102 | Won by 23" while the headline showed 116-97, which is a count-up animation. A screen reader or the "Share" text could capture the intermediate numbers. `shots/s07-b.png`.
- **Stale injury line.** After a 1-week sim the old game report stayed open and showed "Hurt tonight: Jalen Williams, back now". It mutates the old report. Minor.
- **Release staff pick takes two taps.** The first tap turns the label into "Confirm:" with no obvious change. I tapped twice before noticing, and the Start button stayed disabled until I did. It could be clearer.

## Against the genre (FM / BBGM / MyNBA / Hoop Land)
- **Versus Basketball GM:** BBGM is the sim speed benchmark and offline PWA. This matches both, then adds a staff that argues in plain language and a post-game "luck vs looks" breakdown. BBGM has no tactics screen worth the name.
- **Versus Football Manager:** FM's mobile version is pause-and-play and heavy. Hoops Career is better for a 10-minute session because every sim lands on a decision sheet. It lacks FM's slow burn and FM's inbox as story.
- **Versus MyNBA/2K:** nowhere near the polish for casual play, but a far better phone game offline and in 10 minutes.
- **Versus Hoop Land and Draft Day Sports:** the trade and CBA clarity is a step above (tax shown in every offer).

## Would I pay and keep playing
- **$7.99 phone unlock:** Yes. Offline, save safety and the session loop are exactly my needs.
- **$14.99 on Steam:** Probably not for me. The big screen adds nothing today, so I would want a desktop layout.
- **Hours:** about 25 to 40 hours over a few months of commutes. The first season is easy for a contender, so I would take a weaker team on the second run.
- **Recommend to a friend:** 8/10.

## The one change that would most raise my score
Make every long sim, playoffs included, honor the "stop for what needs you" rule: a hurt star, a series start or a series-ending game. Right now the best phone loop in the game (sim, then a decision) is broken in the one place with the highest stakes, so I lost the conference finals without a single input.
