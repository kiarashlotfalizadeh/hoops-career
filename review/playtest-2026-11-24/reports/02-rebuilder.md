# 02 — The Rebuilder
## Who I am and what I played
Chicago Stampede (real league, East), ranked 30th of 30 on the New League screen. GM + Head Coach, job security on. Phone viewport 390x844, 3D court off.
- I played five full seasons, 2026-27 to 2030-31, about 410 regular-season games, all simmed except one live game (vs LAL, Nov 2028) that I watched, then skipped to the final.
- I ran four drafts myself and picked at #4, #6, #9, then staff picks at #22 and later.
- I ran five offseasons: re-signing, free agency, rookie-scale options.
- About 4 hours of play, mostly scripted clicks with screenshots of the important screens.

## Play log
1. **Oct 2026, day one.** The "worst roster" wasn't bad: Giddey 83/85, rookies Wilson (77/87) and Essengue (75/84), Powell 79, Jones 77. Owner: "win about 26 games, give 6,700 minutes to players 23 or under, two seasons without playoffs". I put the kids in the starting five by hand. The rotation sheet warned "cramped spacing" and named the fix (Powell's 89 three-point on my bench). That felt like a real coach tool.
2. **Nov-Dec 2026: 4-9, then 9-17.** The sim stopped for "Buddy Hield has no minutes" (a side effect of my own swap) and wouldn't let me Play until I answered. A bit nannying, but it was a fair catch.
3. **Feb 6, 2027, the deadline sell-off (19-32).** Shopping vets on the Block returned mostly salary-matched vets. The AI rated Powell (33, $21M) near zero. The staff's own "sell" call was weak: Collins for two marginal guys.
   - Fix: I built Jones + Collins + Hield for a Utah 2028 1st (proj #6-17), Ace Bailey (20) and Green. "What would make this work?" auto-built the cash and salary filler, and the confirm sheet previewed my rotation changes.
   - This was the highlight of Season 1. The trade tool is excellent.
4. **Tanking is not really possible or needed.** Players stay 70+ OVR and the owner only wanted 26 wins. I finished 30-52 (12th East, 7th-worst). Owner verdict: "Good", grade +26. The lottery moved me up 3 to #4. The reveal is a good show, with the pick-by-pick reveal and the "You jump to No. 4" splash.
5. **Draft 2027.** The board had staff-read ranges, a combine table with measurables, and a mock with percentages. At #4 the top three went as the mock said. I took Oladipo (18, 75/87-89). The game flagged him "Reach · ranked 7th". Offers for the pick (27 of them) were real, e.g. BKN's 2028 1st (proj #3-9) plus a 2nd. The #1 pick cost "4.2 firsts" in the AI's own words, and the game showed me that number.
6. **Summer 2027: re-sign and free agency.** Declining Powell's $22.6M option showed "you can re-sign him at about $11M". That's a clever, readable trick. Bailey's $12.6M team option was declined, then he re-signed at $1.46M the next summer. The exploit is real and fun.
   - Free agency: I bid at ask for Reese and won. I lost Black (Detroit $14.8M vs my $15.8M, "money matters most to him", which contradicts itself), Castle (Utah paid $27.9M vs my ask $24.1M) and Ware (LAL).
   - Playing time and winning drove most decisions. Bidding at ask rarely wins a contested young FA.
7. **Summer development, Oct 2027.** Oladipo 76 to 81, Wilson 78 to 81, Reese 77 to 81 (+4), Essengue 76 to 79. The "Summer development" mail explained who the staff prioritised (+1.0 for Wilson) and what it cost the others (about 0.4 each). This is the best-explained development system I've seen in a sim.
8. **2027-28: 35-47, 12th.** Still below the roster's ratings. Owner: "Good", +24. The rebuild was working but the team wasn't winning yet. Record 6-10 early, and the staff kept "test-simming Hedge for Blitz" with no change.
9. **Apr 2028 lottery:** I held two firsts (own #9 and UTA's #6, which dropped from 4th). Class was thinner. Draft: Bennett at #6 (72/84, flagged "Big reach · ranked 12th") and Wiggins at #9 (74).
10. **2028-29: the turn.** 11-4 start, 3rd East, ended 50-32 as the 5th seed. The owner's ask was 43 wins and a playoff berth, with "-40 at best" if I missed the play-in. I beat IND 4-1 and DET 4-2, then lost to BOS in Game 7 of the East finals. Owner: "Excellent", +58. This is the moment the rebuild turns, and it did feel like a payoff. It came very fast: 30 wins to the conference finals in two years.
11. **Nov 2028, live game vs LAL (13-3).**
    - The preview page had scouting, matchups and a "Tip off" button.
    - Live, the assistant gave measured advice ("they're winning the glass by 4"; "Their ball screens: 8 points on 4 trips, Drop on ball screens").
    - Post-game: tactics −6.5 pts, shooting luck −4.2, "LA beat the blitz with quick passes into 4-on-3s", and per-player grades. Lost 111-115.
12. **2029-30: 53-29, first-round loss to BOS in 5** with Oladipo (88/98) injured. Owner: "As expected", season grade −9, because "win a playoff round" was missed (−35). Two teams offered me coaching jobs.
13. **Summer 2030.** Wilson asked $52.1M x 5 (86/91), Essengue $39.9M x 5, Giddey $31.8M (he had asked $46M a year earlier, so waiting paid). Payroll hit $233M against a $254M tax line.
14. **2030-31: 58-24, 2nd seed, won the In-Season Cup,** swept DET and MIL, then lost Game 7 of the East finals to Washington. Owner: "Good", +19. The owner's bar had gone from 26 wins to 60 wins.
15. **Development tracker (OVR/POT):**
    - Oladipo 76/89 at 18, to 81/94, 85/96, 88/98, 90/98, 91/99 at 22.
    - Wilson 77/87 at 20, to 81/89, 83/90, 85/90, 86/91, 86/90.
    - Wiggins (drafted #9 at 74, age 18) 75/84, 78/87, 79/88, 81/88, 85/91, 87/93.
    - Bennett 72/84 (age 19), 76/87, 77/88, 79/88, 82/90, 83/91.
    - Giddey peaked at 85 at age 25 and slid to 82 by 28.

## What hooked me (top 5, each with evidence)
1. **The trade desk.** I got a projected top-17 first, two prospects and a salary dump for three vets (Feb 2027). "What would make this work?" built the cash and filler, and the confirm sheet showed minutes changes.
2. **Development with receipts.** "Oladipo +5 to 81, staff priority added about +1.0 to Wilson, cost the others about 0.4 each." I could watch each of my kids climb summer by summer (Wiggins 74 to 87 in 4 years).
3. **The lottery and draft room.** "You jump to No. 4"; "Big reach · ranked 12th"; "#1 is worth 4.2 firsts to them, your pile is 2.8".
4. **Contract mechanics as strategy.** Declining Powell's option, re-sign at about $11M. Bailey went from $12.6M option to $1.46M re-sign. The Giddey ask dropped from $46M to $31.8M by waiting.
5. **The arc.** 30, 35, 50, 53, 58 wins and Cup champions, with an owner whose demands visibly rise ("Excellent" at 50 wins in 2029, "Good" at 58 in 2031).

## What pushed me away (top 5, each marked dealbreaker / major / minor, with evidence)
1. **(Major) The rebuild barely costs anything.** The "worst" roster started with an 83, an 85 and a 77/87 rookie. A tank was never needed, and Year 3 was already 50 wins, then an ECF Game 7 loss. A veteran rebuilder wants the cold winter years.
2. **(Major) Contested free agency is a coin you can't read.** I lost Black ($15.8M offered vs DET's $14.8M, "money matters most to him"), Castle (Utah paid more) and Ware ("playing time matters most"). The messages contradict the stated priority, and bidding at ask never wins.
3. **(Minor) Sims stop for self-inflicted calls and block Play.** "Isaac Okoro has no minutes", "Josh Green isn't in your rotation", "Release 2 players". A rotation edit creates new blocking questions.
4. **(Minor) Staff sell-calls are weak.** "Sell to LAL: Collins for Thiero + Hardy" barely moves the forecast. The Block returns salary-matched vets, not picks.
5. **(Minor) Back-to-back Game 7 losses to Boston/Washington feel scripted.** I can't tell if it is luck or plan, and the staff's "Nothing the staff measured beats your plan" repeats every game.

## Bugs (repro steps, screenshot path)
- The Block has no Escape for the offers sheet, so a script can't hit Play options while the "dg-" sheet is open. Repro: sim 1 month, then Play options (screenshot: `.../players/02-rebuilder/peek.png`). Sims stop on a call and the Continue bar is covered. Playtest-only, it works for a player.
- Free-agent sheet: "Make offer" vs "Submit offer" label changes by bidding state (day-1 vs ask-signing). Not a bug, but easy to mis-tap. Screenshots: `fa.png`.
- Draft staff pick: clicking "Take the staff's pick" with no screenshot of the board left me unsure whether it had drafted for #22 and #13 (two picks played in one click). Minor.
- No crash and `page.errors` stayed empty in all seasons.

## Against the genre (FM / BBGM / MyNBA / Hoop Land: where it wins and loses for me)
- **vs BBGM:**
  - Wins on explanations, scouting-lite, a pick-by-pick reveal, owner stakes, and the trade desk's reasons.
  - Loses on depth of history and speed: BBGM lets me tank hard and build for 10 years with no hand-holding.
- **vs FM:** the staff reads, the post-game decomposition and the roster-fit sheet are close to FM. The league lifecycle is much shorter.
- **vs MyNBA/MyGM:** better numbers, but much less visual theatre.
- **Hoop Land / DDS:** similar spirit, better tools here.

## Would I pay and keep playing
$7.99 phone unlock: yes. $14.99 on Steam: yes if rebuilds were harder and 15 seasons ran as fast as these five did. I'd put in 15-25 hours. Recommend to a friend: 8/10.

## The one change that would most raise my score
Make the rebuild cost something: a weaker start, steeper AI free-agent competition and a slower dev curve, so that Year 3 is a lottery year and the "turn" in Year 5 or 6 feels hard-won.
