# 01 — The FM Lifer
## Who I am and what I played
Dallas (real league, 14th of 30 by roster rank, West), GM + Head Coach, 390x844 phone, headless Chromium with 3D off. I played two full seasons (2026-27: 51-31, 4th seed, lost R1 1-4 to Houston; 2027-28: 52-30, 4th seed, swept 0-4 by Minnesota in R1 with Flagg out). I did the full offseason between them (draft lottery, re-sign, free agency, development roll). I coached 13 games live: 10 regular season, 3 playoff. Only the opener was watched end to end; the rest I played Q1 or Q1-Q2 live, then used Skip ahead > "Sim to final". I sim'd the rest in chunks. Live time was slow: about 3.5 real minutes per quarter on 2D court.
I opened every tactics surface before the opener (System, Change style sheet, Core settings, Advanced drawer, Shape board, roles, Season Analysis, Rotation, Lineups, game plan and inbox staff mails).
## Play log
1. **Oct 20, first hour.** The Today screen gives a 4-step "first games" guide and forces "release 2 players" (17 on the roster, 15 allowed). The staff's cut picks come with a dead-money warning (Caleb Martin, $19.4M dead). Clear. Roster is Irving 86, Lively 86/91, Flagg 83/90 plus real players. Owner goals day one: win ~41, playoffs, 8,400 minutes to under-24s.
2. **Tactics read.** Staff style was Balanced Attack. I tried Heliocentric: the grade strip instantly showed Rim pressure A+ to B+ and Ball movement C to F. I switched to Five-Out Motion: Ball movement A, Rim pressure A, with a "+4" summary. That is a very FM-like feedback loop (edit, see attribute grades move). BUT the headline "HOU 76% to win, by 10.7" did not move with any of three styles or a hedge counter. The preview forecast does not respond to tactics.
3. **Oct 21, game 1 at HOU (full live game).** Lost 103-119. The break board is the best thing in the game: Q1 report said "Dallas' hedges stalled ball handlers... DAL +1.1 pts, over 11 plays", with luck labelled as luck ("shots fell above their looks: HOU +4.8 pts, luck, nothing to change"). My moves log tracks each change with a "since" result (Flagg in for Risacher: DAL 8-6 since; Tempo Balanced to Up-tempo: DAL 15-6 since; Hammer play out of a timeout: "Ran it: came up empty"). I went 7-32 from three and lost on that, which the report flagged as shot quality, not tactics.
4. **Live calls.** Timeout opens a set-play menu (Iso, Hammer, Elevator, Floppy, Chicago, Spain, Horns, each with a one-line description of who it's for). Staff cards interrupt the sim ("Double Durant", "Back off the ball", "Push the pace", "Hedge on ball screens") with Take / Not now. A hot/cold tag and energy per player are on the Players tab, and tap-to-sub works.
5. **Oct 21 - Nov 8, first 10 live games: 3-7 at one point, 5-5 after ten.** I made plans (hedge, ice, zone, press) for HOU, IND, SAS, UTA, BKN. Honest failure: many counter cards read "No clear edge" after the staff finished thinking (first 1-5 seconds they say "Staff still looking..."), so on most nights the game plan told me nothing was worth doing. When I forced the press vs UTA/BKN, the board credited it: "Dallas' full-court press created turnovers, DAL +1.2 pts". BKN won by 20.
6. **Season Analysis (Dec 1).** The staff reported my Balanced Attack to Five-Out Motion change as "still too close to call over 20 games... That was the staff's last look." It never concluded in either direction, even after 82 games ("No clear edge"). Honest, but 80 games of inconclusive evidence makes the tool feel like fake rigor.
7. **Dec 3, 10-11.** Three AI trade offers (NYK Hart 81 + 1st + 2nd for Gafford 81; CHI for Johnson Jr.; LAC wanting Washington + Risacher + a 1st for Garland 82). Offers lapse in days. Accepting is two taps from the inbox sheet. I accepted NYK; my later roster shows no Hart, so I am not sure it executed (see Bugs).
8. **Feb 11 deadline.** Staff: "stand pat. No clear edge from the deal." It offered Curry (87, 38 y.o., $178M over 3) for Irving + Washington and said the forecast barely moves. I stood pat. Record 29-24, then 22-7 to finish 51-31, 4th seed.
9. **Apr 18-27 playoffs vs HOU.** Lost G1 by 24 after trailing all game on HOU shooting luck (the board said +16.9 pts luck). Won G2 112-109 after a staff "Double Durant" call, lost G3 106-110 after leading by 13 at half. Flagg got hurt, lost 1-4. My own script sim'd the elimination game 5 instead of letting me coach it. My bad, but "Sim to next game" does it with no confirmation.
10. **Offseason.** Draft: no pick (traded). Re-sign screen: Lively II asks $43.7M x 5 (86/91, was on $7.2M). A superb negotiation sheet with min / his ask / max, years, option on last year, "Very interested", and tax impact. Irving extension asked $31.9M x 1 (cheaper than his $42.4M). I re-signed Lively at his ask.
11. **Free agency.** A bidding war: Anthony Black (77/82, 3PT D F to D) was my pick because the staff graded my need ("Three-point D, F with your rotation, 28th of 30. Your setting 'Help defense: Balanced' costs it"). I used the mid-level to bid $14.8M; LAL won at $14.9M. A mail said "they paid more." Fair, felt real.
12. **Aug.** Flagg +5 to 90/96 "Breakout". Irving -3 to 83 at 35. Owner season 2: 51 wins, win a round, payroll under $221.6M. Season 2: 38-15 at Feb 5, then Flagg broke his hand for 24 games, 14-15 after, 52-30. Swept in R1. Owner grade: "Season grade -4" (wins +10, playoff round -35, payroll +23). Two coaching job offers (PHX, PHI) arrived, an effective "stay or leave" beat.

## What hooked me
1. **The break board** ("What's deciding it" with +pts and luck separated): Q1 vs SAS "San Antonio's shots fell above their looks... SAS +15.5 pts, luck". That is my FM xG moment.
2. **Moves log with "since" outcomes**: "Tempo: Balanced to Up-tempo, DAL 15-6 since." Every call is accountable.
3. **Re-sign / FA sheets**: market ask, interest level, who else bids, what matters to him (winning 62% wins), tax in the same view.
4. **Season Analysis**: opponent effects ("The opposition packed the paint... cost you") and 14 grades that move live when you edit.
5. **Owner/story layer**: monthly mails with pace math, season grade -4 with the wins/round/payroll split, then job offers from PHX and PHI.
## What pushed me away
1. **Forecast ignores tactics (major).** HOU 76% to win stayed 76% (10.7) through Heliocentric, Five-Out Motion and a Hedge counter. FM's key promise (edit, see the projection change) is missing there.
2. **Counters mostly say "No clear edge" (major).** Every card read "Staff still looking..." and then "No clear edge" most nights, so per-opponent plans didn't feel like they mattered. Where they did (press vs BKN) the board credited +1.2 pts, which is real but small.
3. **Staff verdict never concludes (minor to major).** After 82 games the Five-Out vs Balanced test was still "No clear edge". A lifer wants a number or a "stop asking".
4. **Real-time live game is slow (major for ten games).** About 12 real minutes for a full game even on 2D; Skip ahead is good, but I'd expect a speed control on the court.
5. **Grades vs results (minor).** My team was A+ in rim pressure/ball movement/rim protect and still 10-11, with 3PA rate 28th. Grades describe style, not strength, and nothing says that.
## Bugs
- Game plan "Add" button: "Add" matched by role needs force-click at times (headless); counters list re-orders after the staff finishes, so index-based picks hit the wrong counter (UI itself fine for a human).
- "Plan for your opener (done)" and "Plan for your next game (done)" tick on the Today guide while Games shows "No game plan yet".
- Accepting the NYK offer (Hart for Gafford + picks): inbox showed "Accept", but I could not later confirm Hart on the roster (Gafford was still on the Dec/ Aug rosters I read). Unverified, possibly a no-op. Screenshot: players/01-fm-lifer/offer.png.
- Re-sign screen: "Decline" on team options did nothing visible when tapped (staff auto-declined at FA open instead). Screenshot: resign.png.
- "Sim to next game" in the playoffs plays an elimination game with no live-coach prompt.
- `Open free agency` from the Today bar was dead when "Re-sign players" is shown first. I had to open it from the play-options sheet.
## Against the genre
- **FM:** Wins on tactical-feedback readability (luck vs shot quality, moves log), on staff voices, and on honesty (inconclusive verdicts). Loses on role/instruction depth, per-player individual orders (only shot freedom/crunch go-to), scouting and training. Fake complexity: about a third of the Advanced drawer, since the preview doesn't price any of it.
- **BBGM:** Much deeper coaching and story. BBGM is faster and its trade AI is more fun to exploit.
- **MyNBA / Hoop Land:** Far better analytics and tactics than either. Presentation (2D court, 3D off) is not at 2K level.
## Would I pay and keep playing
$7.99 on phone: yes. $14.99 on Steam: yes if the preview reacted to tactics and live games had a speed control. About 60-80 hours. Recommend to a friend: 8/10.
## The one change that would most raise my score
Make the win-chance forecast and counter cards respond to my tactical edits with a number. Show the expected win probability change when I switch style, coverage, or choose a counter, and show a "tested" delta after the game. Then the great post-game evidence layer has a before-game partner and my plans feel like they matter.
