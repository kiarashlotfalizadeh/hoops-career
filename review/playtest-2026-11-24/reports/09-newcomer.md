# 09 — The Crossover Newcomer (19, 2K MyNBA + a bit of BBGM)

## Who I am and what I played
- Career 1: real rosters, Washington Mages (the default team the wizard pre-selects, 21st of 30 by roster), GM + Head Coach, Teaching ON, all defaults. Played the whole 2026-27 regular season (33-49, 13th/14th East, missed play-in), sim'd the playoffs I wasn't in, watched the draft lottery (we picked 8th) and made pick #8 in the draft. I did not get through the offseason into season 2 (my script for that stalled; my fault, not the game's).
- Career 2: fictional league, Washington again, defaults. Played 19 games (5-14), one of them opened live.
- Live games coached: 1 full-ish (opener vs MIL, W 107-101, through Q1 break, clutch time) plus one fictional game (L 111-128 vs OKC).
- Device: phone, 390x844, 2D court (3D shown briefly). Roughly 30 in-game weeks of play, about 1.5 hours of real-person-equivalent time.

## Play log
1. **/start (0:00).** Landing is clean: "GM and head coach in one, with a tactics layer that tells you why," three stats, "New league". Tapped it. Step 1 of 3: Real rosters vs Fictional. Took Real. Step 2: team list with "roster rank of 30" under each team. Washington was pre-picked (Davis 87, Young 85, Dybantsa 81). I did not know why Washington was default; as a 2K player I'd have picked my own team, but the ranking-under-each-team idea is really good.
2. **Step 3 (0:02).** Name, role (GM + Head Coach / GM only, with one-line explanation), Injuries / Job security / Teaching toggles each with a ? icon. Understood everything. Creation took 0.24 s, instantly in the game. Hook point 1: speed.
3. **Today (0:03).** Big navy "Preseason" card, owner goals in plain words ("Win about 27 games / 10,000 minutes to players 23 or under / payroll under $209.1M"). "Action queue" with a 4-step checklist "Your first games". I did not know what "minutes to players 23 or under" implies for how I should play. Did not know "Action queue".
4. **Guide steps (0:04-0:10).** "Set your rotation" -> opens Rotation with a banner and a "Keep these five" button. That's the right amount of hand-holding: I tapped keep and it ticked. "Pick a playbook" -> Tactics page. Here the screen got heavy: Shape "4-Out, 1-In", roles "Post Hub/Rim Runner/Glue Guy", "Drop" coverage, "Pack the paint", grades F/A+. I understood maybe 40% of the words. "Keep this playbook" saved me. Changing playbook shows "Staff's read: Similar fit / Slightly worse" — easy to read. Step 3 "Plan for your opener" lists "Counters: Switch coverage / Attack the drop... Staff still looking…" and "Special plans: Hack-a-player, Junk defense". Scary. I tapped nothing and the step never ticked (it still reads "Plan for your next game (next)" five games later), so the guide never finishes for people who skip it.
5. **First live game (0:10-0:20).** "Watch & coach live". The broadcast prompt asks "Watch sideways / Keep upright". Intro, then a coach-mark tour (4 steps) and, on this headless box, the 3D court. The 3D court at a glance looks real: players in kits, referee, ball. A "3D is running slowly on this device" prompt offers Use 2D. Staff voice (Scott Ortega) writes plain sentences: "Early. Watching how they want to play." "Young has the hot hand." Hook point 2: the live feed reads like a story. Win chance bar: MIL 44% / WAS 56%, moves each possession. End-of-Q1 report card: score, FG%, threes, "What's deciding it — Milwaukee's shots fell below their looks... That part is luck, with nothing to change." That sentence taught me more about basketball luck than 2K ever did. Late game: "Tired or in foul trouble: Davis 4 fouls" with a "Coulibaly in" button. Won 107-101. Felt clever. First real hook at ~0:20.
6. **Post-win (0:22).** Owner mail "Your first win". Guide at 3 of 4. Felt rewarded.
7. **Sim 1 week (0:25).** Result 0-4, 1-4 and 14th in the East. "Washington blows 16-point lead, falls to Cleveland" and a 95-131 home loss. A "While you were away" sheet explained: "Outscored by 10.2 a game. The shots fell about as their looks were worth. Not luck." That was honest but crushing. Near-quit moment 1. I had no idea what to do about it.
8. **Where do I look? (0:28).** Team > Tactics > Season Analysis: ORtg 109 (29th), DRtg 118 (18th), "Perimeter D: F, Three-point D: F, Spacing: D". I tried the only obvious lever, ball-screen "Switch". The game answered with an amber warning "Switching with a slow big: opponents will hunt Davis. Drop instead" and a chip "Rim protection A→B". That is a great feedback loop: a cheap try, an immediate predicted consequence. But the record never improved: 6-13 at Nov 28 with six straight losses. Later the staff wrote "Test-simmed Drop in place of Switch over the next 6 games, off the floor. No clear edge." That one made me trust the staff.
9. **Sim to deadline (Feb 6, 19-33).** Staff call card: "Trade deadline in 5 days. The postseason is a long shot. The staff's call: sell to Dallas. The deal could hurt." Why: "Max Christie (74, 23 y/o) for Ayton (76). The forecast barely moves your postseason chances." I left it (two-tap confirm). Boston offered Queta + Robinson + a pick for Dybantsa (my 19-year-old). I couldn't see anything to gain and passed. Fine, but I'm 19 myself: I want to know why a 76 is worth swapping for a 74, and nobody told me what "the deal could hurt" means.
10. **Season end (Apr 13).** 33-49, "Your season is over". The Today card is now just a button "Sim to end of playoffs". Owner status still "rising/Secure" because 33 wins beat his 27 target. That felt right, and the first time the owner stuff clicked.
11. **Lottery (Jun).** Draft lottery has a reveal ("Reveal the order" / "Skip to the order"), odds per team ("Washington 6.0%"), "3 places down" arrows. Drawn #8. LeBron retires in the league news with real career numbers (45,028 points, four titles...). 2K feel, honestly nice.
12. **Draft (pick 8).** Board of 72 with ratings and POT. Tap a prospect: "Utility Wing. Best tools: athleticism and perimeter defense. Needs work: rebounding." Combine numbers, "Staff read OVR 73-75 / POT 82-84", and "picks 1-10 have ended a median 4 OVR from the POT read." This is more transparent than anything in 2K. I took James Maxwell (74/POT 82-84, rank 10th, labeled "Reach"). Then "Make the pick? ... Pick #8: 1st round, a 4-year rookie-scale deal." Good friction. NYK offered 2 picks for #8 (didn't understand the value).
13. **Fictional league career (new).** Same 30 cities, same nicknames (Washington Mages), invented players (C. Bradley 85, L. Turner 84). 104-81 win over GSW in the opener. Then 2-5 with five straight losses, star Lamar Turner out 27 games (torn meniscus), "Power rankings: 13th -> 29th". At Nov 25: 5-14. The headline "Washington is one move away, and needs to make it. Dee Okafor on 4 straight losses: go get a star." is cute and funny, then punishing.
14. **Fictional live game.** Lost 111-128 to OKC; report: "Turnover battle: WAS 27, OKC 8" and "Next time: Take care of the ball. Put the ball in your most secure handlers' hands, or slow the pace." with a margin-bridge "looks -18.9 / shooting luck +10.9 / free throws -9 = -17". Actionable and plain.

## What hooked me (top 5)
1. **Why-sentences.** "Milwaukee's shots fell below their looks... That part is luck, with nothing to change." (end of Q1, game 1). It turned a random result into a lesson.
2. **Speed and clean entry.** Career creation 0.24 s; a month sims in about 5 s; the wizard is 3 steps with a roster rank under each team.
3. **Live game story + staff voice.** Hot hand, "Davis 4 fouls — Coulibaly in", momentum run labels, win chance bar moving 56% -> 74% by the end of Q1.
4. **Draft profile honesty.** Combine numbers, scouting days watched, and "median 4 OVR from POT" are better than MyNBA's hidden grades.
5. **Pre-commit warnings.** Switch coverage showed the exact grade cost (Rim protection A→B) and why. First time a game told me a decision's price before I paid it.

## What pushed me away (top 5)
1. **Early losing with no clear fix (major).** 1-4 after the first week in career 1; 2-5 then 5-14 in the fictional one. The report said "Not luck" yet gave only grades, not a button. A 2K newcomer wants a "Fix it" lever. Near-quit at ~0:28 and again at fictional Nov 25.
2. **Tactics vocabulary (major).** Drop, Hedge, Blitz, Ice, Pack the paint, Post Hub, Rim Runner, ORtg, eFG, FTR, "looks". No inline definitions on the board, only a small ? icon I had to hunt for. In the fictional career I just pressed Keep for everything.
3. **Guide never finishes (minor).** "Plan for your opener" stays "(next)" forever if you don't add a counter; after the opener it just renames to "Plan for your next game". There is no "Skip this step" button.
4. **Fictional league has no hook (major for casual fans).** Same team names but unknown players. As a 2K player I don't care about "C. Bradley 85"; I cared about Davis/Young/Dybantsa. The star injury at game 5 felt random, not a story.
5. **Stacked sheets and tours (minor).** The 4-step coach-mark tour on the live court plus "3D is running slowly" banner covered the court on first view; the "While you were away" digest opened over the Today screen and blocked tapping the Continue bar until dismissed.

## Against the genre
- **vs NBA 2K MyNBA:** 2K wins on flash: the 3D here looks like a game, but the menus are text, not faces and posters. This wins on why: the post-game "What decided it" with luck separated from skill, and on honest odds (win chance, lottery odds, POT ranges). MyNBA gives me a number; this tells me what to change.
- **vs Basketball GM:** BBGM is faster to understand in the first minute (spreadsheets, three buttons). This looks much better and has a live game, but the Tactics tab has about three times the words BBGM ever shows.
- **vs FM (guessing, never played):** the staff calls and "Needs you" count feel like an FM inbox, but they didn't feel like homework, which surprised me. The "Plan for your opener" screen with counters and special plans looks the most FM-like and the most intimidating.
- **Hoop Land / Draft Day Sports:** didn't play them.

## Would I pay and keep playing
- $7.99 phone unlock: yes if the free part reaches a first win and a draft pick, which it did in about 25 minutes. I would hesitate if the fictional league is what I'm shown first.
- $14.99 on Steam: not for me; I'd want star faces and posters.
- Hours I'd put in: ~10 in the first week; I'd bounce at the first 0-4 stretch without a fix suggestion.
- Recommend to a friend: 7/10 to a basketball-stats friend, 5/10 to a 2K-only friend.

## The one change that would most raise my score
When the team is losing early, put one "Fix it" card on Today that proposes one concrete change in the same plain language as the live game (e.g. "Take care of the ball: set Young as lead handler, expected +X") with a Try / Keep button, and tick the guide step when I decline.
