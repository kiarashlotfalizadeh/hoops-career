# 06 — The Game-Day Coach (youth coach, 37)

## Who I am and what I played
- Chicago Stampede, real-rosters league, GM + Head Coach, East. Starting roster: Giddey 83, Powell 79, Jones 77, Wilson 77, Smith 77.
- Played Oct 21 to Dec 21 of 2026-27, which is 30 team games (including the Cup). About 26 were coached live in some form: 1x or Key plays speed, my own timeouts and ATO calls, halftime changes, crunch tools. The staff simmed 3 with Quick sim as a control. Because the headless browser was slow, I let a few games run on Key plays and only took over at breaks, runs and clutch.
- Record at the end: 13-16, 13th in the East. Last 10: 4-6. I lost the Cup quarterfinal to Orlando 120-123 in 2OT. No playoffs reached.
- Devices: phone 390x844 for almost everything. Desktop 1280x800 for the 3D judgement and some 2D shots. Roughly 4 hours of wall time, most of it waiting on live games.
- Not done: Transactions beyond a glance, the draft, the offseason.

## Play log
1. **Oct 21 @TOR, my first live game.** Up 41-22 after Q1, lost 129-133. The report said it was 1.5 looks, -3.5 shooting luck and -2 free throws. It told me to "Ice their screens". I felt coached by the report, not cheated.
2. **Same game, a pile of "Your moves".** I tapped the timeout play card repeatedly while the board was open. The report then listed about 70 identical "Iso: Giddey - Ran it: 3 points" rows stamped Q1 5:30. Each tap stacks as its own call and nothing says it's already set. See Bugs.
3. **Oct 21 to 24, 0-3 start.** The pattern was the same each night: "Take care of the ball" (CHI 13-15 turnovers) and the opponent shooting above its looks. Season Analysis showed Net -7.9 (27th), Opp TOV% 9.5% (30th), DRtg 25th. I set Tempo to Patient and Ball pressure to Pressure and later ran zone. At 29 games the Net is -1.6 (17th) and DRtg is 115.3 (10th). That is a measured improvement in the numbers I could act on. ORtg stayed 23rd, and the grades still list shot creation as a D.
4. **Oct 26 @BOS, 117-116.** I trailed late and won it on a 9-0 closing run. The bottom bar flagged Clutch time and offered "Watch at 1x". It was a good moment.
5. **Oct 28 vs NYK, 112-91, and b1.** Down 41-62 at half, I won by 21. The report called it "about a 1-in-165 shooting night" (+20.6 luck). This is the biggest swing I saw, and the game owned up to it.
6. **Nov 8 vs PHI.** I used the new Game Plan page: who guards whom, Double-team, Attack a defender, Hack-a-player, Junk defense. I set Double-team on Maxey and Attack James. The report's Your plan block said "Double-team Brown". I lost 105-125, with luck -16.5, a 1-in-35 night, and 15 turnovers against 5.
7. **Timeouts after opponent runs, measured.** In game 1 the game itself scored my three timeouts. "Timeout: CHI 7-11 since", "9-14 since" and "8-14 since", meaning I was outscored after every one. The Your moves credit across games was +0.2 to +0.4 pts, which is nothing. I called 40-plus timeouts on 8-0 or longer runs.
8. **Halftime "Aggressive + Pressure" when trailing, 2 of 2 games.** Both times the opponent's next run came right after (9-0, 10-0). It's n=2. No game credit shown for those changes.
9. **Dec 5 Cup QF vs ORL, the best sequence of the run.** I was down 5, then tied at 102 with 21.2 left and the ball. A "Last possession" card appeared with a Timeout button, a Call-the-play list ("Iso: Jones - no go-to set, Jones is the best closer out there", "Hammer: Powell - run 1x, they've seen it"), and staff sub suggestions. I ran Iso: Jones. It came up empty, we went to OT, and then 2OT. I lost 120-123. This felt like coaching.
10. **Crunch-time Hack-a-player.** The opponent sheet says things like "At 89% he's no target: fouling him gives away points", with FT% shown for all five on the floor. In three crunch spots every opponent was 74-89%, so the honest answer was "don't". That is a coach's call made by a tool that won't flatter me.
11. **A live game that finished without me.** I left a live game at Q4 1:25 down 7 (loss was coming). When I came back the game was over (129-133). I never pressed "Finish this game".
12. **Staff calls and manual subs.** Tired or foul-trouble cards say "Smith: Tired - Collins in" and are one tap. With "Make the subs yourself" on, I tapped them from Q1 to Q4. Three taps of "Swain in" a few seconds apart looked like the card lingering after the first tap. I couldn't tell if the extra taps did anything.
13. **Staff Quick sims.** 1-2 over three games (115-106 W, 98-103 L, 109-125 L). My live record is about 10-14. Both samples are tiny, so I can't claim my coaching beat the staff.
14. **3D check, desktop and phone.** Judged from stills below.

## What the game tells you about luck versus tactics
- Every report splits the margin into looks, shooting luck and free throws, and the sums are right (looks -6.6, luck +20.6, FT +7 = +21 in b1).
- The luck terms I recorded were -3.5, -14.7, -5.5, +20.6 and -16.5 points, and Q1 stretches like "96% eFG on 57% looks". Per-game swings of 10-20 points are plausible, but a 1-in-35 and a 1-in-165 night in 25 games is more than chance. The sim is streaky enough that a good defensive plan can get erased in one quarter.
- Because the game labels it, I never felt cheated. I did feel that "nothing to change" appears so often that coaching is under-rewarded.

## What hooked me (top 5, each with evidence)
1. **The Last possession card (Dec 5, tied 102-102, 21.2 left).** It asks for a call, names a closer, warns "they've seen it", and then it goes to OT and 2OT. It felt like my decision.
2. **Honest Hack-a-player.** "At 89% he's no target: fouling him gives away points." Only a tool that respects the coach does that.
3. **The game report's "what decided it".** I got concrete lines like "Offensive rebounds: CHI 15, TOR 4", "Turnover battle" and "Next time: take care of the ball". That moved me to change Tempo and pressure, and DRtg went 25th to 10th.
4. **Timeout board with ATO sets drawn on a diagram and a plain description of each play** ("A catch-and-shoot three off the screens, or a curl when his man trails"). My kids' coach brain loved it.
5. **The pregame brief and Game Plan.** Maxey 29.4 pts, 67.2% TS, on/off +15.9; who guards whom with a rating gap line. It feels like scouting.

## What pushed me away (top 5, each marked dealbreaker / major / minor, with evidence)
1. **Major: coaching impact is invisible.** Timeouts after opponent runs were scored as "CHI 7-11, 9-14, 8-14 since", and Your moves was +0.2 to +0.4 pts per game. After 25 games I could not tell if I mattered. The staff's sims finished close to mine.
2. **Major: the wait.** At default speed a full live game is 4-7 minutes. 4x is buried in Settings (Live games, Playback Speed). The pause bar offers 2x and Key plays but never 4x, so I played 20 games before I found it.
3. **Major: sim swings.** 32-0 first-quarter run, 96% eFG on 57% looks in a quarter, a 1-in-165 night, a 1-in-35 night. Labelling doesn't fix a game where the half lead is meaningless.
4. **Minor: pop-up overlays on the live screen.** The coach tour ("1/4 ... Next") intercepts taps on first entry. Staff calls, run cards and tired cards can stack on the right side, with the timeout button grey while a card is open.
5. **Minor: a live game can finish without you.** I left one at Q4 1:25 and found the final score on return.

## Against the genre (FM / BBGM / MyNBA / Hoop Land: where it wins and loses for me)
- **vs FM.** It wins on live coaching: timeout boards, ATO plays with diagrams, tired/foul cards, the last-possession decision. It loses on proof: FM shows me xG and instruction effects, but here Your moves is a one-decimal credit that rounds to nothing.
- **vs BBGM.** Far deeper in-game. BBGM has no live coaching. BBGM's long-run world is more trusted, though, because it doesn't swing this hard.
- **vs NBA 2K MyNBA.** I choose plays and see them run on a court, and I get why a possession ended. 2K gives you the controller; this gives you the clipboard. For a coach, this one is better.
- **vs Hoop Land / Draft Day.** Nothing else I know has run cards, Hack-a-player advice and honest luck labelling.
