# 08 — The Real-NBA Purist

## Who I am and what I played
- Career A: real league, GM only, New York (East). One full 2026-27 season through the Finals, then the draft lottery and the 2027 draft board. About 25 live-less sims.
- Career B: fictional league, GM + Head Coach, Chicago. About 24 games (to Dec 9), plus the first minutes of one live game.
- Career C: real league, GM + Head Coach, San Antonio. One full season through a title run.
- Live games: one, partial (2D court). Device: phone, 390x844. About 3 hours of play.
- I did not play a second season in the same save. Career C stands in as the GM + Head Coach comparison.
- Confidence note: my own knowledge stops around June 2026. I can check the 2025-26 and earlier past hard. I cannot check the 2026 offseason moves, so I mark those "unverified" below.

## Play log
1. **Oct 2026, New York, real, GM only.** Roster opens with Brunson 86, Towns 87, Anunoby 87, Bridges 83, Hart 81. The owner wants 45 wins. The first action is "Release 2 players" (17 signed, 15 allowed). The button needs two taps and the dead money is shown.
2. **Fidelity sweep.** I pulled all 400 ratings rows, all 30 team pages and 460 player pages. Contracts, draft position and college are mostly right (Curry "Davidson 2009 #7", Butler "Marquette 2011 #30"). Rosters carry the real 2026 trades: Harden at CLE, Davis and Young at WAS, Durant at HOU.
3. **The coaches are all invented** (Reggie Tanaka at OKC, Darnell Delacroix at BOS). So are nicknames and arenas (Knights, Twisters, Stirrups, Lagoons, Mages). The preview calls OKC 72-10.
4. **Dec 20: NYK 21-7.** Heavy "While you were away" digest. The AI staff "test-simmed Help defense 60 to 50" and found no edge. Interesting, but it is jargon to a fan.
5. **Season 1 ends 55-27, 1st seed** in a four-way East race (NYK/TOR 55-27, DET/BOS 54-28). OKC finishes 70-12 and beats us 4-1 in the Finals. SGA is MVP and Finals MVP at 37.6 ppg. Brunson hit 53 in a playoff game.
6. **Stat lines vs reality.** Dončić 33.0/7.9/7.4 in 71 GP. SGA 30.8 in 66 GP. Edwards 30.0. Giannis 28.9/10.5. Curry 27.5 with 5.0 3PM. Jokić 26.0/12.0/8.9, 65.6 TS%, 81 GP. Wemby 24.2/10.1/3.2 blk in 29.5 mpg. All believable. Wemby's minutes and rebounds look low.
7. **The record book is the best surprise.** Real finals and MVPs back to 2016-17 match what I remember. Real single-game records appear (Barkley's 33 boards, the Kings' 176-175, Memphis' 152-79 over OKC), and so does the 65-game award rule.
8. **Draft lottery:** "Fourteen teams, four drawn picks", with the LAC pick correctly owned by OKC. The 2027 class is entirely fictional: "Dawson Dort", "Tom Thybulle", "Yves Koloko".
9. **Fictional league (Chicago, 14/15 roster).** Names are bland ("J. Miles", "K. Daniels"). The team is 9-14 by Dec 9 after a 76-122 loss. The owner mail is sensible: "win about 17 games, play the young". The AI gives 3,700 minutes to players 23 and under.
10. **Career C, San Antonio.** I raised Wemby's target to 36 minutes. The Rotation page later read "217 min, scaled to 240" with Wemby at 31. He actually played 26.4 mpg. In January, news said "Wembanyama asks San Antonio for a trade... He has played 18 minutes a game lately. A player of his standing plays about 32." That is the exact thing I tried to prevent.
11. **SAS finished 53-29 (4th), then beat POR, OKC, LAL and CLE for the title.** Wemby was Finals MVP. News said "SGA was the MVP, his third straight". The two worlds diverge in the playoffs, which I like.

## What hooked me
1. **The real past shows up.** Finals results and MVPs for 2016-17 to 2024-25 match, with the right Finals MVPs (Brown in 2023-24, SGA in 2024-25). Memphis' 152-79 and Kendall Gill's 11 steals are in the book. That is trust earned.
2. **Contracts, college and draft slot on player pages.** Curry "Davidson, 2009 round 1 #7" and Butler "Marquette, 2011 #30" are right. Heights and weights look right too (Wemby 7'4", Kessler 7'2").
3. **Role and "Best at" lines read like a scout.** Caruso, Thybulle, Daniels, Mobley, Kessler and Clingan are all flagged elite defenders. Jokić is "Point Center". Kornet is "Rim Protector". Wemby is "Paint Eraser".
4. **Awards and rules are real.** The 65-game rule, the All-League and All-Defensive teams, the In-Season Cup with its MVP, and a 14-team lottery with four drawn picks.
5. **Honest staff notes.** "4 straight losses... shots that didn't fall. That's luck, not the plan." A fan can believe that.

## What pushed me away
1. **All 30 head coaches are invented, minor to major (I would call it major for a purist).** Mark Daigneault, Mike Brown, Joe Mazzulla and the rest are replaced by Reggie Tanaka, Darnell Delacroix and so on. The AI coach "styles" are therefore not anyone's real style.
2. **Invented nicknames and arenas, minor to major.** "Boston Garden" is real, but "Twine", "Mages", "Stirrups", "Wasps" and "Founders" next to real names like Brunson and Wembanyama break immersion every time. "New York Garden" and "San Francisco Arena" read worse. I would take bland over punny. A rename mod would fix this.
3. **Ratings do not match my eye, major.** The scale is very high. 400 players sit mostly at 70+. Kornet is 78, Champagnie 78, Tobias Harris 81 at 34. Anunoby (87) rates above Brunson (86), and Josh Hart is 81. Edwards has "99th percentile three-point shooting" and Kawhi is 92 at 35. Draymond is only 73. Giannis and Jokić both show 99th-percentile playmaking. I would trust the sim more if bench players were lower.
4. **Star minutes, major.** Wemby plays 29.5 mpg as the AI's pick. In my own SAS save the average starter played about 26 mpg, and my 36-minute edit was undone with no message (see Bugs). Real stars play 32-36. Rest games look too clean too: Haliburton 82 GP, Towns 82 GP, Jokić 81.
5. **OKC at 70-12 (preview 72-10), minor to major.** The league has one super-team, and the owner's preseason previews all but crown it. Real best-of-league records are 64-68 wins, so this is a stretch.

## Against the genre
- **Vs Basketball GM:** This beats BBGM on presentation, the real past and the staff voice. BBGM wins on the real-name option: real coaches and team names are one setting there.
- **Vs NBA 2K MyNBA:** 2K has the real shell, but no tactics layer this honest. Hoops Career has better "why did we lose" text.
- **Vs Football Manager:** The role and "Best at" panels and the staff test-sim are FM-shaped. It still lacks FM's scouting depth and match-day detail, though I only saw one live game.
- **Vs Hoop Land / Pro Basketball Manager:** The record book and history are clearly stronger. The fake team brands are the thing I would trade away.

## Would I pay and keep playing
- **$7.99 phone unlock:** Yes, for the history, the roster work and the honest sim.
- **$14.99 on Steam:** Maybe. I would want real coaches, real team names (or a way to type them) and a ratings pass first.
- **Hours:** About 30-50 as a purist. I would play two or three more seasons and then stop when the ratings quirks and invented coaches wore me down.
- **Recommend to a friend:** 7 out of 10.

## The one change that would most raise my score
Let me switch on real team names and real head coaches (or at least rename every team and coach by hand), and pull the bench ratings down so the rotations and minutes look like the NBA I watch. That one pass would fix most of what made me stop trusting the numbers.
