# 01 — The FM Lifer
## Who I am and what I played
- Team: Dallas Wranglers (real rosters, 14th of 30 by roster rank, Flagg/Irving/Lively core). Role: GM + Head Coach. Phone, 390x844, 2D court.
- Seasons: 2026-27 in full (40-42, lost both Play-In games) and 2027-28 in full (49-33, 5th seed, beat Denver 4-2, lost to 68-win OKC in Game 7 of the West semis).
- Live games: about 26 sessions. I coached Q1 and Q2 or Q3 live at 4x with subs, timeouts and staff calls, then used "Sim to final". Three I coached to the end (Play-In vs MEM, playoff elimination G6 and G7). The first game I coached around 3 quarters. One regular-season game (Oct 31) I did not coach because my script fell through to Sim 1 day.
- Per-opponent plans: ~16 (10 "Should help" counters, the rest untested ideas). The rest of the schedule was weekly or monthly sims.
- Time: about 3.5 hours wall clock. Most of that was waiting on live games, which run ~2.5 min a quarter at 4x.

## Play log
1. **Oct 20, preseason.** The Today queue ("Release 2 players", a guide checklist, owner goals set on day one) was clear. Staff picks for cuts showed dead money up front ($19.4M for Caleb Martin). Two-tap confirm. Good teaching.
2. **Tactics tour.** System > Change sheet (offense / defense / mentality, each style with a "Staff's read" fit label), Shape (4-Out/5-Out/Horns/Double Post with "hides a non-shooting big" and "2 non-shooters" tags and +/- bullets), Advantage (play-type mix as % of trips), Finish (expected shot mix 32/31/37), Transitions, Defense scheme and coverage, a role board with 1-5 star fit per player, Advanced drawer (go-to player, after-timeout play, foul/2-for-1 rules, auto-timeout trigger, sub-out energy), Season Analysis with 14 letter grades. This is more transparent than FM's tactic screen.
3. **I picked Heliocentric** (Irving 86 plus Flagg). Grades instantly showed Ball movement F and Transition D ("was C"). The game told me the cost before I played.
4. **Oct 22 I moved to Five-Out Motion plus Switch Everything.** The tactics page warned "Switching with a slow big: opponents will hunt Lively II... Drop instead". I ignored it. Over the next games the post-game report said so five times: "Indiana hunted switches -5.3", "OKC hunted switches -8.3", and "Stop switching with a slow big on the floor. Go to Drop". Record 3-2, then I fell to 2-6 and 5-10 with Irving and Washington hurt. The feedback loop punished my mistake and said why.
5. **Nov 10 and Dec 17 staff verdicts.** Mancini: "Your change: too close to call ... between -0.9 and +1.0 points a game", then "Switch to Drop seems to have helped: +0.6 a game give or take 0.3 over 20 games. I'd keep it." This was the best moment in the game for me. It is the closest thing to FM's staff reports, and it admits uncertainty with a CI.
6. **Game plans.** Per-opponent counters show a verdict ("Should help", "No clear edge", "Could hurt") from staff replays. I added Press after makes vs UTA (won 131-128; post-game "Your plan: Press after makes +2.4 pts"; the "Dallas' full-court press created turnovers +6.1" line). Double-team Porter Jr. (+0.8). Switch coverage x3 (+0.8 to +1.1, one "bad night, over many games it should still help"). Plans are worth 1-2 points a game and the game says so.
7. **Oct 28 vs OKC, Nov 21-ish.** Live break screens ("What's deciding it") separate luck from looks: "HOU +7.9 pts luck", "made 69.6% eFG on looks worth 49.1%". Nothing in FM tells you this. Strong.
8. **Nov 16 to Feb 5.** Sim from 5-10 to 25-25 (20-15 stretch) in 21 seconds. Lively out 36 games (foot), Irving 21. Staff proposed trading Irving 86 and Washington for Curry 87 (38 years old, 3 years $178M): "the forecast barely moves your postseason chances". I stood pat. Odd advice to push.
9. **Apr 13, Play-In vs MEM, coached fully.** Lost 109-146. Memphis shot 82% eFG in Q1, 66% for the game on looks worth 53.7%: shooting luck -21. Then NOP 100-117 ("about a 1-in-25 shooting night", luck -33.6). The honesty is great. Two double-digit blowouts in a row from luck alone also feels bad, and it cost me the playoffs.
10. **Offseason 1.** Owner grade -7 ("Win 39: 40 wins +5. Play-in loss -35"). Re-sign screen is excellent: Lively asks $43.7M x5 with years/option sliders ("each year off adds 3%"), Bird rights, team options with staff verdicts (declined Risacher $17.4M and Biberovic, kept payroll $212.7M against a $213.1M tax line). Free agency day 30 exposed an exploit: Thybulle asked $12.75M on day 1, then $2.9M for 1 year on day 30 (77 OVR). Waiting out the market is a +EV loop. FA list also shows fit tags ("Three-point D D->F at 16 MPG") and the staff verdict on the offer sheet ("he doesn't fill it").
11. **Offseason 2 progression.** Flagg 83->87 (pot 95), Lively 86->87, Irving 86->82 at 35. The aging "Declining" tag and the "Summers are when young players take their steps" note landed.
12. **Season 2.** Tried Pace & Space (Transition A+, Spacing B->C+, Ball movement A+->B: the preview reads like FM's tactic stats). 12-9 then 49-33, 5th seed; first playoff series win "A first series win for FM Lifer".
13. **Game 7 at OKC.** The staff counter "2-3 zone: Should help" was my plan. I lost the offensive glass 23-6 (-17.9 pts). The post-game then said "no zone against strong rebounding teams" while crediting the plan "+1.4 pts". The pre-game recommendation contradicted the post-game diagnosis against the same team.
14. **Instances of rubber band?** Not seen. SAS 41-12, OKC 68-14: the world has clear tiers.

## What hooked me (top 5)
1. Feedback loop with evidence: "Your change: too close to call, -0.9 to +1.0 points", then "+0.6 a game give or take 0.3 over 20 games". The grade chips (was F, now A+) respond immediately.
2. Luck vs looks in every report: "made 64.9% eFG on looks worth 54.8%: DAL +13.2 luck", "about a 1-in-25 shooting night". It teaches basketball probability.
3. Tactic previews have numbers: expected shot mix 32/31/37, trips-ending %, "11% of trips on the break", figures "measured over 29 simulated games".
4. Contract negotiation depth: years vs price (+3% per year off), options, tax-line math, Bird rights, option verdicts from staff.
5. Player-fit explanations: roster role board 1-5 stars, FA offer sheets saying "he doesn't fill your need" and what grade changes at what minutes.

## What pushed me away (top 5)
1. **Major:** Live games are slow and low-leverage. ~2.5 min per quarter at 4x for a 2D dot court; my timeouts logged "DAL 11-12 since" which says they did nothing measurable. The break screen is more useful than the quarter. The staff call card ("Ice their screens") sat under the nav bar and vanished before I could scroll to it (Q4 vs HOU).
2. **Major:** Staff counters contradict themselves. "2-3 zone: Should help" (Game 7 vs OKC) -> lost glass 23-6, -17.9 pts. Post-game: "no zone against strong rebounding teams". Also the staff pushed Irving + Washington for a 38-year-old Curry while saying it "barely moves your postseason chances".
3. **Major:** Single-game variance blunts coaching. The play-in loss to MEM (-21 luck) and NOP (-33.6 luck) ended my season while the game says "nothing to change". Honest but the "Tactics" number per game (-0.1, +0.1 pts) shows tactics barely move a night.
4. **Minor:** Complexity that is shallow. Roles are five labels on a star scale, Shape has four options, counters list is 4-9 items with "No clear edge" for most. After ten opponents the answers rhyme: "Win the glass", "Take care of the ball", "Beat the zone with motion".
5. **Minor:** FA day-30 sale (77 OVR for $2.9M) and the staff's Curry advice make the market feel gameable.

## Against the genre (FM / BBGM / MyNBA / Hoop Land)
- vs FM: wins on explanation. FM tells me "your pressing is working" in a ratings pane; this game gives a confidence interval. Loses on levers (no per-player instructions or training schedule), no set pieces, no scouting tree, no press conferences. Complexity is mostly honest, not fake; the fake part is the near-duplicate staff counters.
- vs BBGM: far richer tactics and ownership; BBGM has faster seasons and no live play at all.
- vs MyNBA/2K: this is the "why", 2K is the "show". The 2D court is weaker than 2K; 3D untested here.
- vs Hoop Land: mobile-first like it, with sharper analytics.

## Would I pay and keep playing
- $7.99 phone unlock: yes. $14.99 Steam: only if live games get a faster mode or a tactics lab; I'd pay it for the report tabs alone.
- Hours: 40-60 hours to ten seasons, then I'd want a real replay or lab view to keep playing.
- Recommend to a friend: 8/10 for FM people, 6/10 for casual fans.

## The one change that would most raise my score
Make a coaching decision visible in the live game itself. Tell me, in the break report, which of my moves (timeout, sub, coverage) moved the margin by measured points and stop the staff from recommending counters that its own post-game then calls wrong (zone vs strong rebounders). That one fix would make a live game as satisfying as the post-game report already is.
