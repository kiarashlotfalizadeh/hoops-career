# 02 — The Rebuilder
## Who I am and what I played
Trust-the-Process fan, 26. I played Chicago (30th of 30 on the New League screen) as GM + Head Coach in the real league, with job security on. I played five full seasons, 2026-27 to 2030-31, about 410 regular-season games plus playoffs. I coached one game live: the 2028 play-in win at PHI, which I skipped from the Q1 break to the final. I simmed everything else. Device: 390x844 phone, headless. Rough time: about 3 hours of wall clock over about 110 scripts. One honest limit: I never found a lever for the staff's "Development coaching 22nd–24th of 30" figure, and I did not buy a star at a deadline.

## Play log
1. **Oct 2026, day one.** Chicago ranked 30th but held Giddey 83/87, Wilson 77/86 (20), Buzelis, Essengue and Swain, so it never felt like a wreck. The owner letter was clear: win about 27, give 6,700 minutes to players 23 or under, "two seasons without the playoffs", and "under 19 wins is a failed season". I liked being told the floor up front.
2. **Nov 20, 4-10.** I used the Block tab: pick a player and every team's best package appears. Jones (77, 27yo) went to Utah for Keyonte George (77, 23yo) plus two 2nds. Powell (79, 33yo) went to Cleveland for Watson (75, 24) and Proctor (68, 22). Collins went to Orlando. The confirm sheet gave the staff's read: "Into the starting five: George (new)... payroll after $162.9M". It felt like real GM work.
3. **Dec 27, 5-26.** The tank worked, probably too well: 1-16 after the trades. The staff raised "On pace to miss the owner's floor" with two buttons, "Chase the floor" or "Tell him it's a development year". I confirmed the development year. The text said "your standing drops 10 points now", but the visible job security went 42 to 39. Finished 22-60, 13th in the East (14-37 at the deadline, owner "rising"). I cleared the floor of 19 by 3 wins.
4. **Feb 2027 deadline.** Offers for my vets were all 2nds. Smith (77, expiring) fetched Ace Bailey plus a UTA 2032 1st. The AI offered 1sts only for my kids: Buzelis for Dunn plus a 2030 1st, Essengue for Achiuwa plus a 2032 1st. I kept the kids. Giddey broke his foot (27 games), which helped the tank.
5. **Jun 2027 lottery.** I was projected #3, drew #4 ("You slip to No. 4... the balls didn't bounce your way"), and Houston jumped from 2nd to 1st. I drafted Vasilije Nikolić (PG, 75/83-87). The 2027 draft had a combine, mock draft percentages ("68% Nikolić at #4") and 27 trade offers for my pick. Nikolić later stalled at 74-77 and his POT fell to 80. Wilson was All-Rookie 1st Team.
6. **Summer 2027 FA.** I had no cap space and used the mid-level on Cam Whitmore (75/80, 22yo). He had just won Most Improved on his old team; the moment screen was a nice touch.
7. **2027-28: 40-42, 10th.** Same roster plus one summer: 7-7, then 21-18, then 26-27, finishing 40-42. The owner gave a Coach of the Year award (expected 26 wins) and a +56 grade. I lost the play-in elimination at BOS. The report said Chicago won the free-throw margin by 22, which I did not believe.
8. **Summer 2028, the turn.** I looked at 2028-29 cap sheets and saw a big FA class (Castle 84/90 at 23, Topić 81/87, A. Davis). I dumped George (to LAC for a 2nd) and Watson (to SAC for Dunn), which cleared $54.9M of space. I offered Castle the max and signed him on day 6: **4 years at $45.5M**. This was my favourite beat of the playthrough. Castle went 87 and then 88.
9. **A trap on the way.** I used my mid-level on Stewart while Castle's offer was pending. The mid-level took the cap space, and the Castle offer "fell through" with $45.5M left against his $46.35M ask. I only learned that from an inbox message afterward. I released Cooks, re-bid $45.5M (a little under his ask), and he accepted.
10. **2028-29: 43-39, 7th seed.** Swept 0-4 by 62-20 Charlotte with Castle (broken hand) and Giddey out. The owner said "I am proud of them" for a +13 grade.
11. **Giddey.** I extended him at $49.1M x4 as 85/88 at 25. Then 83, 84, and 80/80 at 27. I could not trade him: "No team has a package for Giddey that works on value and salary". That is a real GM lesson, and it hurt.
12. **2029-30: 49-33, conference finals.** After the deadline we went 19-9, 6th seed, 4-3 over MIL, 4-3 over PHI. The story moments fired in order: first series win, first conference finals, a franchise playoff record. Season grade +58. Orlando phoned with a job offer (they wanted to hire me away). This was the satisfying arc.
13. **Summer 2030.** I re-signed Wilson (86/90, 23yo) at $52.1M x5 and Whitmore at $16.5M, signed Mara (C, 75/79) on the mid-level, and the payroll hit $274M.
14. **2030-31: 46 wins, lost in the play-in.** The owner's new third goal, "payroll under $264.0M", failed by $10.2M (-55), and I paid $37.7M in tax. Season grade -18, job security 57 "Steady". OKC (4 titles) and SAS (43-10 at the break) ran the league. Five seasons: 22, 40, 43, 49, 46 wins, but I never finished above the 6th seed.

## What hooked me (top 5)
1. **Free agency as a real negotiation.** "He weighs offers and decides on day 3. Bidding: MEM, DAL +15", "no other team can pay his ask... price comes down, never under $34.8M", and then the loss mail "Hartenstein chose MEM... their .634 against our .524... about $16.9M a season from us". I have not seen another game explain why you lost. Castle at $45.5M was my best moment.
2. **The Block tab.** One tap shows each team's best package, labelled "Fair / Light / Weak / Salary dump", and the confirm sheet states the effect on starters and minutes. It made selling vets a 10-minute puzzle.
3. **The owner as a character.** The letters carry numbers, there is a floor ("under 19 wins is a failed season"), a two-season grace, and the "Tell him it's a development year" tradeoff. Season reviews give a grade by part. The rebuild clock feels real.
4. **Growth page and priority slots.** Staff priority has two places, each with a stated cost to the others, so choosing Brown over Wilson was a decision with a number on it. The after-summer mail names who rose and fell. Wilson 77 to 86, Brown 74 to 82, Castle 84 to 88, Giddey 85 to 80 is a story I can follow.
5. **The turn is visible.** 22-60, then 40-42, 43-39, 49-33 and the conference finals, with a franchise record and a first series win along the way. The arc exists, and the dip at the end made it feel less scripted.

## What pushed me away (top 5)
1. **Mid-level silently breaks a pending offer (major).** With cap space and a $46.3M Castle bid pending, signing Stewart on the mid-level ate cap space and killed the Castle offer. The sheet showed "Over the cap after $0.9M" but never said it would void the bid. I found out from the inbox. It cost me three days and a trick (releasing Cooks, bidding under his ask).
2. **Trade lists show only this season's salary (major).** I got George at "$6.6M thru 31-32" in the Block list. In 2027-28 he cost $28.7M, and the summer left me over the cap. Showing the full year-by-year deal in the offer card would stop a lot of bad rebuild planning.
3. **Development is mostly a coin flip, and I have no lever (major).** Staff priority is +0.3 to +0.6 OVR a year, while summers swing -4 to +4. Nikolić (drafted #4) stalled at 74-77 and POT fell 84 to 80; Giddey fell -4 at 26-27 right after $49M x4. "Development coaching 22nd of 30: +0.0" never changed over five years. A hardcore rebuilder wants a facilities or staff lever.
4. **The playoffs button (minor).** While alive in a series, "Sim to end of playoffs" is not offered. There is only "Sim 1 day" or "Quick sim this game", and each elimination game stops on a confirm sheet. It is a chore when I have no intention of coaching.
5. **The tank is lopsided (minor).** Selling two starters turned 4-10 into 1-16 and 22-60 with a 77-83 core still there; Chicago's "30th of 30" label did not match a roster with Giddey 83, Wilson, Buzelis and Swain. I still could not tell what a tank costs: I missed the dev-year text by 7 points of job security, and the pick still slid to #4.

## Bugs (repro steps)
- **Mid-level voids a pending cap-space offer.** Day 1 of free agency with $54.9M space: submit a max offer to a top FA, then sign a mid-level FA. Day 2 the inbox says "Our offer... fell through: we no longer have the cap space". Screenshots: `players/02-rebuilder/castle.png`, `whitmore2.png`.
- **Development-year text vs outcome.** The call said "Your standing drops 10 points now"; job security went 42 to 39. Screenshot: `devyear.png`.
- **"What decided it" does not add up.** After the 2028 play-in win the report listed +16.8, -16.7, +13.2 and +4.4 and said "free throws +22" in the margin sentence, with CHI 33-42 from the line against PHI 11-17. Hard to trust. Screenshot: `live5.png`.
- **#4 pick labelled "Reach · ranked 7th"** even though the mock gave Nikolić 68% at #4 and he was the highest-rated name I took. Screenshot: `drafted.png`.
- **Elimination-game sheet blocks "Sim to end of playoffs".** A scripted loop stalled 60 rounds on Apr 20 until I clicked "Sim it"; a player would see the same dead-end only if they missed the sheet. Screenshot: `quick.png`.
- **Lottery oddities:** the #1 pick went to a team with 2% to 14% odds in all three lotteries I watched (HOU 2nd-to-1st, MIL 11th-to-1st at 2.0%, ORL 9th-to-1st at 5.3%). Possibly genuine variance, but three in three felt rigged; HOU and OKC also held several lottery picks via old trades, which felt odd for contenders. No console errors in the sessions I logged.

## Against the genre (FM / BBGM / MyNBA / Hoop Land)
- **vs Basketball GM:** it wins on explanation (why a player signed elsewhere, what a trade does to my rotation) and on the owner layer. BBGM's sim feels more honest at the top: its stars drive wins, while here an 88/86/82 core went 46 to 49 wins.
- **vs FM:** the Growth page, priority slots and training focus echo FM's development screens, but FM gives me coaching and facility levers and this does not. The draft board (combine, mock percentages, staff read) is FM-quality.
- **vs MyNBA:** better cap and FA rules, much better text. It lacks MyNBA's lottery show: the reveal is a table.
- **vs Hoop Land:** better world and story; Hoop Land still has more depth on live play.

## Would I pay and keep playing
$7.99 on a phone: yes, easily, for the offseason layer alone. $14.99 on Steam: probably, once a facilities or development lever exists and the sim stars matter more. I would put 40 to 60 hours in, mostly per-season loops of about 20 minutes. Recommend to a friend: **8/10**.

## The one change that would most raise my score
Give the rebuilder a real development lever (staff, facilities or a youth-development budget that moves the "+0.0 OVR a year" line), and show the full year-by-year contract on every trade and FA card. Together they turn "which summer rolls well" into "which plan I chose".
