# 06 — The Game-Day Coach (37, youth-basketball coach)

## Who I am and what I played
- Real league, Phoenix Flares (West), GM + Head Coach. The brief said "take a mid team". Phoenix turned out to be a lottery team: owner target "win about 33", net rating -4.8 over 30 games.
- Phone (390x844) for the 2D live games. 3D looked at on desktop (1280x800) and on a phone in a second profile.
- Played Oct 21 to Dec 26 (31 games, record 10-20, 12th West).
  - 17 games opened live. The game counts 15 as "coached live". The other 2 I auto-skipped as blowouts.
  - 3 of the 17 were close games decided in the last 5 minutes. The rest were decided by Q3, so I used the "Game decided / Sim to the end" prompt.
  - 13 games were simmed by the staff (one "Sim 1 month", 3.2 s) as a control group.
- I did not reach 25 live games or the playoffs.
  - A live game takes about 9-12 minutes of wall time in headless Chromium. That is Q1 to Q4 at 2x, plus pauses for staff calls, ATO and last-possession calls.
  - The rest of the Season Analysis reading below is therefore on 15 live games, not 25.
- About 6 hours of wall time, mostly waiting on live games.
- Tools used: timeouts, subs, staff calls, the Tactics tab (mentality, coverage, "Every setting", "Late game" sheet), last-possession and ATO play calls, Hack-a-player and "Double a star" options (looked at), the live 2D and 3D court, and the four-tab game report.

## Play log
1. **Oct 21, opener at POR (L 100-121).**
   - Before tip I read the game plan page. It lists who guards whom, with the numbers ("Ball handling 96 vs perimeter defense 79", "Your defender gives up 7 inches"), plus Special plans: Double a star, Hack-a-player, Junk defense.
   - Halftime card, down 10: "Portland's shots fell above their looks: 66.7% eFG on looks worth 49.5% — that part is luck, with nothing to change."
   - Final report: Tactics -1.6, Shooting luck -30.7, Your moves -0.6. We shot 8-43 from three. I would never have believed the number if the game had not itemised it.
   - I lost by 21 and felt it was the dice, not me. That is rare for a sim.
2. **Counterfactual on my timeouts.** The Summary tab says "The rest of the night replayed 200 times with your moves and without them." Each timeout then shows "Replayed without it: +0.2 pts ±0.2, inside the noise." I have not seen this in any other sim, and it is the best thing in the game for a coach.
3. **Oct 24 vs GSW (W 128-114).** Up 20 at Q4 7:29 at 99%. Tapping "Sim to the end" is a two-step confirm ("Skip the last 2:45? Yes, sim it"). Fine.
4. **Staff call at the Q3 break vs DEN: "Hedge on ball screens" (Denver's ball screens: 21 pts on 14 trips).** Accept or "Not now". It felt like a real assistant talking. I accepted. The Season Analysis later listed "Hedging: Helped".
5. **Blown lead vs ORL (Nov 5, L 117-132).** Up 75-58 early in Q3 (73% to win). Lost by 15. At 89-89 with 1:37 left in Q3, ORL ran a 10-2 and I burned a timeout. Pemberton had flagged nothing. I did not see a *reason* for the collapse in the report beyond "free throws" and shooting. I wanted a coach's answer (fatigue? foul trouble? who was on the floor?).
6. **Crunch time vs CHA (Nov 12, L 113-118).**
   - Q4 3:58, PHX +3, 74% to win, then CHA went on an 11-2 run.
   - Opened the **Late game** sheet. It has foul-when-trailing, foul-when-up-3, 2-for-1, offense-defense subs, foul to give, a Hack-a-player threshold slider and "Auto timeouts to stop runs (8-0)". I felt like a coach for the first time here.
   - Last possession, down 3, 0 timeouts left: the game paused on a "Call the play" card. Options were "Iso: Booker", "Hammer: Kennard (a corner three on the kick-out)", "No call". It showed a board diagram (arrow = cut, bar = screen) and a foul-trouble swap list (Booker 5 fouls, Green in). I picked Hammer, and we lost.
7. **Out-of-timeout play call.** After a timeout the same card says "Out of the timeout: any set, or an iso for Booker", with "Run 1x, they've seen it" on repeated sets. That is genuinely ATO depth.
8. **Nov 12 data check.** Season Analysis after 9 live games: "Your live coaching +0.7 pts ±2.4 ... No proof either way yet ... The staff's bench would have done about as well." 26 timeouts +2.1 ±1.7. Nothing proven.
9. **Nov 25, W 116-115 @ HOU.** My one close win. Booker 34. The game was decided on the last possession, with a play call.
10. **Control group.** After 17 games (5-12, net -4.9) I simmed 13 straight games with the staff: 5-8, net unchanged at -4.8 over 30 games. By results, my live coaching and the staff's sims are indistinguishable. The game says the same thing in words (+2.3 pts ±3.4 over 15 live games).
11. **Dec 26 stats.** 10-20, 12th West. Owner "holding, Steady". Three trade offers (ATL wants Dunn for Newell + 2 picks). I did not engage the front office.
12. **3D look (Nov).** See below.

## What hooked me (top 5, each with evidence)
1. **The "Your moves" counterfactual.** "The rest of the night replayed 200 times with your moves and without them" and "Replayed without it: -0.5 pts ±0.4, inside the noise" for every timeout, on the Summary tab of each game. It tells a coach whether the thing they did mattered. No other sim does this.
2. **The luck ledger.** Halftime "That part is luck, with nothing to change" and the final "looks +21.7 · shooting luck -30.7 · free throws -12 = -21". I stopped blaming myself for a 21-pt loss.
3. **Last-possession and ATO play calls.** Down 3 with 21.7 left and no timeouts: "Call the play: Iso Booker / Hammer Kennard", with a diagram and foul-trouble swaps. The pause on the last possession is exactly what a coach wants.
4. **The Late game sheet.** Foul when trailing / up 3, 2-for-1, offense-defense subs, Hack-a-player threshold, auto-timeouts at 8-0. The auto-timeout default means the staff covers the most common coaching reflex.
5. **Staff calls at the break.** "Hedge on ball screens: Denver's ball screens: 21 points on 14 trips. The staff thinks this helps from here." Accept/Not now, with evidence. The Season Analysis later graded it ("Hedging: Helped").

## What pushed me away (top 5, each marked)
1. **My coaching provably does not matter in the numbers the game itself shows (major).** +2.3 ±3.4 over 15 games. 36 timeouts at +2.0 ±2.1. 28 subs at -0.9 ±2.0. The staff's sim over 13 games went 5-8, the same as my 5-12 in rate. As a hobbyist this is honest and deflating; as a coach I want one lever with clear, large effect.
2. **Blown leads with no coach's answer (major).** ORL: up 17, lost by 15. CHA: up 13, lost by 5. The report cites free throws and shooting luck, but not fatigue, foul trouble or who was on the floor. I wanted "Booker sat 9 minutes in Q1 and we lost the first quarter."
3. **Staff rotation oddities (minor).** In game 1 the Q1 floor was Gillespie, Green, Highsmith, Dunn, Brooks while Booker (88 OVR) sat with 9 minutes. Highsmith (71) played 19 minutes for 0 points. Gillespie got an F in game 1. I could fix this at breaks, but the default looked wrong.
4. **Phone viewing is small (minor).** Upright 2D court is fine. The 3D court upright on a phone is a ~210px strip with a "Watch sideways" overlay that blocks it until you tap it. A coach holding a phone in one hand will probably play 2D.
5. **Live games take 9-12 minutes each (minor, a feature for some).** There is a 2x speed button but no visible faster speed (see Bugs). 25 live games would be an evening for a dedicated coach, a week for a casual one. The Skip ahead prompt at 99% is the right release valve.

## Against the genre
- **vs FM:** FM has press, pause, and hundreds of shouts. This game has fewer levers but each one is explained and graded. The "Your moves" counterfactual and "luck vs looks" ledger are better than anything FM ships.
- **vs BBGM:** BBGM has no live coaching at all. This wins easily on depth.
- **vs NBA 2K MyNBA:** 2K lets you play, not coach. Here you coach but cannot play. If you want a possession-level coach seat, this is the only one.
- **vs Hoop Land / Draft Day:** Hoop Land is more fun to watch. This has far more explanation.

## Would I pay and keep playing
- **$7.99 phone unlock:** yes, for the game-day seat alone.
- **$14.99 on Steam:** probably yes, if the franchise side matches (I did not test it).
- **Hours:** 30-40 on a first season if I coached live, 100+ if the playoffs and FA are as good as game day.
- **Recommend to a friend:** 8/10 for a basketball nerd, 5/10 for someone who wants to feel clever fast.

## The one change that would most raise my score
Give me one lever whose effect I can *see* and *prove*. For example: a guaranteed, visible response to a timeout in a real run (the staff or the opponent's run stops ~70% of the time, not "inside the noise"), or an in-game "if you sub X for Y you gain Z" preview that the replay counterfactual later confirms. Right now the game honestly tells me my coaching reads +2.3 ±3.4, and the staff's sim is no worse. A coach needs to feel that coaching can win games.
