# 07 — The Career Journeyman
## Who I am and what I played
Real league, GM + Head Coach, job security on, phone 390x844. I started with Charlotte (East, 39-win goals) and played 11 full seasons across three jobs: Charlotte 2026-30 (4 seasons), Toronto 2030-32 (2), Milwaukee 2032-37 (5), then I was fired. I coached one live game (a Game 7 tip-off, then "Sim to final" from the Skip menu). I simmed everything else, took the staff's calls as "ignore", and made the key offseason moves (re-signs, one free-agency summer with $66M of space, job changes). I also tried deliberate sabotage to get fired. About 6 hours, mostly scripted loops with a 2D court. `npm run season-test` ran once; `star-check` not run.

## Play log
1. **2026-27 Charlotte, 40-42, 7th seed.** Release 2 players to reach 15: staff picks Connaughton and Dillingham, $10.7M dead money, one tap. Play-in win in OT at PHI, then lost Game 7 at DET 105-107. Owner grade +25. Clear, honest, and I felt the stakes. I coached the Game 6 tip-off: the live screen is dense but fine on a phone. Close and resume works (the app said "Picked up where you left off").
2. **Summer 2027: re-sign.** Miller (85/87, age 24) asked $43.7M x5 (market $42.8M). The negotiation sheet (ask, interest, years, options, "each year off what he wants adds 3%") is the best contract UI I have seen in this genre. I let the staff's pick decline the rookie option on Salaun (58/64, $10.4M), which keeps me under the tax line by $2.6M.
3. **2027-28 45-37, 2028-29 52-30.** I lost the first round both years (2-4 to DET, then 2-4 to PHI). Youth growth did the work: Steinbach went 74, 79, 84, 86 (a draft steal), Knueppel 85, 88, 89, Anderson Jr. 73, 76. The "season in development" mail reads like a real report.
4. **2029-30 Charlotte, 50-32, 4th seed, Finals.** I beat MIL 4-1, ATL 4-1, then BOS 4-3 (Game 7 win) and lost to NOP 2-4. Coach of the Month, first Finals, "Signature wins" on the career page. This was the best stretch. I felt hooked.
5. **Took a job from the Jobs tab (Toronto, 21-61).** One tap on "Take the job", with **no confirmation**. I walked away from a Finals roster by misclicking `nth(1)`. Then the inbox kept my Charlotte mail but re-labelled it with Toronto staff names ("You hold #23, #35, #53" was Charlotte's lottery; Toronto actually picked #4).
6. **Summer 2030 Toronto: free agency with $66.4M space.** Most stars are "Not keen" because Toronto won 21. Giannis (82, age 35) wanted $20.4M x1. I offered it and lost to OKC. The inbox then said OKC offered $15.4M, and that their record and market make "the same salary count about 39% more". That is the best FA loss message I have seen in a sim. I signed Karim Lopez (80/86, age 22, $35.5M x4) and Barrett (77, $17.35M x3).
7. **Toronto 20-62 (grade -46) then 28-54.** The roster was 81/80/78/78/76 plus 20-year-old prospects, and still the worst team in the East. The owner accepted it as a rebuild ("expects 29 wins"). Security stayed at 46, "Steady". Reputation fell from 63 to 53 (10th to 20th of 30 coaches) with plain numbers.
8. **Milwaukee (36-46), 2032-37: 38-44 (8th seed, lost 0-4 to IND), 44-38, 36-46, then 18-64.** I accepted several "Take the job" offers. Re-sign asks looked strange: Ament 84/87 age 25 asked $53.4M x4, but at 29 and 89 OVR he asked **$20.3M**.
9. **Sabotage attempt.** I turned off the assistant and swapped my top five with my bottom five. The game refused to start the season: "4 regulars with no minutes... Give the staff's share / Keep them out." Choosing "Keep them out" lets you tank. Stars ask for trades ("He expects 32 minutes a game and plays 0. He won't re-sign while it stands"), and every time one of the top eight becomes healthy the sim stops again with the same prompt.
10. **2035-36 18-64: grade -64, "Warm seat" (37).** The owner wrote "A second one in a row and you are out, whatever came before." That is a real warning with a stated rule.
11. **2036-37 16-66: fired.** At the break (13-45) the sim stopped on a full-screen "The owner's ultimatum: Win 19 games, or you are gone this summer." After the season: "You're fired" moment, "Between jobs", the league frozen until I take an offer. Denver offered; Houston, Miami and Chicago passed. The Jobs screen showed "Rated you 42, Mercer 42" and "Passed on you: Rated you 42, McAllister 71". Reputation was 46, 23rd of 30. Career: 387-515, .429, 7 playoff trips, 0 titles. The firing arc was dramatic and fair.
12. **Living league.** LeBron retired after 25 seasons (45,528 points, number one all-time); Curry and Durant both in 2028; Giannis in 2033 (number retired by MIL); Jokic 2033. Embiid and Towns went in the 2032 Hall class, Tatum in 2036. In-game news, records, HOF and coach carousel all update. Champions: OKC 3, DET 2, LAL 2, NOP/SAC/DAL/UTA 1 each in 11 years. SAC went 14-68, then 68-14, then 73-9.

## What hooked me (top 5)
1. **The career layer.** Season-by-season record, "Hall of Fame case... press's bar: two titles or 600 wins", signature wins with tip-off odds, milestones ("beaten every team in the league", "Coach of the Month"). Evidence: Profile page after 2029-30.
2. **Honest job market.** Openings list names who was hired and fired, "Rated you 53, Prescott 60". Reputation is a number with a rule. Moving from CHA (Finals) to TOR to MIL feels like a career.
3. **Firing arc.** Warm-seat mail, mid-season ultimatum sheet "Win 19 games", fired moment, league frozen until the next job.
4. **A living world.** Retirement mails with career lines (LeBron 45,528), number retirements, HOF classes, record book updating (73-9 SAC, 40.8 Uzoh), a changing coach table.
5. **Free agency explained.** "Giannis chose OKC over us... the same salary counts for about 39% more from them" and "Winning him would have taken about $21.4M a season from us."

## What pushed me away (top 5)
1. **Superstar creep (major).** PPG leader climbs 32.7 to 40.8 over 8 years while league PPG is flat; Uzoh won the scoring crown every year after 2031 and Lefevre/Dordevic/Uzoh sat at 98 for 5+ seasons. Peak players do not age: Wemby 97 to 93 at 33, SGA still 94 at 33.
2. **Unguarded irreversible job move (major).** "Take the job" on the Jobs tab fires with one tap and no sheet. I left a Finals team by accident; the inbox then carried Charlotte's mail under Toronto staff names.
3. **A bad team can't tell when it's bad (minor).** Toronto's 81/80/78/78/76 five lost 62 and 54 games. The staff's explanation ("shots that didn't fall", "That's luck, not the plan") fired on 9-game losing streaks at -15 a game.
4. **Pricing cliff by age (minor).** Ament asked $53.4M at 84 (age 25) and $20.3M at 89 (age 29). A 29-year-old star at 40% of last year's price is exploitable.
5. **Giving up control is blocked or loops (minor).** The release-to-15 prompt renames per player ("Release Fred VanVleet · staff's pick", 76 OVR cut for 'least value'); my scripted re-sign loop spun for 80 iterations because "Keep negotiating" is the default close of the open-FA sheet.

## Bugs (repro steps, screenshot path)
- **Inbox re-labels old mail after a job move.** Career, Jobs, Take the job (Charlotte to Toronto). Inbox still shows Charlotte's lottery and development reports under Toronto staff names. `.../players/07-journeyman/peek.png`
- **"Take the job" has no confirmation.** `takejob.png` was never produced because the sheet never opened; the one tap immediately changed the club.
- **Owner goals reset between preseason and review.** First-hour card said "win about 39 games"; the season review said "Win 38 games" (preseason final numbers move; stated though).
- **Contract total drifts.** Offer sheet: Miller $43.73M x5 = Total $256.5M; the news item later says "signs for 5 years, $241.6M".
- **"Rating the 7th seed" label.** Matchup card showed Charlotte "8th East" while the bracket said "(7) Charlotte" after winning the play-in.
- **Standings abbreviation** shows "PHI / PHI" in the pre-season standings table.
- **Tactics 0.0 pts next to three listed positive causes** in the final report: header "Tactics 0.0 pts" beside "+4.2 / +3.5 / +3.0" decisive items (Game 6 report, `s11b.png`).

## Against the genre (FM / BBGM / MyNBA / Hoop Land)
- **vs BBGM:** BBGM has the same 20-year hook, but this game's mail and news give each year a plot, and its owner/firing rules read like FM's board. BBGM's league stays steadier at the top because ratings have no 98 cluster.
- **vs FM:** the ultimatum/sacking/agent/job market is as good as FM's, with clearer numbers. Tactics depth is less visible when you sim.
- **vs MyNBA:** this wins on honesty and offline sim; MyNBA wins on presentation.
- **vs Hoop Land / Draft Day Sports:** history and records pages are far better here.

## Would I pay and keep playing
$7.99 phone unlock: yes. $14.99 Steam: yes, if the top-end drift is trimmed. Hours: I'd put in 60 to 100 over a career, mostly in the 10-30 second sim loop plus the offseason sheet. Recommend to a friend: 8/10.

## The one change that would most raise my score
Cap the superstar tail: let 30+ stars decline faster, stop the PPG leader from reaching 40, and keep one or two 98s a decade instead of three at once.
