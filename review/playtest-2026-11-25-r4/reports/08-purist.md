# 08 — The Real-NBA Purist (22)

## Who I am and what I played
- **Career 1 (profile/):** real league, New York Knights, **GM only**, one full 2026-27 season plus play-in, playoffs, awards, retirements, draft lottery, draft and re-sign period. 82 games, 6 playoff games. 0 live games coached (I wanted to watch how the AI coaches).
- **Career 2 (profile2/):** real league, New York again, **GM + Head Coach**, a second full 2026-27 season. 1 live game (Oct 20 vs PHI, ~5 min watched, then "Sim to final"), the rest simmed.
- **Career 3 (profile3/):** fictional league (New York), GM + HC, 30 games (to Dec 21).
- Phone viewport 390x844, 3D off. About 3 hours of play, plus a lot of table scraping: 446 real ratings rows, 420 fictional ones, 301 stat lines, 30 team pages, about 40 player pages.
- Caveat: I know the real league to about June 2026. Where the game is ahead of me, I say so.

## What hooked me (top 5)
1. **The real past is there, in depth.** Title counts are exact: BOS 18, LAL 17, GSW 7, CHI 6, SAS 5, OKC 2 (1979 plus 2025), MIA 3, SAC 1 (1951 Royals). Franchise leaders are real (Westbrook 18,859 for OKC, Brunson 9,688 for NYK, LeBron 23,119 for CLE). The record book has Adebayo's 83 against WAS on Mar 10, 2026, and the SAC 176-175 LAC game. LeBron's career total is 43,440 before this season.
2. **Real salaries and stat lines.** Jokić $59.0M, Giannis $58.5M, Butler $56.8M plus a torn ACL, SGA 31.1/4.3/6.6. This is not a ratings-only roster.
3. **The 65-game rule and the award logic.** Dončić, Harden, Brunson, SGA and Cade are all flagged "Ineligible (<65 GP)" for awards. The result is that the MVP in run 2 is Jokić, not the 50-game SGA.
4. **Postgame honesty.** "Toronto made 68.8% eFG on looks worth 51.3%: -23.1 pts" is what a hardcore fan wants. It admits the game was a fluke without calling it a bug.
5. **The league moves without you.** Retirements, a coach carousel (Atlanta hires Will Sorensen after 52-30 in LA), hot takes and all-star picks (run 2: Giannis, Tatum, Mitchell, Maxey, Brunson in the East). Two runs gave two different champions (CLE, BOS) and two different MVPs.

## What pushed me away (top 5)
1. **Invented names break immersion (major, but by design).** Every head coach is fictional (Mike Ivers, Glenn Mancini, Andre Crenshaw). I know Mike Brown, Mazzulla and Kenny Atkinson, and I never see them. Nicknames mix the clever with the bland: Lagoons/Cutters/Stirrups/Twine/Prospectors/Founders vs Knights/Warlords/Shamrocks, which are one letter off real ones. Arenas repeat: "Atlanta/Denver/Detroit/Cleveland/Miami/Los Angeles Arena" and two "Forums" (Memphis, Milwaukee). Worse, the record book keeps real franchise names ("Golden State Warriors 73-9", "Charlotte Bobcats", "Detroit Pistons 186-184"), so the game does not hold to one story.
2. **The AI breaks up star teams (major).** In run 1 Curry went from GSW to MEM, Booker from PHX to HOU, Adebayo from MIA to SAS and Sabonis from SAC to CHA. In run 2 Booker was on MEM, not HOU, and Curry stayed in GSW. A star leaving each year is fine; four marquee names in one season is not. GSW (27-55) and PHX (28-54) both collapsed after a star left. Real GMs do not dump Curry in January.
3. **Star usage and scoring redistribute oddly (major).** Embiid, with the same weapons as before, falls from 26.9 (2025-26) to **17.9 ppg** in 62 GP while Jaylen Brown averages 26.2. Giannis falls to 24.1/9.3 in 28.7 min (27.6/9.8 real). Kessler falls to 10.6 from 14.4. In run 2 **Anthony Davis wins Sixth Man of the Year** off the bench at 18.0 ppg on a team (WAS) that rates him 87 and a real starter. Brunson went up 2 ppg to 28.2, so the rule seems to be that the player with the ball scores. Also, **Kawhi plays 80 games at 34.6 min** and Curry 80. Real load management and injuries would cut these.
4. **A few 'it is a game' glitches (minor).** Age is year-based (LeBron 42, Wemby 23, Giannis 32; they are 41, 22, 31 at tip-off). Tyler Kolek (25, OVR 66), whom I released, **retires** after the season and shows up in the Hall of Fame "Notable retirements" list. LeBron and Westbrook go in the Hall in their retirement year (the real wait is 3+ years). The Edwards/Cunningham/Haliburton/Tatum player pages show both "All-League" and "2× All-League" badges, a duplicate.
5. **The fictional league is inflated and old (minor, the user-flagged mode).** Two 97s (Ezeli, Miles) and a dozen 90+ stars at ages 30-34 (Fowler 34, Bowman 34, Larentzakis 30). Real stars peak at 25-29 and the best players are usually under 31. Names recycle real surnames (J. Missi, J. Koloko, P. Adebayo, Ike Okongwu, N. Cunningham), which a purist reads as "wait, is that real?". Top-scorer lines of 52-57 pt games and TS% of 74% (Drew Burke, DET) come from generated stars.

## Bugs (repro steps, screenshot path)
- **Standings show "PHI PHI" for Philadelphia** instead of the city (every standings row, and the league playoff bracket). Open `/league?tab=standings`. `.../players/08-purist/shots/stand1.png` (and `_league_tab_standings.png`).
- **"Sim to end of playoffs" appears to do nothing** until you answer the closeout or elimination bottom sheet, which opens a beat later. My script clicked it 8 times with no change. Screenshot `shots/stuck2.png`. A human sees the sheet; a script or an impatient player may not.
- **The landing screen flashes on load.** Opening `/` for a moment shows the splash with "New league / Import backup" while the save loads. A new player might think the save is gone. I hit it once with `go(page,'/')` (the page text read "Hoops Career ... New league").
- **Live game "Skip ahead" is hidden** behind the speed control until you tap 2x, or at the next break. It is not listed in the live toolbar. Screenshots `shots/t5e.png`.
- **Team-card parse noise**: some "key players" on team pages show "Out 1 game" in the name slot; I did not screenshot it, so confidence is lower.
- **Franchise leaders footer is contradictory**: "Leaders count real seasons before 2026-27 too. The figures above count this league's only." (NYK Teams tab).
- **Career 1 draft**: the lottery text says "Houston 5th via BKN" while I saw 20-62 BKN in the table, which is plausible; not a bug.

## Against the genre (FM / BBGM / MyNBA / Hoop Land)
- **vs BBGM:** BBGM's real-player file is community-made and goes stale. This game ships real contracts, real history and an owner who reacts to them. It loses on random-player depth. The league also feels more alive than BBGM's: news, retirements, a coach carousel.
- **vs NBA 2K MyNBA:** 2K has real names, coaches, arenas and drafts; this game renames them. It wins on honesty (luck vs looks), the CBA and the sim log, and loses on the real-name feel.
- **vs FM:** the assistant "Nate Grantham" with tactics calls on a live game is the nearest thing to FM I've seen in a basketball game. I could not test depth deeply as a purist, but the playbook, shape and roles menu looks right.
- **vs Hoop Land / Draft Day:** better history, better live game, worse stat-card flexibility.

## Would I pay and keep playing
- **$7.99 phone unlock:** yes, because the real 2026-27 league is the hook. **$14.99 on Steam:** yes, only if the real-name layer (coaches, draft class, arenas) gets closer to real. I would not pay $15 for a league that renames Mike Brown.
- **Hours:** about 40, mostly one real-league franchise over 3-4 seasons, then I'd stop when the generated draft classes take over (the 2027 class is fictional, with a rated 87 at age 18).
- **Recommend to a friend: 7.5/10.**

## The one change that would most raise my score
**Stop the AI from breaking up real teams in season 1, and keep the real coaches.** One week of "Curry traded to Memphis in January" costs a purist more trust than any rating mistake. Then add the real head coaches (or at least their real names in the staff view) so the world I know stays the world I play.
