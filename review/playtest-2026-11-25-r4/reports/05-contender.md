# 05 — The Win-Now Contender, 24

## Who I am and what I played
Real league, Oklahoma City Twisters (SGA 96, Holmgren 92, Jalen Williams 86, Hartenstein 84), GM + Head Coach, job security on, 390x844 phone. I played four full seasons (2026-27 to 2029-30), about 330 regular-season games (mostly simmed) and about 60 playoff games. I coached roughly 55 playoff games live. In practice that meant pausing, taking Skip ahead to "Clutch time" and playing the last five minutes with the staff's calls. I watched one full first quarter, one elimination game start-to-clutch and one last-possession play call by hand. Season-4 R1 to Finals was all live. Roughly 3 hours of wall clock.

## Play log
1. **Oct 2026, first hour.** The Today screen opens on "owner expects 63 wins, reach the Finals, payroll under $232.7M", with a 71-win forecast and a four-step "first games" checklist. I understood the stakes in 30 seconds. I never found out how to start live coaching from the checklist. I only found it in the Play sheet.
2. **Oct 28 at DAL, first live game.** The staff's "2-3 Zone" call card appeared in the first quarter (5 points inside on 2 trips). I took it and won 126-106. The post-game screen is excellent: Tactics -0.5, Shooting luck +13.1, Your moves +2.1 pts ("replayed 200 times with and without"), four factors and player grades. Best analytics moment of the run.
3. **Dec 2026.** Out of the In-Season Cup in the semifinal 143-133 (DEN). Job security was 42 "warm seat" at 24-12, because I was on a 54-win pace against a 63-win ask. The owner bites even when you win.
4. **Feb 2027 deadline.** The staff's call was "stand pat". I tried Jalen Johnson (88) from ATL for the LAC 2027 1st plus Caruso plus Jaylin Williams. The GM said "Not even close", and "What would make this work?" explained that all my firsts count as 2 picks in one pile because extras count for less. Honest and useful.
5. **Apr 2027 R1 vs GSW (8th seed, 40-42).** Won G1 94-91 (led 49-25 in Q2, "GSW 1%", then lost the third quarter 13-32). Lost G2-G4 (I had left them on Quick sim by mistake, but SGA and Jalen Williams were hurt too). Down 1-3 as the 1 seed with an elimination-game tag. I coached G5 (+20), G6 (+16), G7 (+24) and won 4-3. Real drama.
6. **May-June 2027.** Beat DAL 4-0 and SAS 4-1, then CLE 4-1 in the Finals. SGA had 52 in a playoff game and a franchise-record note landed in the inbox. Title: "Champions, 4-1 over Cleveland", Finals MVP SGA, a "No. 0 to the rafters" story and an owner letter. I never saw a full-screen title moment, because my script clears them. This is a hole in my evidence (see Bugs).
7. **Summer 2027, the tax wall.** Wallace (78/84, 23) asked $18.3M. The re-sign sheet said "tax +$103.4M" on top, because I was $44M over the line and the marginal rate is ~5.6x. I dumped Caruso for Horford (saved $12.4M, about $60M of tax) and then let Wallace walk. A bargain young player was lost to the tax.
8. **2027-28.** 45-10 at the deadline, 64-18 final, 11 games clear of #2. Holmgren (92) went down in the second round (groin, 11 games) and UTA, the 5th seed, beat me 4-2. Owner grade -18 even with 64 wins.
9. **Summer 2028, the rookie-deal cliff.** Mitchell, Topić, Jaylin Williams and McCain all expired and asked $69.6M together. I re-signed all four to test the extreme. Payroll went to $303.9M and the tax to $333.8M. No warning beyond the numbers.
10. **2028-29.** 66-16 (+10.7), the best record of my four years, then lost R1 2-4 to a 47-35 team. My stars were hurt late. The owner then docked -100 for the payroll goal (I was $45.6M over) and -100 for the first-round loss. Grade -50 and "a second in a row and you are out, whatever came before." This was the most believable owner moment in the game.
11. **Summer 2029, I paid for it.** Topić went to PHI for Philon (75, $6.5M, saved $16.5M). Jalen Williams went to PHI for Kaba 80 plus Bitadze 79 plus Jordan 72 (saved $12.7M). Mitchell went to HOU for Eason 82 (saved about $2M). Payroll fell from $297M to $254M and then $242M after I let Hartenstein and Sorber walk. The Block tab ("best package from every team") plus the two-step "Make the trade" review ("you shed $16.5M, payroll after...") made this fun.
12. **2029-30.** 64-18. I coached the whole postseason live (skip to clutch). Finals G2 vs ATL stopped itself on "Last possession, your ball, down 2" with a play-call menu (Iso SGA / Chicago / Post split / Spain Mara). I called Iso. SGA drew a foul and went 2/2 to tie at 112 with 3.6 s left. Beat ATL 4-1 for the second ring.

## What hooked me (top 5)
1. **The owner has teeth.** 64 wins and a Cup but a second-round exit graded -18. 66 wins plus a $45.6M overshoot graded -50 and "you're out next time." Stakes survive winning.
2. **Post-game truth.** "Shooting luck +13.1 / Your moves +2.1 ±1.0 / 'replayed 200 times'" after my first live game told me exactly how much I mattered. I trusted the game from then on.
3. **The trade desk speaks.** "Not even close" with the pick math, a Block tab with 20+ packages, and the review sheet that reports payroll after the deal. I got Philon, Kaba, Bitadze, Eason and Horford out of slow-motion dumps.
4. **Playoff pressure beats.** "Elimination game" tags, the "You can close it out tonight: coach or sim it" sheet, and the last-possession play call with a timeout trade-off made Finals G2 feel like the Finals.
5. **A 1-3 comeback happened.** R1 vs GSW at 1-3, then +20/+16/+24. It felt earned, and it was a real swing in the series card.

## What pushed me away (top 5)
1. **Tax is a dynasty-killer (major).** Re-signing Wallace ($18.3M) added $103.4M of tax. Mitchell ($22.9M) added $63.7M. At $302M payroll my bill was $323M, and the books note was just "make it count." A championship core cannot keep its own rookie-deal stars and nothing tells you early. By year 3 the game is dumping salary every June, which is interesting once and a chore after that.
2. **Regular season is too easy (major).** 63, 64, 66, 64 wins; 7 to 11 games clear of #2 every year; net +10.7 to +12.8; 45-10 at the deadline twice. The staff said "stand pat" every deadline because nothing helped. No chase, no pressure until the playoffs.
3. **Playoff dice (minor to major).** A 64-win 1 seed lost in R1 or R2 in two of four years and was down 1-3 in a third. Injuries were the cause (Holmgren out 11, SGA/Jalen out). It feels random when it isn't ours to fix, and the injury news arrives with no counter-move except waiting.
4. **Repetitive staff calls (minor).** "2-3 Zone, the staff thinks this helps from here" was the call in about 20 of my ~35 live games, sometimes at 5 points inside on 2 trips. A coach tool that says the same thing stops reading as insight.
5. **Title celebration isn't there in my run (major, but my evidence is limited).** After both rings I got an inbox line and an owner email ("Champions. I knew that lineup would work."). I did not see a parade, a trophy screen or a full-screen moment, because my clearMoments calls swallowed them. If it exists it's easy to skip, and it didn't reach me.

## Bugs (repro steps, screenshot path)
- **Win probability / comeback pattern.** S1 R1 G1: led 49-25 in Q2 ("GSW 1%, OKC 99%") and won 94-91 after losing Q3 13-32 (screenshot: players/05-contender/r1g1-end.png). One game is not proof, but 99% to 3 points is the signature of a hidden catch-up. Worth a check against the engine log.
- **Contradictory attribution lines.** After the DAL game (s1 end screen): "Oklahoma City's zone clogged driving lanes: +21.6" and "Dallas won second chances off the zone's loose box-outs: -13.1", yet "Your moves +2.1" and "Tactics -0.5". Readers cannot reconcile +21.6 with +2.1.
- **Live game left on the Today bar.** If you Watch & coach, then leave the screen, the game keeps advancing in the background, and "Resume live game" says "Picked up where you left off". Hard to tell from the player's side whether it's paused.
- **Skip ahead stops on injuries and re-asks.** After "Skip stopped: Jal. Williams is hurt", a script that retried Skip took 60+ s per game. Fine for a person, but a bit heavy.
- **Quick sim on a decisive game opens a "You can close it out tonight" sheet.** Not a bug, but easy to miss (my loop silently looped 7 times on R1 G5 until I added "Sim it").
- **Release-to-15 flow is two taps** (staff's pick, then Confirm). Black's release left $7.0M dead money on the payroll the owner measures (screenshot: rel2.png).
- **Dead trades from the Block tab.** Two of my McCain/Stirtz "Open then Propose" attempts did not complete, because Propose opens a review sheet that needs "Make the trade". Not a bug, but the first Propose looked like a failed click.

## Against the genre (FM / BBGM / MyNBA / Hoop Land)
- **Beats BBGM** on coaching: counters, who-guards-whom, timeout play sets, last-possession menu, and the "replayed 200 times" move verdicts. BBGM has none of that, and its owner is a toy next to this one.
- **Beats NBA 2K MyGM** on honesty: no scripted losing streaks, and the sim tells me what was luck. 2K's franchise mode never says "shooting luck +13".
- **Loses to FM** on rhythm: FM makes you manage an ongoing match with a live voice and a squad that ages. Here the regular season is a long skip, and the playoffs are the only place the game wants me.
- **Loses to Hoop Land / Draft Day** on dynasty bookkeeping depth: the tax is a wall, not a puzzle, and there is no "second apron" style rule I can plan around.

## Would I pay and keep playing
- **$7.99 phone unlock:** yes, for the owner, the playoff series and the trade desk. About 25 hours for me.
- **$14.99 Steam:** maybe. I'd want the regular season to push back and a visible title celebration first.
- **Recommend to a friend:** 7/10.

## The one change that would most raise my score
Make the cap and tax a plan I can see: show next summer's tax bill when I sign a rookie-scale player, and let a champion keep one or two cheap young stars (a Bird-rights or rookie-extension discount), so the dynasty years feel like building, not triage. Pair that with a regular season that can actually bite a 66-win team.
