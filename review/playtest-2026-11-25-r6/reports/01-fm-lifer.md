# 01 — The FM Lifer
## Who I am and what I played
Fifteen editions of Football Manager, new to basketball sims. Real 2026 league, Dallas Wranglers (West, roster rank 14th of 30), GM + Head Coach, 390x844 phone. I played two full seasons plus the offseason between them, and the start of a third (the draft lottery of 2028). That was 82 + 82 games, 2 play-in runs and two playoff runs.
- Season 1 (2026-27): 41-41, 8th seed through the play-in (lost to GSW, then beat NOP). Swept 0-4 by OKC (69-13). Owner verdict "Good".
- Season 2 (2027-28): 38-44, 8th seed again. Beat OKC (65-17) 4-3 from 2-1 up, lost to MIN in round 2. Owner verdict "As expected" (-3). HOU and NYK then offered me their coaching jobs.
- Live games: about 40 sittings, mostly partial. I skipped to each break, read the report, changed things, then skipped on. Roughly 24 in S1 and 16 in S2, including both play-ins and 7 playoff games. The rest were quick-sims. About 130 turns of tool use.
- Per-opponent game plans: set before about 35 games. Counters were added by hand, because the staff's counter suggestions mostly read "Staff still looking…".

## Play log
1. **Oct 20, 2026, start.** Team picker shows each roster's rank, which is a nice touch. Today asked me to cut 17 to 15. The staff's picks were McGee (free) and Martin ($19.4M dead money), and it told me the dead-money cost before I confirmed. The owner's goals (about 40 wins, playoffs, 9,200 minutes for players 23 or under) came with 31% of the grade on youth minutes. I liked that a lot.
2. **Opener at HOU, live.** Pre-game screen: "Who guards whom" with matchup flags ("Finishing 92 vs interior defense 76"), special plans (double a star, hack-a-player, junk defense). This is the first basketball sim that made me feel at home as an FM player. The live break screen shows "What HOU is running" with points per trip by play type, plus a Hurting you tag and coverage buttons. I switched Drop to Hedge at the Q1 break and the game logged my move with "DAL 26-33 since". It was honest about my change failing. Lost 116-126 after a 24-41 fourth quarter.
3. **First post-game report.** Tactics +1.3, shooting luck -2.6, "how the margin adds up", four factors, player grades, "Next time" suggestions. This is the best part of the game. It beats FM's post-match analysis because it separates luck from tactics.
4. **1-5 start, Oct 31.** Irving, Flagg and Gafford all got hurt. Season Analysis said my live coaching read -0.9 pts +/-1.2 over 3 games: "no proof either way... the staff's bench would have done about as well". I wanted that number for years in FM. Grades also showed spacing F, ball security F, shot creation F. I switched the style to Five-Out Motion, and the screen showed the effect live (Spacing F to D, Ball movement D to B+).
5. **Oct 31, Lively extension.** Ask $43.7M x5. I typed $41.0M and he countered at $42.4M, with the tax consequence shown before I accepted. Real negotiation, and it felt like FM.
6. **Trade, Dec 17.** I asked ATL for Alexander-Walker for Prosper and Cisse. The UI said "Not even close... salary doesn't match: $14.4M against a $7.4M limit". "What would make this work?" proposed Christie + a 1st + $8.1M cash. I declined. The explanation was clear and the AI asked far too much.
7. **Dec 17 to Dec 27, S1.** The sample of games stayed even (13-12, then 15-15). I tried manual counters (Hedge, Ice, Switch, pressure) against every opponent and could not tell any of them from the others. Tactics/luck lines swung wildly: in one game "-1.6 tactics / +26.0 luck" for a 19-point win, in another "-4.4 / -23.3".
8. **Deadline, Feb 11.** Staff said stand pat on both a Bridges trade (Washington + Marshall + a 1st) and the inbound offers ("no clear edge"). I stood pat, 26-27. Then I sim'd to the end and finished 41-41.
9. **Apr 2027 play-in and playoffs.** Lost the 7/8 game 92-107 (a 18-31 fourth), then won the elimination game 110-101. In the OKC series (a 69-13 team) I used "This series" plan (Box-and-one on SGA + Hedge). Lost 0-4, two of them close (102-104).
10. **Offseason 1.** No draft picks (traded away before I arrived). Re-sign screen: I declined Risacher's $17.4M option and Biberovic's. Payroll fell from $227.4M to $207.0M, under the tax. FA: I bid the maximum my mid-level allowed for Small (lost to TOR by $0.1M) and Jaquez (lost to ATL), and the inbox post-mortems explained why in plain numbers ("TOR rates higher, for their record (.585 vs .500)... about 4% more"). I re-signed Risacher on the mid-level after he hit the market, plus two minimum vets.
11. **Season 2.** 6-4 start, then 12-23 by Jan 2 with Christie out for 27 games. Season Analysis at 56 games: net rating -0.2 (15th), 24-32, and every setting of mine (Drop, Ice, Switch, not crashing the glass) flagged "Cost you". Job security fell to 44, "Warm seat".
12. **April 2028.** Won the play-in (HOU) after losing the first one (NOP). Beat 65-17 OKC in 7: 2-1 up, lost two blowouts, then won Game 7 102-100 with the last break reading "OKC got the better looks +8.8". Round 2 lost 1-4 to MIN. Offers arrived from HOU and NYK for the head coach job.
13. **Sim speed.** A 4-5 game live batch took 8-10 minutes. A full season to the end sim'd in 8-15 seconds.

## What hooked me (top 5, each with evidence)
1. **The post-game "what decided it" layer.** "Houston got the better looks: shots worth 56.7% eFG to 48.5%, made 61.6% to 51.6%", plus "shooting luck -2.6 pts" and "how the margin adds up". It told me the same night what my plan did and what was variance.
2. **My live moves are graded.** "Ball-screen coverage: Hedge -> Ice | DAL 56-65 since" on the same screen as the half. Season Analysis then says: "over 3 games your in-game moves read -0.9 pts, inside the noise... the staff's bench would have done about as well." No other game dares to say that.
3. **Player-level negotiation with consequences.** Lively counter at $42.4M with "crosses the tax line, +$24.6M". The FA loss notes explained exactly how much more I needed to pay.
4. **Opponent play-type breakdown in the live break.** "P&R ball handler | 10 points on 5 trips | Hurting you" with the coverage buttons right under it. That is FM's opposition instructions, in basketball.
5. **Stakes that bite.** Job security dropped from 49 to 44 and went to "Warm seat" with a plain warning ("two of those in a row and he fires you"). Then NYK and HOU came calling after a 38-44 year.

## What pushed me away (top 5, each marked dealbreaker / major / minor, with evidence)
1. **"Staff still looking…" never resolved (major).** On every Game Plan page I opened (about 35 games), including a day before tipoff and the playoff series page, every counter read "Staff still looking…". Only twice did the Brief say "Switch coverage should help". The staff-measured counters are the game's pitch and I almost never saw them. I added counters blind.
2. **I could not tell if my game plans mattered (major).** Tactics and luck lines swung from +10 to -10 for the same plan. After ~35 plans I have no evidence they changed anything; Season Analysis said my own settings (Drop, Ice, Switch, not crashing the glass) "Cost you". The tool is honest, but the system gives the FM Lifer no reward loop.
3. **Luck dwarfs tactics (major).** "Tactics -1.1 / luck -7.8" and "+1.1 / +30.2", "+1.5 / -34.1". If luck routinely swings 30 points a game, coaching reads as noise. Either the luck model overstates, or basketball scores here are too volatile.
4. **Live games are slow to read on a phone (minor).** Real-time play at 2x with skip-to-break is fine, but nothing tells you what to tap first. I found Skip ahead by accident, and its options change by quarter (End of Q2 only appears after Q1).
5. **Capped team can't win FA (minor).** I lost Small by $0.1M and Jaquez by $0.05M, both because the mid-level max was below their lead price. Fair, and explained, but with a mid-level cap I never won a contested bid.

## Against the genre (FM / BBGM / MyNBA / Hoop Land: where it wins and loses for me)
- **vs FM:** it wins on speed of feedback (FM can't tell me what a mid-game shout did) and on honesty ("inside the noise"). It loses on depth of opposition instructions: no per-opponent individual instructions beyond who guards whom, no set-piece library, no training schedule, no scouting reports. FM's staff counters actually show up; here they mostly didn't.
- **vs BBGM:** BBGM's trades are an exploit-friendly AI; here the AI asked more than a fair price and told me why. BBGM has no live coaching, so this is far ahead on game day and coaching.
- **vs MyNBA/2K:** no contest on tactics clarity; 2K has nicer visuals and no analytics.
- **vs Hoop Land / Draft Day:** better explanations and CBA clarity than either.

## Would I pay and keep playing
- $7.99 phone unlock: yes, immediately.
- $14.99 on Steam: yes, if the staff-counter state and the game plan reward loop get fixed. Otherwise it feels thin on strategic stakes.
- Hours: about 60 in the first month, then I'd depend on whether tactics visibly change results.
- Recommend to a friend, 0-10: 8 for basketball fans, 7 for FM players.

## The one change that would most raise my score
Make the staff's counter suggestions actually show before games ("Staff still looking…" resolving into a measured gain), and then show a running "your game plans over N games" ledger. Right now the best analytics in the genre end up telling me my coaching didn't matter, and I can't act on that.
