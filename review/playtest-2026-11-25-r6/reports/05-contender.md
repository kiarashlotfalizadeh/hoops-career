# 05 — The Win-Now Contender, 24
## Who I am and what I played
Real league, Oklahoma City Twisters (best roster, 1st of 30; SGA 96, Holmgren 92, J. Williams 86), GM + Head Coach, job security on, 390x844 phone. I played 3 full seasons (2026-27, 2027-28, 2028-29) plus two offseasons, ending at the 2029 draft lottery. I did not reach season 4. About 25 live games coached (all of two playoff series, one regular-season game), the rest simmed. The machine was loaded (load average 14 on 4 cores), so live games were slow and I lost many hours to scripting. Records: 68-14 (champions), 67-15 (champions, 4-0 Finals), 60-22 (lost conference finals).

## Play log
1. **Oct 20, 2026 opener at SAS.** The owner card asks for 63 wins and the Finals, and the forecast says 71. Opener vs Wembanyama: 56% to win, a live game with a shot chart and "What's deciding it" at each break. Calloway said to stop blitzing good passers. I switched Blitz to Hedge in the Q3 break and the change appeared under "Changed tonight". Won.
2. **Nov 20, 14-1.** Four trade offers. "Why" gave a plain staff verdict ("fair value"). I accepted two (Spencer 80 for Topic 77, Melton 78 for Sorber 74). Payroll fell $216.2M to $213.7M and the tax bill fell from $27.3M to $21.2M. Confirming takes two taps. No story moment fired for either trade.
3. **Deadline Feb 2027 at 43-10.** The staff said "stand pat". My own run at ATL's Jalen Johnson (88) got "Not even close" with an explanation: ATL rate their side at about 4.7 firsts and mine at 1.9. A "What would make this work?" button is there. I stood pat.
4. **Regular season ended 68-14, +14.5 a game, 7 games clear.** Nothing pushed back. I was never worried before the playoffs.
5. **Conference finals vs LAL.** LAL had Doncic, and I lost G1 (102-117) with Jalen Williams and Hartenstein hurt. FT were 6-8 vs 22-31, and the game report said "looks +14, luck -13, free throws -16". G2 was a 107-117 loss, so 0-2. Then G3 W 108-103, G4 W 120-118 (timeout, called Iso SGA, "Est. +5% win"), G5 W 130-119, G6 L 108-111 (last-possession play call, down 3, lost), **G7 W 100-93 at home.** Best stretch of the game, and it felt earned.
6. **Finals vs CLE.** Lost G1 109-133, then won 4 straight. SGA scored 54 in the playoffs, a franchise record. Finals MVP and title No. 1.
7. **Offseason 1.** The cap sheet showed committed 27-28 payroll at $244.6M, tax line $213.1M, luxury tax $79.8M. I let Melton and Wallace walk, since re-signing both would have cost about $68M in tax. A confirm sheet lists who walks.
8. **Season 2 deadline.** I traded Mara, Riley, two firsts and two seconds for Deni Avdija (83) at only +$2.3M payroll. Went 67-15, won the Cup in December, swept CHA in the Finals (simmed). Title No. 2. I never saw the celebration moment because my script dismissed it.
9. **Offseason 2.** Committed payroll was $295.8M with a $277.7M tax on it. "Keep everyone" showed a tax of about $500M. Mitchell's extension ($28.2M) and Avdija's new deal ($34M) pushed payroll up. I let J. Williams and McCain walk and signed Pippen on the mid-level for 1 year.
10. **Dec 2028, 20-7.** The owner card dropped to 44, "Warm seat", even though I had won two titles and was 20-7. The owner's payroll line is the contracts I inherited plus $18.6M. It "moves with those deals, never with what you add." Payroll was $304.5M, so the Money goal scored -100.
11. **2028-29 finish: 60-22, lost the conference finals.** SGA tore his ACL (out 99 games). Season grade -38, job security 59. The tax bill was $338.6M. A dynasty arc in the shape the brief asked about.

## What hooked me
1. **The 0-2 comeback to a G7 win** over LAL, with clutch timeouts and play calls that moved win probability.
2. **"Why did that happen" reports.** The game report said looks +14.2, shooting luck -13.2, free throws -16 for a 15-point loss.
3. **The cap sheet and re-sign screen.** The Keep everyone / tax line / 125% maths made every re-sign a real decision.
4. **The trade AI explains itself.** ATL "rate your side at 0, 4.7 firsts vs 1.9".
5. **Owner card with side-bet, past reviews and per-goal scoring.** It made job security tangible.

## What pushed me away
1. **Regular season has no pushback (major).** 68-14 and 67-15 with a 71-win forecast. Two titles in the first three years made the sim feel solved until injury and tax intervened.
2. **Owner payroll goal is structurally unwinnable for a dynasty (major).** The line is inherited contracts plus room and never rises with what you add. Extending stars alone (Mitchell) made the Money goal -100, and security fell from 77 to 44 at 20-7 with two titles.
3. **Luxury tax has no practical cost beyond the owner line (major).** I paid $89M and $338.6M and the only effect was the owner score, which seems disconnected. The $500M "keep everyone" tax figure is absurd.
4. **Blocking salary dump (minor/major).** Trade offers for Caruso only returned salary, so I could not shed $20M+ even when it was the obvious move.
5. **Live game control (minor).** A closed page or a timeout with no Resume leaves a game stalled or completed. Quick skip settings do not auto-resume from the timeout screen.

## Against the genre
- **FM:** The coaching and press-your-own-staff depth is shallower (no training schedules), but live game coaching has more drama than FM's match view.
- **BBGM:** Much better presentation and explanation; trade AI is more honest. Loses on sheer speed of sim and sandbox.
- **MyNBA:** The CBA and cap sheet is clearer and the owner pressure is real, but there is far less roster flavor.

## Would I pay and keep playing
$7.99 phone: yes. $14.99 Steam: yes if a rebuild path holds up. About 40 hours. Recommend 8/10.

## The one change that would most raise my score
Make the owner payroll goal scale with a contender's stars, or let the tax bill itself hurt the franchise (revenue, budget) instead of only a goal that cannot be met. That would make dynasty management the central decision.
