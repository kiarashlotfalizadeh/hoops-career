# 02 — The Rebuilder
## Who I am and what I played
- Team and mode: Chicago Stampede, real league, GM + Head Coach, job security on. Chicago was 30th of 30 on the New League screen.
- Device and time: 390x844 phone viewport, headless Chromium, about 4 hours of wall time. The machine was shared and loaded, so big sims often took 1-3 minutes of real time.
- Seasons: 2026-27 to 2030-31 (five regular seasons, 410 games). The fifth was down 0-3 to ATL in round 1 when my time cap hit. I hit the lottery and ran the draft three times, and did three offseasons of re-signs and free agency.
- Live games: none coached. I let the staff's rotation run (with a manual tank rotation in year 1), so this is a roster-building report. It says little about the live game.

## Play log
1. **Oct 2026, first look.** The roster read "30th" but had Giddey 83, Powell 79, Jones 77 and Smith 77, plus prospects Wilson 77/86 and Essengue 75/85. It did not feel like a teardown. The owner said "win about 27 games, give 6,700 minutes to players 23 or under", and gave me two seasons without the playoffs.
2. **Nov 20, 4-10.** I shopped vets on the Block. The AI offered young 70-72 players, but never a first-round pick for any vet. Examples: Powell (79, 33yo, $21.5M) drew "weak offers"; Hield returned nothing. I sold Jones to DET for Holland (72, 21yo) plus two seconds, and Smith to MEM for Clayton (73, 23yo). Selling vets for picks mostly doesn't work.
3. **Counter-offer wording.** Asking BOS for a first for Powell, "What would make this work?" answered "BOS would do it if you add Nic Claxton + 2030 1st". It reads as if I owe them more than they give, and I couldn't tell whose 1st it meant. A confusing counter.
4. **Feb 11 deadline, 16-38.** The staff said "sell Zach Collins, no contender bites". That was honest. I finished 26-56 and went 10-18 after the deadline, because I couldn't sell the 83 PG and the kids were good. Owner security went 50 to 52, and the 25-win goal was met. The game never punished or rewarded a tank. It just didn't let me tank hard.
5. **Lottery 2027.** My 10.5% shot landed the No. 1 pick. The reveal screen is clean, with a "Skip to the order" button and a big "#1 You won the lottery" banner. It's okay, but it isn't an event. There is no ball drawing, tension or sound that I could see.
6. **Draft night, the best thing in the game.** Sixteen teams made offers for the No. 1 pick, and they were real players. MIA offered Dylan Harper, POR offered Clingan plus No. 4, and MEM offered Cameron Boozer (80, 19yo, POT 89) plus Karim Lopez plus No. 28. I took MEM. Boozer became my franchise player: 80 to 82 to 86 to 87 to 88. Each pick also gets a "Fair / Steal / Reach" verdict.
7. **Scouting honesty.** Tarik Akin showed 88 / POT 89-99 in preseason and 81 / POT 87-91 by draft night, so the preseason board was inflated. The Picks tab states the scouting error openly (±2 OVR, ±4 POT). Pape Diakhite was a "staff read 66/76" and became 62/70 on arrival. It's believable, but the 7-point drop on the top prospect is a shock.
8. **Re-sign period 2027.** The page is rich: Bird rights, team options, a decline confirm that explains consequences, and extensions. A smart trick: decline Powell's $22.6M option, then re-sign him at $10.8M. I cleared $25.7M of cap space for free agency.
9. **Free agency 2027, bidding.** I bid on Coulibaly at his ask ($16.75M) and lost to NYK. The inbox then explained exactly why: NYK was rated higher on record and market, so $14.8M from them beat my $16.8M, and winning needed about $16.9M. That is unusually good feedback. The market also moves fast, and most of the good young FAs were gone by day 3.
10. **The turn, 2028-29.** The summer development report is the satisfying moment: Boozer +4 to 86, Essengue +3 to 82, Filipowski +4, Holland +4. The owner set 48 wins and "win a playoff round". We went 20-10 down the stretch to 51-31 and the 3rd seed. The "All 29" moment (beaten every franchise) and the 10-game win streak were fun.
11. **The heartbreak.** We lost a Game 7 to NYK after leading 2-0, with Buzelis out with a broken hand. The owner graded the season -4: wins +20, no playoff round -35. I had made the playoffs and still got a negative grade.
12. **Re-sign trap, 2029.** Essengue and Giddey were both expiring. My script backed out of the second "Tap again to sign him" confirm, and both went to the open market (my mistake, but easy to repeat). I could only afford one. Essengue accepted $39.4M, below his $42.1M ask, "Very interested". Giddey (84) walked. The game was clear that over-the-cap meant a minimum deal or the MLE only.
13. **The rookie-scale cliff, 2030.** Boozer asked $52.1M (was $16.5M) and Wilson $36.6M (was $14.9M). The page showed "Keep everyone: $272.2M, tax about $32.7M". That's a great, honest bill for a successful rebuild. I re-signed Boozer, Wilson and Lopez and landed at $251.6M, $2M under the tax line.
14. **The ceiling.** A core of Boozer 88, Essengue 85, Wilson 84, Holland 85 and Weber 81 (a team that should be a title team) went 48-34 twice and lost round 1 to ATL both times. Wilson (82 OVR) averaged 8.5 points in 24 minutes, and Essengue (82) averaged 10.2. I never became a contender.
15. **Owner patience.** Security hovered 50-54 all five years, and I was never near the "Fired under 25" line. The goals were fair and clearly stated. After season 2 the line "no grace left" appeared, then the owner asked for the playoffs. I was never in danger of being fired, so I never felt the stakes.

## What hooked me (top 5)
1. **Draft night with real offers.** Sixteen offers for the No. 1 pick, including Boozer + Lopez + a first, felt like a real GM moment, and the "Steal / Fair / Reach" cards on every pick made me watch the board.
2. **Free agency that explains itself.** I lost Coulibaly and the inbox told me why in dollars, with the figure needed to win him. Day-3 decisions, rival bidders and the "lead with $X" hint kept me making bids.
3. **A development report I trust.** Growth shows staff priority, +0.4/yr coaching, ceilings, "+3 since last season" and the cost to non-priority guys (about -0.2 each). Boozer's 80/89 to 88/91 path read as earned.
4. **The re-sign cliff.** Boozer's $16.5M to $52.1M ask and the "keep everyone, $272.2M, tax $32.7M" line made success feel like a real cost.
5. **The turn.** Moving from 31 wins to 51, plus streaks and "All 29" moments, gave the rebuild a real arc.

## What pushed me away (top 5)
1. **Major: tanking isn't a thing.** The roster starts with an 83 PG and 77s, so Chicago was "30th" but not bad. I can't sell vets for picks (no first offered for any vet, ever). The staff just says "No contender bites". I went 10-18 after the deadline. There was no real tank/sell lever.
2. **Major: the plateau.** A young core rated 80-88 won 48 games three years running and lost in round 1 every time. The top of the roster is all PFs: Boozer, Essengue, Wilson, Holland, Buzelis, Kuminga, Lopez. Wilson (82) averaged 8.5 ppg. The game never flagged the position glut or why ratings weren't turning into wins.
3. **Major: easy to botch re-signing.** Giddey and Essengue's expiring deals were lost when I backed out of the "tap again to sign" confirm and then opened free agency. The game let me open free agency with two stars unsigned and no warning. It cost me an 84-OVR guard.
4. **Minor: counters are hard to read.** "BOS would do it if you add Claxton + 2030 1st" didn't say whose first. The staff's "Trade for Nikola Jokić" button at the deadline was never explained.
5. **Minor: flat lottery.** "Reveal the order" then "Skip" is the whole flow. The lottery is where a rebuilder's heart is, and it's a text list.

## Bugs (repro steps, screenshot path)
- **Block-offer Propose silently did nothing the first time** (2026-11-20, Jones to DET): tapping Propose with no confirm sheet showed an empty toast, and the trade didn't happen. A second try with "Make the trade" confirmed. MEM's Clayton deal went through the same way, so it's inconsistent. The confirm sheet appears only sometimes.
- **Re-sign "Open free agency" button on /office/resign does nothing.** It stays on Re-sign. Only the Play options entry "Open free agency" works. No screenshot.
- **Rotation swap doesn't carry minutes fully.** I swapped Powell and Wilson, but Powell kept "closer" with 25 minutes and Wilson became a 33-minute starter. Screenshot: players/02-rebuilder/rot3.png. Probably intended, but it's confusing.
- **Play options chevron missing on some screens** (Draft lottery, draft, re-sign, FA, where the Continue bar has one button). A script that calls "Play options" fails. A human would just tap the main button, so it's only a kit issue.
- **Owner page loads blank once** at /office/owner (an empty read right after navigation). It rendered on retry.

## Against the genre (FM / BBGM / MyNBA / Hoop Land)
- **Beats BBGM:** a draft where AI teams offer real players for the pick, rich re-sign and FA psychology (rival bidders, day-3 decisions, "why he picked them"), buyouts and clear owner grades. BBGM's draft is just a list.
- **Beats MyNBA GM:** MyNBA's development is opaque, and this game tells you the +0.4 staff priority and the -0.2 cost to others.
- **Loses to FM:** there is no scouting grind. I can't assign scouts, watch prospects, or follow staff reports. Draft prospects arrive pre-scouted with a ±2 OVR band. There is no regen/hype loop for the kids.
- **Loses to BBGM on tanking:** BBGM lets me gut a roster and trade vets for picks to the AI. Here the AI never pays for vets.
- **Loses to NBA 2K on spectacle:** the lottery has no show.

## Would I pay and keep playing
- $7.99 phone unlock: yes, for the draft and free agency loops alone.
- $14.99 on Steam: maybe. I'd want a harder, more active tank/trade market and a visible reason why a stacked young roster stalls.
- Hours: about 30 hours before the loop repeats (5 seasons took about 4 real hours, mostly sim waiting).
- Recommend to a friend: 7.5/10.

## The one change that would most raise my score
Make selling veterans and tanking real. Let AI teams pay first-round picks for useful 28+ players at the deadline, let a roster-wide "develop, don't win" rotation setting exist, and show a position/usage warning when a young core is all one position. The rebuild loop is already great at the draft and in FA. It only needs a real sell side and a payoff that matches the ratings.
