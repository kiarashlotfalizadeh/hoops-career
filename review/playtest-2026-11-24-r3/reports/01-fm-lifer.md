# 01 — The FM Lifer
## Who I am and what I played
Dallas Wranglers (real rosters, West, 14th of 30 by roster rank), GM + Head Coach, one phone-size browser (390x844). Two full seasons: 2026-27 went 33-49 (0-9 start, 2-15 at Nov 23, 31-34 after), 11th in the West. 2027-28 went 41-41, then 2-0 in the play-in (113-101 vs MEM, 123-122 @POR), then lost round 1 to 66-16 San Antonio, 1-4 (game 5 in OT without Flagg). I coached 10 games live (HOU, IND, SAS, HOU, BKN, IND, MIL, both play-in games, playoff game 1). Games 1 to 5 were full, later ones were Q1-Q2 live plus "Clutch time" skip. I set per-opponent plans before roughly 14 games. Time spent: a long session (about 5 hours wall clock), of which a lot was waiting on live games, since 2x/4x playback is slow.

## Play log
1. **Oct 20, preseason.** Today shows a 4-step checklist and a forced "release 2 players" call. The staff's cut list tells me the dead money ($19.4M for Caleb Martin) before I tap, and the button asks for a second tap to confirm. Good.
2. **Tactics, hour one.** Change > Offense list ranks the styles by fit for my roster ("Similar fit / Slightly worse / Worse fit"). Tapping a style applies it as a draft with a before-to-after grade diff (Five-Out Motion: ball movement F to A, ball security D to C). Grades are 14 tiles. I felt like I was in FM's tactics screen. I took Five-Out, set Irving to Primary Creator, Flagg to Slasher (role star fit 2 to 4 of 5). The role sheet has per-role star fits for every player, in and out of possession.
3. **Advanced drawer.** PnR/iso/post/handoff frequencies, drive rate, crash glass, transition defense, press, switch off-ball screens, go-to player, foul/late-game rules, auto timeouts. Deep. I set crash glass to "Rarely" to hold back for transition. That was a mistake the game later told me about with evidence.
4. **Opener @HOU, lost 94-123.** HOU shot 18-34 from three. Break report: "Houston's shots fell above their looks: 72.7% eFG on looks worth 52.7%, HOU +12.7 pts, luck." That line is the best idea in the game. Staff also told me at the first break "They have 5 more threes."
5. **Games 2-3 (IND, SAS) lost 110-120, 108-116.** Break cards ("Dallas passed up second chances by staying off the offensive glass", "Stop trapping", "2-3 Zone, Not now") nudged me. Post-game report for the 105-108 HOU loss: "Offensive rebounds DAL 5, HOU 15, -10.5 pts. Next time: Win the glass, crash harder." I flipped crash to "Crash often". On Nov 22 the staff mailed: "Your change seems to have helped: +1.6 a game, give or take 0.4 over 10 games." This loop (change, forecast, check in 10 games, verdict) is FM's feedback loop done honestly.
6. **0-9, then 2-15.** Lively out 23 games (broken hand), Flagg out 9, then 6. I never found a lever that fixed it. The Today card said "The slide: 8 straight losses. Outscored by 13.1 a game. The box scores point to turnovers." Owner mails escalated (left a game at halftime). Staff call on Nov 23: "tell the owner it's a rebuild" (costs 10 standing points for good, the card says so). I confirmed it.
7. **Nov 23, Lively extension.** Rookie deal expiring, he asks $43.7M x5, "Interest: Not keen" (because we were losing). Signed at his ask; clean two-tap flow.
8. **Feb 5, deadline.** Staff call "Stand pat", plus a buy-out offer "Trade for Joel Embiid" (Irving + Washington + three 1sts). The card said the forecast barely moves postseason odds. I stood pat. Good call by the tool.
9. **Offseason.** Draft (no pick), re-sign page with team options and the staff's recommendation per option, 30-day free agency. Each FA row says how he fixes a weakness ("Three-point D D to C at 31 MPG"). Signed DiVincenzo with the mid-level (he'll be 31, game warns "most often down" for 3 years). Flagg went 83 to 88 (POT 94).
10. **Oct 2027, 0-4 again.** Season Analysis showed Three-point D = D. I previewed the four other defensive schemes by grade diff and moved to Switch Everything (3pt D D to C, perimeter C to B+, rim A to C+, transition C to F). We went 20-9 after the deadline from 21-32.
11. **Play-in, Apr 7 and 9.** Two live wins. I accepted "2-3 Zone" and "Pressure the ball" advice at breaks. 99% win chance flashed on the last frame of a 123-122 game.
12. **Round 1 vs SAS (66-16).** Series plan (counters, double Wembanyama, plan lasts "This series"). Game 1 won 108-95 with +16.5 pts of shooting luck, flagged honestly. Lost 4 of the next 4.

## What hooked me (top 5)
1. **The luck line.** After every quarter: "Dallas' shots fell above their looks: made 88.6% eFG on looks worth 52.5%, DAL +13.4 pts, luck. That part is luck, with nothing to change." Post-game: "looks -2, shooting luck +7, free throws -8 = -3." It stops me from overreacting to variance. FM has no equivalent this clear.
2. **Draft preview with grade diffs.** Flipping defensive schemes shows "Rim protection A to C+, Perimeter D C to B+, Transition D C to F" before I commit. That's the "I understand the trade-off" moment.
3. **"Your moves" counterfactual.** Game report replays the night 200 times with and without each timeout/play call: "3 timeouts -0.9 pts ±1.1, 3 play calls 0.0, 1 tactical change +0.7, inside the noise. The staff's bench would have done about as well." Brutally honest.
4. **The check-in mail.** "Your change seems to have helped: +1.6 a game, give or take 0.4 over 10 games" / "Still too close to call over 21 games. That was the staff's last look." My assistant manager actually behaves like one.
5. **Per-opponent plan depth.** Who-guards-whom with mismatch notes ("Ball handling 94 vs perimeter defense 58", "gives up 3 inches"), double-team a star, target a defender (staff star-pick), hack-a-player, junk defense, counters labelled "Should help / No clear edge" with a reason ("Their ball handlers are loose with it, security 50").

## What pushed me away (top 5)
1. **Major: Live games are slow and brittle.** 4x playback takes about 10-13 min for one game; breaks are good but I wanted a ~5 min cap. A timeout screen leaves the game paused until I pick a play and press Resume; I got stuck there twice with no obvious "back" (needed play + Resume).
2. **Major: Plans do not show their value before the game.** Pre-game win % never moved when I added a counter, a double-team and a matchup (75% before and after the HOU plan; 51% to 51% for SAC). Staff said it can't measure it until games, and the post-game "Your plan" box only says "Tonight: DAL offense 1.04 a trip... coming in 1.04". Per the brief: do game plans visibly matter? I could not see it in a week, a month, or 10 live games.
3. **Major: Two ugly starts that the preview didn't predict.** 0-9 (pre-game odds 50-75% to lose in most; one at 59% to win) and 0-4 in 2027 with a healthy 88-rated Flagg and 87 Lively. In 2027 I was 21-32 at Feb 5, then 20-9. Either bad luck twice or the odds are generous to me. I can't tell which; the luck lines say "shots fell below looks" in many losses, but ORtg 26th-ish says it's me.
4. **Minor/Major: "Complexity that's fake".** Mentality (very cautious to very aggressive) and some Advanced toggles never showed any evidence on any report. "Tactics +3.9 pts" and "Your moves +0.7" are inside the noise. Shape (4-Out/Horns/5-Out) is described but I never saw its effect separately.
5. **Minor: sheets and queue.** The "While you were away" sheet and the post-sim digest blocked the Play button on a phone until dismissed; the big sims stop for staff calls, which is right, but "Sim to end of regular season" stopped me and a call took two taps plus Confirm each time.

## Bugs (repro steps, screenshot path)
- **Timeout pause lock (live).** In a live game, tap Timeout, don't pick a play, press Escape: game stays paused; resume only works after picking a play then tapping Resume. Screenshot: players/01-fm-lifer/g2-to1.png.
- **Game stays on "Final" until I leave /live.** After the IND home game (Nov 9), the Games tab still showed it as "Today" while the live view said "Final". Minor.
- **Staff brief shows odd scouting numbers.** IND game 2 brief: "Haliburton 91, 5.0 PTS, 27.2% TS, 19% USG, on/off -40.0". Small sample from last year's injured season; reads weird next to "Best shot creator".
- **Counters flicker.** "Staff still looking..." re-appears every time a counter is added (the list recomputes), so a second tap can add a different counter than the one I meant (I added "Stay home on shooters" by accident).
- No page errors collected (page.errors empty all session).

## Against the genre (FM / BBGM / MyNBA / Hoop Land)
- **vs FM:** Where it's deeper than FM: the luck/looks split, counterfactual replays of my own calls, quarter-by-quarter "What's deciding it". FM gives xG and heat maps; this tells me why I lost and how much was luck. Shallower: no set-piece/training/individual-development panels, no scouting network, no real media/dressing-room layer (it is intentionally signals-only). Fake complexity: Mentality and a few Advanced toggles that never show an effect.
- **vs BBGM:** More coaching, less brute-force trading. BBGM's trade AI is simpler to farm; here AI wants real value ("no clear edge" on the Embiid deal) and the CBA is lighter but present.
- **vs MyNBA/MyGM:** Far more honest and less cinematic. No arcade skill layer.
- **vs Hoop Land / Draft Day:** Tactics layer is much richer.

## Would I pay and keep playing
$7.99 phone unlock: yes. $14.99 on Steam: yes, if live games are quicker and the plan impact becomes legible. Hours I'd put in: 40-60 (a few seasons, mostly for coaching experiments). Recommend to a friend: 8 (for FM people), 6 for casual fans.

## The one change that would most raise my score
Make a game plan's effect legible before and after the game: show how the staff's counter moved the matchup, the possessions it changed, and a running "plans have been worth +X over N games" in Season Analysis, the way it already does for tactical changes and timeouts. Right now plans are the deepest-looking surface and the one I can least feel.
