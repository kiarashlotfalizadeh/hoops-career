# 09 — The Crossover Newcomer (19, MyNBA every year, a bit of BBGM, never played FM)

## Who I am and what I played
- Career 1: real rosters, Washington Mages (the preselected default), GM + Head Coach, Teaching ON. One full regular season (34-48, missed the play-in), the lottery (jumped from 9th to No. 2), the draft, and the start of the re-sign period. I did not play the playoffs, because we missed them.
- Career 2: fictional league, Washington again (default), same role, Teaching ON. 25 games (8-16).
- Live games: 1 mostly watched to the end (vs MIL, 136-124 win) and 1 in the fictional career (a timeout called, then abandoned).
- Device: 390x844 phone, headless. 3D court asked "3D is running slowly, use 2D". I did not judge the 3D look.
- About 1.5 hours of real time, which is about 2 in-game seasons of attention. I would have been out around minute 25 without a hook (see below).

## Play log (first hour, in steps)
1. `/start`. One screen: "GM and head coach in one, with a tactics layer that tells you why", Teams 30 / Games 82 / Season 2026-27, New league / Import backup. Clear in 3 seconds. Liked it. Step 1 of 3 asks real or fictional. The "544 real players" subtitle is a good hook.
2. Team pick. The card shows the owner's plan ("26 wins · rebuild · builder"), the payroll and the top 3 players, and the roster rank ("21st of 30"). Good. Washington is preselected. I tapped "Take over Washington" and never questioned it. A newcomer takes the default, and the default is a rebuild with a $191.5M payroll "$26.5M over the cap". I did not understand "over the cap" yet. The owner text changed between the card ("26 wins") and the next step ("27 wins"). Minor, but I noticed.
3. Step 3: role plus Teaching toggle. The toggle copy ("The first-week guide on Today, its mail and Today's Glossary link") is understandable. Career created in 0.2 s. I was on Today within a minute of opening the page. Very good (BBGM is the only thing that matches this).
4. Today: a big navy card with "Preseason", the owner's three goals in plain language ("Win about 27 games / Give about 10,000 minutes to players 23 or under / Stay under the luxury tax") and a 4-step checklist: Set your rotation, Pick a playbook, Plan for your opener, Coach a game live. This is the best new-player screen I've seen in a sports sim. I followed it in order.
5. Rotation: "Keep these five" button. I tapped it. The table is dense for me: columns "MIN / STA / Target / OVR" with "—" under MIN before any game. I did not know what STA was. The stint-plan bars at the bottom looked cool and meant nothing to me.
6. Playbook: "Balanced Attack · Drop & Protect". The "Change" sheet lists "Pace & Space, Heliocentric, Grit & Grind" with "Best fit first / Similar fit / Slightly worse". That is a 2K-style choice I understand. "Drop", "Hedge", "Ice", "Blitz" meant nothing to me. I kept the staff's pick. Grades "A+ Second chances / F Three-point D / F Spacing" gave me a first reason to care about my roster.
7. "Plan for your opener": three of four counters said "Staff still looking…". I tapped "No plan this time" and it ticked. Fine, but a newcomer on game one has nothing to plan with.
8. Start regular season; the opener, Game 1 vs MIL, "56% WAS to win". I tapped "Watch & coach" then "Tip off". Pre-game page: starting fives, "Their threats" (Porter Jr., Herro, Turner with plain-English reasons: "Best shooter, three-point 81. Don't leave him open."). I liked this a lot. It is what 2K never tells me.
9. The live game. After a short intro the 3D court appeared with a 4-step coachmark tour ("Timeout stops play. Players has subs, Now has staff calls."). The assistant coach "Mark Abernathy" speaks in a card ("Early. Watching how they want to play." "We're winning the glass by 5."). 1x/2x/4x and a Skip ahead menu ("End of Q2 / Clutch time / Sim to final"). I used it. First real hook: Sarr hit a three, "Bang! Sarr buries the three." The end-of-quarter break shows a shot chart and the box. I did not make any call in this game, and the checklist step "Coach a game live" still said "(next)" after the game ended: it only ticks when you make a call. I wondered if the game was broken. A 19-year-old would have decided the guide was buggy.
10. Result: W 136-124. The post-game card shows "Tactics −0.2 pts / Shooting luck +24.4 pts" and "about a 1-in-875 shooting night". Honest, and I'd never see that in 2K, but at this point I did not know what "luck +24.4" meant. It was explained in text below. I understood after reading, which is homework-ish.
11. Weeks 1-5: simmed 1 week at a time. "While you were away" sheet with "The moment of the stretch", "The staff's calls: Test-simmed Ice in place of Drop over the next 6 games... Keeping Drop, nothing changed", then results. I loved the "moment" headline (George rallied from 15 down at CHI, Nov 20). The digest pops up as a bottom sheet after every sim, which is good for me.
12. Nov 28, 9-10: first free agent. Staff says "Shot creation F" for my roster, 98 players available. I signed Pat Spencer (69, min deal). The offer sheet has a slider, years, "Player option / Team option", "Cap impact", and a "Confirm: sign him · 3 yrs · $7.6M" button that says "a signing can't be undone. Tap again to sign him." Good. Lots of words I didn't get ("min deal", "mid-level", "Lukewarm/Neutral" interest).
13. Dec 9, Sarr out 54 games (stress fracture). Sim stopped with a card: "The staff's call: ride it out." "Why" explains it. "Look for a trade" jumped to the trade finder pre-filtered to Cs 77+. This felt like a real GM moment. I tried to buy Victor Wembanyama (97). The game said "Not even close" and "What would make this work?" explained it: SAS rates him "about 63 first-round picks like your best one". That is funny and clear, and it is exactly the answer to my 2K instinct to trade for the best player in the league.
14. Feb 3, 20-31. A BOS trade offer (Champagnie for Cenac Jr. + a pick, "Stands until Feb 8", the GM quote "We're 37-13, 1st in the East..."). I accepted. A deadline "staff's call: sell to LA Lagoons" turned out to be Ayton (76) for Wiggins (75), which is a sideways swap, not a sale. I said Stand pat.
15. 34-48 at the end, which beat the owner's 26-win goal. The season ended at "Your season is over at 34-48" with the Continue bar saying "Sim to end of playoffs". The digest said I was 11th in the East "well above the 14th they had you". Nice.
16. The lottery: "Reveal the order" ran, and "You jump to No. 2 — Up 7 places from 9th". That was the best moment of the whole run, with real tension and no spoilers. GSW jumped from 6th to No. 1. At the draft I took "the staff's pick" (Dillon Cox, 75/84) and then "Auto-draft the rest".
17. Re-sign period. This is where I hit the wall. Words: "Committed $205.9M, Keep everyone $234.4M, tax ≈ $43.6M", "Bird rights", "Team options · 4 / Rookie scale · staff: decline", "No Bird rights: up to $9.7M", "Extend", "Sarr asks $34.4M x 5". Coulibaly (22, 76/82) asked $16.95M x 5 and the page said it "crosses the tax line" (+$15.5M tax). The owner told me on day one to stay under the tax. I did not know whether to sign. I closed the app in my head here. This is my "nearly quit": 40 minutes of fun, then a page that reads like an accountant's.
18. Career 2 (fictional): created in 0.3 s again. The same checklist. Washington's best is "Trae Lloyd 82" and "Kyrie Bradley 81": invented names, close to real ones, which is amusing but gives me no one to care about. 1-3, 2-6, 5-7, then a 6-game losing streak ("The slide · 6 straight losses. Outscored by 20.0 a game. The box scores point to turnovers."), 8-16 at 25 games. I got the same trade offer cards ("PHX want Marshall for Alexander + Exum"). No hook.

## What hooked me (top 5)
1. The Today screen with the owner's 3 plain-language goals and the 4-step guide (minutes 3-6). It told me what to do and why.
2. The draft lottery reveal: "You jump to No. 2 — Up 7 places from 9th" and a real pick order with odds per team ("9.0%", "4.5%"). Pure drama.
3. "While you were away" digests with a headline moment ("Washington rallies from 15 down to beat Chicago", George 17 pts) and the staff's tested calls.
4. Honest answers instead of a no: "Not even close... about 63 first-round picks like your best one" for Wembanyama, and "about a 1-in-875 shooting night" on a lucky win.
5. Staff narration in live games ("We're winning the glass by 5", "Their spot-up plays aren't working: 3 points on 11 trips") with one-tap fixes ("Davis in" for tired Sarr).

## What pushed me away (top 5)
1. **Major**: Re-sign and cap pages (minute ~45). "Committed / Keep everyone / tax ≈ $43.6M / Bird rights / Rookie scale / Team options / No Bird rights: up to $9.7M" with no plain-English line. The owner's tax goal collided with re-signing Coulibaly and I did not know which to pick. I left without signing him.
2. **Major**: Fictional league has no faces I know. "Trae Lloyd 82" is a wink, not a star. Together with an 8-16 start and a roster ranked 23rd, the first 25 games had no emotional anchor. In the real league I at least had Davis, Young and Dybantsa as "my guys".
3. **Major**: Washington is preselected and is a rebuild with a tax-level payroll. I took the default and had a 20-31 season by Feb. A newcomer needs a "Good first team" tag or a "Contender / Rebuild / Starter-friendly" filter on the team picker.
4. **Minor**: "Coach a game live" stays unticked after you coach a game with no calls. It only ticks when you make a call, and the page only says "ticks off when you make the call, not when you look" in an inbox mail I did not read. Combine that with live games running at real time (12 game-seconds per 6 real seconds at 2x) and I wondered why I was watching.
5. **Minor**: Numbers I did not understand: STA (stamina), the "—" in MIN before a game, "Payroll $191.5M" vs "$176.5M" shown on the Office card, "Tactics −0.2 pts / Shooting luck +24.4 pts" on a 12-point win, "Drop / Hedge / Ice / Blitz", "Lukewarm", "PO / TO" after a contract year, "Drawn pick". Each has a "?" but I did not look.

## Bugs (repro steps, screenshot path)
- Live-game resume: leaving a live game and returning shows the pregame page with "Resume the game" and, after resume, the game is sometimes at a different point (Q1 11:49 earlier vs "End of Q1" later) with a staff call already waiting. No data loss. Screenshots: `.../players/09-newcomer/k0.png`, `k2-tactics.png`.
- "Use 2D" / "3D is running slowly" banner covers the court on every resume. Clicking it via the normal tap often failed to take effect in my headless run (probably headless timing). `h3.png`, `i0.png`.
- "Coach a game live" does not tick after a live game with no manual call (see log 9). `m1.png`.
- "Sell to LAL" deadline card is Ayton 76 for Wiggins 75 and the game says "No clear edge". Not a sell. Screenshot `x1.png`.
- The owner's goal says "26 wins" in the mail, "about 27" on Today, and "26 wins" on the team card. Fine, but it looks sloppy.
- The Continue bar overlays content, and after a big sim the digest sheet must be dismissed manually; scripts that tap Play options fail until it is. For a player this is one extra tap per sim.
- Open free agency (Re-sign page) did not change the phase when I clicked it once (status stayed "Re-signing · 2027"); I did not retry.

## Against the genre
- vs 2K MyNBA: I lose the flash (no cutscenes, no cinematic presser, trading is a table, not a glittery card). I gain a game that explains itself: "why did we lose" in plain text, owner stakes, and no dice-roll feeling in trades. 2K lets me trade for anyone; here it says "63 first-round picks" and I believe it.
- vs BBGM: BBGM is cleaner and faster to read. This one has more depth and more story (moments, owner mails, lottery reveal), but also more words. BBGM never put "Bird rights" in my face on a screen with a buy button.
- vs FM: I never touched the full tactics depth, so I can't compare. The "staff makes tactics calls" toggle is a lifesaver for someone like me.
- Where it is harder to learn: contracts, the cap and the tax; matchups ("Your defender" lists); words like "drop".
- Where it is more rewarding: stakes (owner, job security 54/100 on the Office page), real stats, and the honesty about luck.

## Would I pay and keep playing
- $7.99 phone unlock: yes if the first season has a hook. I'd pay for the real league only. For a fictional league alone I'd think twice.
- $14.99 on Steam: no, I'm a phone-and-couch player.
- Hours: about 6-10 in the first week (a season or two), then it depends on whether year two is a story or a spreadsheet.
- Recommend to a friend: 7/10 for a friend who likes BBGM, 5/10 for a friend who only plays 2K.

## The one change that would most raise my score
Make the first offseason as guided as the first week: when the re-sign page opens, show one line per player in plain words ("Keep him: $16.9M a year puts you $15.5M over the tax. The owner said no tax. Staff says: sign him, then trade a veteran") and a single "Do what the staff suggests" button. A newcomer quits on the contracts page; the rest of the game is already good enough to keep them.
