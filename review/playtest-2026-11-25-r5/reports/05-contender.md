# 05 — The Win-Now Contender, 24
## Who I am and what I played
- Oklahoma City Twisters, the league's best roster (ranked 1st of 30 on the team picker). Real 2026 league, GM + Head Coach, job security on. Phone viewport 390x844, 2D court.
- Played 3 full seasons plus 2 offseasons (the brief asked for 4; I ran out of budget, not interest). Results: S1 54-28, 3rd seed, **champions (4-0 Finals)**. S2 64-18, 1st seed, MVP + DPOY + Coach of the Year, **lost Round 2 Game 7 at home**. S3 53-29, 5th seed, lost Round 1, owner grade -51.
- Live games: about 35 (a few full, most as halftime read, then skip to clutch time). I picked the plan, subs and last-possession plays in the close ones. Roughly 4-5 hours of play.

## Play log
1. **Oct 20 2026, opener at SAS.** Today screen: 55% to win, owner wants 63 wins and the Finals, "forecast 71 wins". I coached it live. Halftime was 70-60 with a "What's deciding it" card ("shots fell above their looks: +6.5 luck"). Won 137-124. Good first hit.
2. **Oct 21 to Nov 20: 7-8.** SGA (calf), Wallace (hand), Mitchell and Holmgren all missed games. OKC had the league's best defense (1st DRtg) and the 30th-ranked offense (104.5 ORtg), and the owner's mood went to "falling". The game explains it honestly ("Don't panic. Yet. Look at the margins"). It felt pushed back on, not rigged.
3. **Nov 20 live vs NOP, down 17 at half, lost 120-123 in a last-possession finish.** I took the staff's 2-3 zone prompt and the "Iso: Mitchell" last-possession call. It came up empty. The decision was mine and the loss was clear. That screen is the best thing in the game.
4. **Dec 21 offers.** MEM offered Spencer (80, shooter) for Mitchell (81). The staff said "even", spacing F to B. MIA offered Adebayo for Hartenstein + Caruso + 2 picks and the staff showed "+$20.8M tax". I declined it.
5. **Feb 6 deadline, 31-22.** The staff's "Trade for Curry" and "Trade for KAT" cards both cost me Jalen Williams (86) and Caruso. They are bad ideas dressed up as "no clear edge". I stood pat and went 22-5 after the deadline to finish 54-28.
6. **Moreau chase, Feb S2.** SAC's Ousmane Moreau (90/98, $13.7M, "asked for a trade"). The trade screen said they value him at "about 31 first-round picks like your best one" and my whole pile of firsts at 3.9. That is a clear answer, and it kills the idea of buying a young superstar.
7. **Playoffs S1.** R1 beat GSW 4-2 (G4 lost in OT 134-141 after a last-possession Iso; series 2-2, then won G5 104-88 and G6 102-85). R2 swept MEM. **WCF vs LAL (Dončić 95): down 2-3 with Jalen Williams, Mitchell, Topić and Jaylin out.** I set "Double-team Dončić, this series". Won G6 117-103 and G7 126-102 at home. Finals 4-0 over DET.
8. **The title.** Delivered as inbox mail ("Your first title", "Champions, 4-0", "No. 0 to the rafters", a history page with "Your title"). I did not see a full-screen celebration. My scripts clear overlays, so I may have swept one away (see Bugs).
9. **Offseason 1, the tax bites.** Committed 27-28 payroll was $244.7M against a $213.1M tax line, so the tax bill was $80M, or $108M if I kept everyone. Wallace (77/82) wanted $14.8M and the market wanted more for Cam Johnson, who chose HOU at $14.4M over my $13.1M (the game told me exactly why). I let Wallace walk and declined McCain's option.
10. **Season 2, 42-12 at the deadline.** "All 29: Contender completes the set" (beat every franchise) was a lovely story beat. I finished 64-18 and was the 1st seed.
11. **S2 R2 vs DEN, Game 7 at home.** SGA hurt in Q1 ("Skip stopped: Gilgeous-Alexander is hurt and out for the game"). Lost 116-125. Season: +20 grade, "lost in the second round".
12. **Offseason 2: the real fork.** Re-sign Mitchell at $19.3M added **+$77.6M tax** on top of $53M (about 4 dollars of tax per dollar over the line). "Keep everyone" showed tax ≈ $393M. I re-signed Mitchell and let Topić (81/86, 22) and Jaylin Williams walk. Painful and credible.
13. **S3.** SGA out 32 games on day 2 ("Ride it out / Look for a trade"). 53-29, 5th seed, lost R1. Owner: grade -51 ("A second poor one and you are out"), job security 44, warm seat. LAL (Dončić) won the title.

## What hooked me (top 5)
1. **The last-possession screen.** "Down 2, 19.7 left. Iso: Mitchell / Spain: Holmgren / Timeout", with the staff noting who the best closer is. G4 vs GSW went to OT and my call mattered.
2. **Honest "why" everywhere.** Halftime "What's deciding it", "shooting luck +8.9 pts", "Hartenstein 4 fouls, Tilly in", a trade deadline card that says "no clear edge".
3. **The tax is a real dynasty tax.** $244.7M against a $213.1M line meant $80M in tax; one $19.3M re-sign added $77.6M; "keep everyone" showed $393M. I really did lose Topić for it.
4. **Free-agency feedback.** "Cameron Johnson chose Houston over us. Their offer: 3 yrs at $14.4M. Ours: $13.1M." That is the whole lesson in one message.
5. **Owner and press as characters.** The napkin lineup side-bet ("He noticed. It counts a little"), Coach of the Month, "All 29: completes the set", a jersey retired on the home opener.

## What pushed me away (top 5)
1. **Injuries decided too much (major).** SGA missed 14 + 8 + 6 + 5 games in S1, was hurt in Game 7 of R2 in S2 and missed 32 games in S3. The star-dependence feels punishing; losing a Game 7 to an in-game star injury felt like the sim, not me.
2. **"Skip ahead to clutch time" can eat the game (major).** A "decided game goes on to the final", but a 10-point halftime lead became a final before I could act. Real clutch coaching only happens in games that stay within 5 minutes of the end.
3. **Title celebration is thin (major for this persona).** Series wins and the championship came as inbox mail and a history row. I saw no parade, no trophy moment and no "dynasty" framing. After a 4-0 Finals sweep the next screen was the draft lottery.
4. **Rubber-banding smell (minor to major).** Several leads of 15-20 at the half ended within 5 (NOP lead 17 at half then OKC lost close; DEN 63-45 at half → 111-106). I can't prove it, but it felt like the game wanted every game close.
5. **Staff deadline cards are bad deals (minor).** "Trade for Curry / KAT" always costs Jalen Williams, the best young piece, for an older star with "no clear edge". The card nudges a bad move.

## Bugs (repro steps, screenshot path)
- **Live game keeps running while the screen loads.** Re-entering "Resume live game" from Today starts playback; in my script the lead flipped 108-114 to a loss before I could pause. Repro: start a live game, tap Leave, tap Resume live game. Screenshot: shots/e2.png in my folder.
- **"Skip stopped" leaves the game mid-quarter.** After an injury stop (WCF G1 and R2 G7), the next Skip option sometimes did nothing and the Today bar said "Resume live game Q3". Needed "Finish this game".
- **Two-step confirms are easy to miss.** Trade "Accept" shows "Confirm: Accept" (my first Accept did nothing, then I turned the offer down by accident). Re-sign "Make offer" needs "Confirm: sign him" (shots/rs2.png).
- **Play options sheet becomes unclickable** when the "You can close it out tonight - Coach it live / Not yet / Sim it" sheet appears on game day. It blocks the Continue bar.
- **Free-agent "Negotiate" sheet** lists a Mid-level button for a player whose ask is already over the exception, until you move the slider.
- Page errors: none seen in `page.errors`.

## Against the genre (FM / BBGM / MyNBA / Hoop Land)
- **vs Football Manager:** the staff voice (Isaiah Whitfield), matchups, counters and the opponent "plays a 2-3 zone in stretches" scouting is FM-adjacent and better than anything in the basketball genre. FM has deeper long-run player development and morale. Winning here is more binary.
- **vs Basketball GM:** BBGM has faster sims and better stats pages. This game wins on the trade "what would make this work?" explanation, the owner, and the tax math. It loses on speed: a live playoff game costs me about 90 seconds, which hurts a 4-round title run.
- **vs NBA 2K MyGM:** more honest, less spectacle, and no celebration. 2K would give me a parade and I want one.
- **vs Hoop Land / Pro Basketball Manager:** the possession-level "why" cards and last-possession screen are ahead of both.

## Would I pay and keep playing
- **$7.99 phone unlock:** yes, today. **$14.99 on Steam:** yes if there is a 3D court that isn't slow and a proper title ceremony; otherwise borderline yes.
- **Hours:** about 40 for a first dynasty run, then I'd restart to try the "let a star walk and rebuild around Moreau" route.

## The one change that would most raise my score
Make the playoffs and the title a **full-screen ceremony with a dynasty frame**: series-clinch moment, Finals MVP, banner raised, "year N of the window", then the cap fork. Right now my first championship, earned by coming back from 2-3 down with four players out, arrived as an inbox line. Pair that with "skip to clutch" never auto-finishing a game inside 15 points, and winning would feel like winning.
