# 09 — The Crossover Newcomer
## Who I am and what I played
19, plays 2K MyNBA yearly, opened Basketball GM once, no Football Manager. All defaults: Real 2026-27 league, the default team (Washington Mages, the card shown first), GM + Head Coach, Teaching on, injuries and job security on. Phone 390x844.
Played 2026-27 in full (28-54, #3 pick), the draft, re-sign, free agency, then 2027-28 in full (47-35, 5th seed, won round 1 4-2, swept by Atlanta in round 2). Then a fictional-league career as Washington, 17 games (10-7). Live games coached: two real ones, the opener (127-119 OT win vs MIL, I played about a quarter, then Sim to final) and playoff Game 1 vs CHA (lost 99-101, with tactic change and a timeout play call). Plus about a quarter of the opener at 2x. Rough time: about 2.5 hours of tapping.

## Play log
1. Oct 20, 2026, creation: 3 steps, took 5.7 s. The team card with its "21st of 30" roster rank is clear. But step 2 says the owner is "rebuild · win-now" and step 3 says "rebuild · frugal". I could not tell what those meant, or why they changed.
2. Today screen at preseason: owner goals shown plainly (win about 28, 10,000 minutes to players 23 or under, stay under the tax). The first-games checklist is a good hook. I did "Keep these five" and "Keep this playbook", but the checklist still said "0 of 4 done" afterwards. It also stayed at 0 of 4 on game day. I could not tell what ticks a step.
3. Opener, live: the first open of the live screen stacks a coachmark ("1/4 Score, clock, fouls...") on top of a "plays sideways, rotate your phone" banner on top of a tip-off card. I had to dismiss three overlays before I saw a possession. Once clear, it is excellent. The scorebug, run tags ("MIL 8-0 run"), hot-hand notes, the end-of-Q1 report and "What's deciding it" (free throws, looks vs luck) all teach me something. It stalls at end of quarter until you tap "Start Q2". Closing the browser mid-game keeps it as "Resume live game".
4. Post-game: 127-119 OT win. "How the margin adds up: looks -2.9 · shooting luck -3.1 · free throws +14 = +8" is the best thing in the game for me. I understood why I won, and it did not make me feel dumb.
5. Nov 21, 8-9: two trade offers, "Why" button says "Staff: fair value". I turned both down. Clean modal, but I had no idea if Walsh 71 for Champagnie 75 was good beyond the staff's word.
6. Jan 31, 22-28: Trae Young out 21 games. The sim stopped with the staff's call "ride it out / look for a trade". I rode it out. The team went 6-26 from there and finished 28-54. I never understood whether anything I did could have stopped the slide.
7. Free agency day 1 error: I signed Miles McBride (77 OVR, $12.6M x4, mid-level) while over the tax. The sheet showed "27-28 tax +$20.5M" as one small grey line. My owner goal was "get under the tax". There is no big warning for a $20M tax hit. I then traded Carrington and Riley to ATL for nothing to cut $11.1M, which still left $1.6M over and a $2.4M bill. I felt dumb, which is the exact thing this persona was testing for.
8. Re-sign: Coulibaly asked $19.2M x5 (was $9.2M). I signed him. I declined Tre Mann's $8M team option, and his market ask was $2.9M. That felt clever. The "Open free agency?" confirm listing who walks is excellent. Ayton walked because I lacked Bird rights, and "Bird rights" had no explanation in context.
9. Draft: lottery order revealed, WAS jumped 6th to 3rd. Board shows "56% gone by #3" odds per prospect. I took Dalen Ferguson (77, POT 85-93 scouted). In the summer he came out 71 / POT 79. A bust, and a convincing one. The Growth tab said "Rookie watch: yet to play".
10. 2027-28: owner jumped to "win about 41, make the playoffs". Fair. Summer development: Sarr +5 (86), Dybantsa 84. The Growth page is a real strength. Team went 47-35 after an 8-4 start and a late 11-15 slump.
11. Playoffs R1 G1 live at CHA: "Plan the series" has a counter suggestion with evidence ("Charlotte attacked drop with pull-up jumpers, +5.3 pts a game for them in 8 meetings"). I added Hedge coverage. In-game "Changed tonight: Mentality Balanced -> Aggressive" confirms what I did. In the timeout the "Call the play: Iso: George / Elevator: McBride" menu was fun. "Ran it: came up empty. WAS 0-3 since." Lost 99-101.
12. Fictional league: the same WAS is a 61-win Finals-or-bust roster. Job security was 44 "warm seat" after 17 games at 10-7. A newcomer gets thrown into a high-pressure situation, with no cue that the real league is the easier start.

## What hooked me
1. The post-game report ("How the margin adds up", Four factors, "What decided it"). It made basketball feel solved and learnable. Evidence: the opener.
2. The first-games checklist plus owner goals on the first screen. I always knew what to do next.
3. The re-sign and free agency flow. The "Open free agency?" confirm, Tre Mann's cheaper market ask, and mid-level labels worked for me.
4. Draft lottery and board. "56% gone by #3", progress tracker, and a believable bust.
5. Owner and job security that bite (warm seat at game 17 on the fictional save).

## What pushed me away
1. Luxury tax warning is too quiet. Major. A $20.5M tax bill sat in one grey line when I signed McBride, against an owner goal of staying under the tax.
2. Jargon without in-context explanation (Bird rights, mid-level, team option, rookie scale, OVR/POT, eFG, ORtg/DRtg). Major. The "?" icons exist on some, but Re-sign and trades show raw terms.
3. First-games checklist stays at "0 of 4 done" after Keep rotation, Keep playbook and No plan. Minor/major. I never learned what counts as done.
4. Live-game overlay stack at tip-off (coachmark + sideways banner + tip card). Minor.
5. "Staff still looking..." sits on every Counter row even when the expanded row has an actual suggestion. Minor. It reads like a loading state that never finishes.

## Bugs
- Play options sheet stays open after picking a call like "Ride it out" (sim flow was blocked by the open sheet until I closed it manually). Screens: none saved.
- Checklist "0 of 4 done" after all choices.
- Red "Widen the slider past $X" line appears under the offer slider even when the offer is fine (Harkless $1.3M, Coulibaly $19.2M). It reads like an error.
- Owner label differs between team select ("win-now") and the career screen ("frugal"). Screenshot: step3.png.
- Free agency lets me exceed 15 players ("16/15, 1 to cut by opening night") with no immediate cut prompt, which is fine, but the roster full state at day 1 was confusing since the draft picks were already counted.

## Against the genre
- Versus 2K MyNBA: far better at explaining why I won or lost, and the CBA is simplified without feeling fake. Weaker on spectacle and star presentation.
- Versus Basketball GM: more guidance, a nicer phone UI, owner story and a live game I can coach with real calls. BBGM has fewer words on screen.
- Versus Football Manager (what I hear): not tested.
- Versus Hoop Land and others: not tested.

## Would I pay and keep playing
$7.99 phone unlock: yes. $14.99 on Steam: probably, if the depth grows past two seasons. I would put 10-20 hours in before tiring. Recommend to a friend: 8/10.

## The one change that would most raise my score
Make money consequences loud at the moment of decision: a plain-language banner on any signing or extension that crosses the tax ("This costs $20M in tax and breaks the owner's goal"), plus tap-to-explain on Bird rights, mid-level and options.
