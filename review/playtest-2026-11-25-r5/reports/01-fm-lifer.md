# 01 — The FM Lifer
## Who I am and what I played
Dallas Wranglers (real-roster league, West, 14th of 30 on roster rank), GM + Head Coach, phone viewport 390x844. Two full seasons plus the offseason between them. 2026-27: 51-31, 5th seed, lost 3-4 to Memphis in round 1. 2027-28: 49-33, 4th seed, lost 3-4 to Denver in round 1. Both exits came in a Game 7.

I coached roughly 35 games live, a few of them as a partial sitting (Skip ahead to halftime, then clutch time, then Sim to final). That includes both Game 7s. I set a game plan for nearly every opponent, 30+ in total. I also did a deadline trade, a salary dump, an extension and re-sign round, the draft and the free-agency window. Time spent was about 4 hours of wall clock, mostly waiting on live games (about 2.5 minutes each at 8x). Big sims took 2-5 s.

My automation (a script clicking through) did the game-day adjustments, so my live moves were simple and repetitive: Slow down or Push pace, Cautious, Attack the top matchup. That shapes my verdict on "does my coaching matter". Real FM-style tinkering would test more than I did.

## Play log
1. **Preseason, Oct 20 2026.** Today screen: owner goals (39 wins, playoffs, 9,200 minutes for players 23 and under), a "release 2" queue item, and a 4-step guide (rotation, playbook, opener plan, live game). Learnable at once. The staff's cut picks were McGee and Caleb Martin ($19.4M dead money). I cut Biberovic ($3.0M) and McGee instead, and the dialog showed the dead money before I confirmed.
2. **Opener at HOU, Oct 21.** The game plan page is real FM. It has counters ("Hedge coverage / Should help / Add"), a who-guards-whom board, Double a star, Target a defender, Hack-a-player and Junk defense. I added Hedge and "Attack Durant" and lost 97-113. The halftime report said I trailed on shot quality and turnovers, and the post-game said "one night is too few to grade your plan". I felt taken seriously.
3. **Live loop learned.** The game runs in real time at 1x/2x/4x/8x and pauses. In-game tabs are Now, Players, Tactics and Stats. Tactics has mentality, tempo, three-point rate, P&R coverage, help defense, physicality and per-scorer "Attack/Stop him" buttons, all showing what the opponent is running ("Spot-up: 18 points on 15 trips"). This is the best live panel I have seen in a basketball sim.
4. **Nov 2026 stretch.** 5-3 after 8, then 11-7 on Nov 25, 7th in the West and +4.6 per game. High-scoring league: 129-118, 136-126, 133-109 were normal.
5. **Season Analysis, Nov 15.** "Your live coaching: -2.8 pts +/-1.5 over 10 games. 19 tactical changes. Nothing clear in them. The staff's bench would have done about as well." Later it read -2.3 pts +/-1.7 over 12 games. In season 2 it read 0.0 +/-1.0 over 4 games. This is the most FM-like feature I have seen in any basketball game: the game audits me. It also stung, because my moves were slightly negative.
6. **Lively extension, Nov 15.** The Re-sign page opens extensions during the season. Lively (86/91, 22) asked $43.7M x 5. I signed at $43.4M. That pushed 27-28 committed money to $225M, $12.7M over the tax line with a $21.5M tax bill. The game warned me in the sheet ("about $20.6M in tax as things stand") and I clicked through anyway. My mistake, and the information was there.
7. **ATL offers.** The AI offered Alexander-Walker for Martin, Cisse, de Larrea and two firsts. I declined, because the ask was lopsided.
8. **Trade deadline, Feb 11 2027.** The staff's call was "stand pat". Its best buy was Jimmy Butler (37, expiring) for Irving, Gafford and three firsts, which is a silly suggestion. I built my own deal with ATL: Alexander-Walker for Risacher plus a 2031 1st and a 2029 1st (LAL) plus $1.7M cash. The AI said "Not even close", then "Close: add a little more". It blocked me with the Stepien rule ("can't be left without a first in back-to-back drafts"), and "What would make this work?" built a counter with cash. The confirm sheet showed rotation impact: who starts, who loses minutes, what I give up later. That was the most satisfying FO beat.
9. **Season 1 end.** 51-31, Sixth Man for Aldama, 2 All-Stars, Coach of the Month twice. Game 7 at MEM: halftime -13, lost 132-149. The report said my shots were better by +17.1 pts and shooting luck cost -21.7. That was honest and cold.
10. **Offseason.** Salary dump: I put Washington (76 OVR, $19.8M) on the trading block. MEM took him for a 2nd, which took me from $8M over the tax line to $8.3M under it. The re-sign page ran team options, a 3-yr Christie deal at $11.9M, and Irving's extension at 35 ($31.9M x 1 yr, cheaper than his $42M). I opened free agency over the tax with the MLE, so I only browsed. The draft was one 2nd-round pick (auto).
11. **Season 2 development.** Flagg +3 to 88, Irving -3 to 83 at 35, Martin -4. The Growth screen explains each change.
12. **Season 2, Game 7 vs DEN.** Down 3-2, I won G6 at home, then lost G7 107-103. DEN had 18 offensive rebounds to my 5 (-13.7 pts) and 24-32 on free throws. The in-game panel had told me to "Win the glass". My script never acted on it. A human would have.
13. **Owner review S2.** Season grade -6: 49 wins (+5), first-round loss (-35), payroll under the limit (+24).

## What hooked me (top 5, each with evidence)
1. **The game audits me.** "Your live coaching -2.8 pts +/-1.5 over 10 games... The staff's bench would have done about as well" (Season Analysis). No other basketball sim tells me my tinkering is noise.
2. **The post-game report separates tactics, shooting luck and my moves.** Example: "looks -13.2, shooting luck -4.8, free throws +2 = -16", and "the rest of the night replayed 200 times with your moves and without them".
3. **The game-plan screen.** Counters labelled "Should help / No clear edge / Could hurt", who-guards-whom with the mismatch stat ("Finishing 92 vs interior defense 76"), and plans that last one game or a series.
4. **The trade AI and CBA.** Stepien block, counters that add cash, impact preview ("Into the starting five... Minutes a game: de Larrea 12 -> 0"). The tax warning on Lively was visible before I signed.
5. **Halftime as a real decision point.** A report with what is deciding the game, what the staff thinks, "Both benches", "Their coach: no plan or adjustment yet", and "Your moves" logged afterwards.

## What pushed me away (top 5, each marked dealbreaker / major / minor, with evidence)
1. **Live coaching rarely moves the needle (major, for this audience).** Both seasons' "Your live coaching" read about 0 to -2.8 pts, inside the noise. If the honest answer is that you cannot beat the staff's bench, the depth feels decorative. A human who reads the panel may do better than my script, but I could not prove it.
2. **Game 7 pain, twice, from luck (minor to major).** MEM -21.7 pts of shooting luck; DEN's 18 offensive boards. Honest, but two exits in a row in Game 7 at 55% made me suspect scripted cruelty. I cannot prove it either way.
3. **Staff deadline advice is poor (minor).** "Trade for Jimmy Butler" meant Irving, Gafford and three firsts for a 37-year-old expiring contract, labelled "No clear edge".
4. **Live game is slow and verbose to drive (minor).** Each game is minutes of real time; no pause-on-event by default; the Now panel and sheets are tall on a phone. Skip ahead helps.
5. **Staff meetings and "Staff still looking..." (minor).** "No meetings yet, 4 left" never explained what meetings do for me; counters often said "No clear edge", so many plans felt empty.

## Against the genre (FM / BBGM / MyNBA / Hoop Land: where it wins and loses for me)
- **vs FM:** wins on explanations (shot-quality vs luck, replayed alternatives) and on how readable the match-day panel is on a phone. Loses on training schedules, individual instructions, set pieces, scouting depth, and the sheer number of levers. The audit feature is something FM should copy.
- **vs BBGM:** much deeper on tactics and story; BBGM wins on speed, openness and numbers I can mine.
- **vs MyNBA/MyGM:** better cap/CBA and trade AI; far less visual spectacle.
- **vs Hoop Land / Pro Basketball Manager:** more modern, better explained, cleaner on mobile.

## Would I pay and keep playing
$7.99 phone unlock: yes, easily. $14.99 on Steam: yes if it stays this explained and gets more tactical levers (training, individual roles, set plays). I would put 40-80 hours in, mostly in rebuilds and per-opponent plans. Recommend to a friend: 8/10 (friends who like FM, 6/10 for casual fans).

## The one change that would most raise my score
Make live coaching provably matter in at least some spots, and show me when it did. The audit says my moves are noise; a spot where a coach's call visibly swings an outcome (a mismatch hunt, a rebounding plan, a foul-trouble gamble) would make all of the depth feel earned.
