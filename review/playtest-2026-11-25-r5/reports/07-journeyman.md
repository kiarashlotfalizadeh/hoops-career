# 07 — The Career Journeyman

## Who I am and what I played
Real 2026 league, GM + Head Coach, 390x844 phone. Started with Charlotte (37-45, play-in loss). Took the LA Cutters offer after year 1. Spent 8 seasons there (24-58, 24-58, 37-45, 42-40, 46-36, 47-35, 25-57, 32-50), tanked on purpose, got fired after two poor seasons, took Washington's offer and went 45-37. That is 10 full seasons (2026-27 to 2035-36), about 820 games, nearly all by sim. I coached 1 game live (a play-in elimination game, 8x then "Sim to final"). I ran season-test, star-check and real-check once each. Roughly 4 hours of real time, a lot of it fighting my own script.

## Play log
1. **Oct 2026, hook.** Create took 7.5 s. Today screen asks me to cut 17 to 15 and offers "staff's picks" with dead-money numbers. Clear, two-tap confirm. Owner goals (37 wins, play-in, 4,600 young minutes) felt concrete. Month 1: 6-8, a "don't panic about Charlotte" story. Liked it.
2. **Jun 2027, offseason one.** "2 openings would hear you out" appeared, with Career → Jobs showing each owner's rating of me against the coach they hired (43 vs 39, 43 vs 48...). This is the best career-layer idea I have seen in a basketball sim. Asked LAC, took it. A new-job moment card said "can't fire you in your first 2 seasons."
3. **Draft 2027.** #16 pick, Navarro 72/POT 81-85. Good board and a clear "Draft him" sheet. Only my one first-rounder, because my 2029 #3 pick was already owed to PHI and the game never told me when I took the job. I found out in 2029 when the lottery gave PHI my #3 pick.
4. **Free agency 2027 and 2028.** Interest labels ("Not keen", "Very interested") and the loss emails are excellent: "Reese chose NYK. Their offer 4 yrs $14.4M, ours $14.8M. He rates NYK higher for their record (.561 vs .244) and market, so the same salary counts about 13% more." Markkanen in 2029: 39% more. I never won a contested free agent as a bad team. Day-8 bargains are real: Kyrie Irving (78 OVR, age 37) for $4.8M, Gafford 77 for $8.4M against $14.1M on day 1.
5. **2028-29, 24-58 again with Castle (81) and Wagler (80).** My own fault: I built a roster of guards with no centers. The Brief and staff notes said "defensive glass" and "pick-and-roll D" and I ignored them. DRtg was 29th. It felt fair.
6. **Apr 2030, play-in elimination.** The Brief screen is great (ORtg/DRtg/pace ranks, starting fives, matchup arrows). I watched at 8x and used "Sim to final": LAC won 140-115 with 84.0% eFG on looks worth 58.3%. The game report called it "about a 1-in-200 shooting night, +43.5 pts of shooting luck". Honest, but an 84% eFG night in the one game I watched is suspicious.
7. **Jun 2032.** Tyson Bradley (98/99, MVP) hit free agency and was "Very interested" in me. I had $17.7M of space and he asked $53M. Tried to dump salary: block offers all send salary back, and AI teams are over the cap (DET "over the cap by $22.5M", can't absorb). Could not clear space. A cap-space market this thin makes the best free-agent class unreachable for everyone.
8. **2032-34, steady.** 46-36, 47-35, three first-round exits. Owner copy repeated: "An honest season from an honest team" four times.
9. **Nov 2033, tank attempt.** Swapped my five stars with bench scrubs on the Rotation page. The game blocked sim with "3 regulars with no minutes" (Keep them out / Give staff's share). Wagler asked for a trade within a month. Staff note later: "Your change seems to have hurt −5.3 a game." But the Today card said "The slide: 6 straight losses ... That's luck, not the plan" while my benched stars were the cause. Contradicts the other note.
10. **2033-34, 25-57, then 2034-35, 32-50.** Owner after year one: "Poor. A second in a row (-40 or worse) and you are out." After year two: "You're fired", a "Between jobs" state, an agent with offers, WAS 44-38 offered. The arc is clear and fair. Reputation went 66 to 44 and I was still hired.
11. **2035-36 Washington.** Dybantsa (93) on my new roster, 45-37, first-round loss. Frugal owner, payroll cap goal ($342M).
12. **Long-run world.** Same stars for ten years (SGA, Luka, Wemby 97, Maxey, Edwards, plus generated Tyson Bradley). Titles: OKC, CLE, SAS (7 total by 2029, a mini-dynasty), OKC, MEM, CLE, DEN, DEN, BKN, DEN, DEN. Scoring leader: 33.3 (2027), 33.6, 37.0 (Maxey, 2030), 33.4, 35.8, 38.0 (Bradley, 2033), 35.9, 34.4. League PPG from season-test: 112.9 to 117.0 in two years.

## What hooked me
1. **The job market.** Career → Jobs shows "Rated you 43, Haskins 39" per opening, and who passes and why. I took a job, was fired and was hired again within one session, and each step explained itself.
2. **Fired on purpose, with a rule.** "A second one in a row (-40 or worse) and you are out" was in the owner letter, I followed it, and it happened as promised, with a Between-jobs screen.
3. **Free agency explanations.** The email naming both offers and the "same salary counts 13/30/39% more from them" math made losing feel fair, and taught me to wait for day 8.
4. **Reactive staff.** "3 regulars with no minutes", Wagler trade request after 0 minutes, "your change seems to have hurt −5.3 a game". The world pushed back on my choices.
5. **A living league.** Honors, Cup winners, retirements ("LeBron says goodbye after 24 seasons, 44,935 points"), reputation anchors, and a record book that keeps filling.

## What pushed me away
1. **Major: owed picks are invisible on arrival.** I took LAC without being told my 2029 #3 pick belonged to PHI. I found out at the lottery, with a "Draft lottery results: you hold #24 and #60" message.
2. **Major: no way to clear cap space.** Block offers always send salary back, and AI teams are capped out. The league's best free agent (98/99) was untouchable for me.
3. **Major: the "luck" narrative lies when you cause the slide.** "Shots that didn't fall. That's luck, not the plan" with my stars benched.
4. **Minor: owner copy repeats.** "An honest season from an honest team. I can build on that." four times across a decade; the owner grades my results against targets set from my own roster, so mediocre teams are rarely in danger.
5. **Minor: scoring drift.** Top scorers 33 to 38 ppg; league PPG +4 in two seasons per season-test. A fictional MVP (Bradley 98) wins MVP five years running. Still a dynasty-light league, but star dominance is high.

## Against the genre
- **vs Basketball GM:** much better narrative and career layer (jobs, firing, reputation); BBGM wins on trade AI transparency and having cap-space dumps.
- **vs FM:** the job market and board-room tension are FM-level; staff roles, scouting and tactics depth are lighter, but the "why" emails match FM's feedback quality.
- **vs MyNBA / Hoop Land:** more honest sim and no rubber-banding that I could see; fewer visual flourishes on my phone.

## Would I pay and keep playing
$7.99 phone unlock: yes. $14.99 on Steam: probably, if trades and live coaching are as good as the career layer. I would put 40 to 80 hours in a dynasty save. Recommend to a friend: 8/10.

## The one change that would most raise my score
Make the cap market playable: let me dump salary (teams with space, or a "salary dump with sweetener" in Block) so that a great free agent in a "Very interested" state is reachable, and show owed picks when I take a job.
