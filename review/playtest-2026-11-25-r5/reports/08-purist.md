# 08 — The Real-NBA Purist
## Who I am and what I played
- Career 1: real league, New York Knights, GM only. One full 2026-27 season (52-30, 3rd East, swept 4-0 by Toronto in round 2), awards, retirements, lottery, draft (I held #37, #45 and #56), re-sign period, free agency, and the 2027-28 preseason. No live games.
- Career 2: real league, San Antonio Stirrups, GM + Head Coach. One full 2026-27 season: 52-30, 3rd West, beat MEM 4-0 and DEN, beat LAL in the conference finals, lost the Finals to CLE. I coached 2 live games, both in part: the opener vs OKC (lost 116-112) and Game 1 at DEN (won 98-93). I used Skip ahead for most of both. The rest was simmed.
- Career 3: fictional league, Boston Shamrocks, GM only. Created and inspected, no games played.
- Device: 390x844 phone, headless Chromium, 2D court. Roughly 3 hours of play and notes. Sims were fast: a full regular season took 3-10 s per chunk, and career creation took about 5 s.

## Play log
1. **Oct 2026, Knicks start.** The roster is eerily right: Brunson, Towns, Bridges, Anunoby, Hart, McBride, Alvarado, Shamet, Dadiet, Diawara. The game made me cut to 15 and offered "staff's picks". It showed the $2.4M dead money on Clarkson before I confirmed. I liked that.
2. **Ratings sanity.** Wembanyama 97, Jokić 96, SGA 96, Dončić 95 at the top, which is believable. Anunoby 87 and Towns 87 sit above Brunson 86, which a Knicks fan would argue with. Tobias Harris at 81 (age 34, on the Spurs) is rated above Keldon Johnson (76) and level with Castle (80).
3. **Nov 19, 9-5.** Brunson was at 32.7 PPG and "the league's best". The first-week sample is noisy but fine. The Knicks scored 161 and 156 in two games (labelled "franchise records since 1996-97"). Those are absurd for 2026 and broke the spell. The league's team ratings by Jan were sane (OFF ratings 111-126).
4. **Jan 25.** Anunoby had a foot stress fracture and was out 41 games. The doctor wrote that it "can take a little off his speed for good". The league news also had a Cole Anthony / Khris Middleton trade, DeAndre Jordan and Horford retiring, and Draymond Green calling it a career. Those retirements are well calibrated to age and service.
5. **Trade UI.** I tried Okongwu from ATL for Drummond + Dadiet. The answer was "Not allowed: Salary doesn't match: NYK takes in $16.1M against a $7.1M limit, 125% of $5.4M sent". I padded it to a 4-for-1 and it blocked that too: "NYK would drop to 12 players. The in-season minimum is 13." The CBA is enforced and explained in plain words. That impressed me.
6. **Feb 6 (SAS).** OKC were 47-6, a 72-win pace. They finished 67-15, and 65-17 in the Spurs world. That is believably dominant but a touch too dominant for a league where the Thunder lose Holmgren-level minutes to injury.
7. **Playoffs, Knicks.** "Sim to end of playoffs" ran straight through a 4-0 sweep with no pause. The only drama was in a "While you were away" wall of text. For a GM-only save that is thin.
8. **Awards.** SGA MVP, Wembanyama DPOY, Holmgren Finals MVP, Dončić flagged ineligible at 63 of 65 games, Tatum at 50 games. The 65-game rule is a real-league detail I did not expect, and it is right. Sixth Man was Ja Morant on POR, and Rookie of the Year was Darius Acuff Jr. (SAC), a real 2026 draftee.
9. **Draft, May 2027.** The lottery reveal has real mechanics (14 teams, 4 balls drawn, NOP jumped 6th to 1st). **All 72 prospects are invented.** "Kyrie Hughes", "Josh Ibeh" and "Filip Ilić" fit a fictional league, but I was playing a real one. The 2026 rookie class was real, so the cliff to made-up names a year later is jarring.
10. **Re-sign.** McBride asked $11.9M x 4, "market $13.1M", "Very interested", with a slider and a two-tap confirm. Drummond asked $2.6M x 2. Towns asked $42.0M against his current $61.0M. The staff said "decline" on Hart's $22.4M option for an 81-OVR, which looks like a bad rec. The "tax ≈ $118.4M" headline did not update as I re-signed people (payroll $248M against a $213.1M tax line gave a $93.7M bill in the detail line).
11. **Free agency 2027.** Thin: at most 78 OVR on the mid-level (Mathurin, Mbeng). No star hit the market. AI signings read real (DeRozan to MIN at the minimum, Miles Bridges to IND, Ben Simmons to MIN). Westbrook (38) was listed as "SF" at 73 OVR for the minimum. A former two-way like Bez Mbeng at 77/82 is a stretch.
12. **Progression, 2027-28 preseason.** Brunson 86 to 83 at age 31, Towns 87 to 85, Drummond 74 to 70, Dadiet 69/76 to 75/81 (tagged "Breakout"). The #37 pick Eli Payne fell from 65/81 to 61/70 overnight. Brunson dropping 3 at 31 feels steep.
13. **Spurs, live opener.** Matchup page, then a halftime report. SAS trailed 65-50 and the report said OKC's shots "fell above their looks: 64.0% eFG on looks worth 49.2%" and called it luck, with a shot chart. I switched ball-screen coverage Drop to Hedge and transition to "Sprint back", and Q3-Q4 went SAS 62-51. Final report: looks +8.6, shooting luck -15.6, free throws +3 = -4. This is the best analytics screen I have seen in a basketball game.
14. **DEN series.** The game plan screen is deep: who guards whom with matchup hints ("Ball handling 94 vs perimeter defense 79"), double-team and show-high on a chosen creator, hack-a-player, junk defense. Fox went down again in Game 1, and Harris went down in the opener. Injury frequency felt high but was not absurd.
15. **Season 2 result.** Spurs reached the Finals at 52-30. Wembanyama averaged 23.1/10.9/3.1 with 3.2 blk in 28.5 min (74 GP). That is a bit below his real 2025-26 and his minutes were low.
16. **History book.** The book had "2025-26: NYK Knights def. Stirrups 4-1, Finals MVP Brunson". I can't tell you whether that matches the 2025-26 Finals, and I'd want the champion list checked. The all-time records are mostly real (Adebayo 83 at WAS on Mar 10, 2026, Barkley 33 reb, Rondo 25 ast, LeBron career totals).
17. **Fictional league.** Boston Shamrocks (94-rated Rayan Sarr), names are plausible and diverse, and the rating spread looks the same as the real league's.

## What hooked me (top 5, each with evidence)
1. **The real rosters feel right on day one.** Spurs: Wembanyama 97, Fox, Castle, Vassell, Champagnie, Harper, Kornet, Quaintance. Knicks and OKC (Mitchell, Hartenstein, Jaylin Williams, Sorber, Topić) are correct. I trusted the game within minutes.
2. **The halftime report.** "OKC +11.3 pts · luck / That part is luck, with nothing to change", then a click on Tactics and a visible effect (Q3-Q4 SAS 62-51).
3. **CBA that explains itself.** "125% of $5.4M sent" and "in-season minimum is 13" appear in the trade sheet. Nothing is hidden.
4. **League details that only a real fan checks.** The 65-game award rule (Dončić 63 games, Tatum 50), real retirements (Jordan, Horford, Green), All-Star MVP, the In-Season Cup MVP, Adebayo's 83 in the records.
5. **Quality of the team-stat table.** Ratings per 100 possessions, TS%, USG%, ON NET: it reads like a real tracking site.

## What pushed me away (top 5, each marked dealbreaker / major / minor, with evidence)
1. **Invented 2027 draft class and a thin 2027 FA class** (major). Real 2026 rookies exist (Acuff, Wagler, Harper, Quaintance), and one year later every prospect is made up. The best FA is 78-OVR on the mid-level. This breaks the real-league promise after season one.
2. **Aging and progression swings** (major). Curry 87 to 81 in one summer, Brunson 86 to 83 at 31, Dadiet +6, Payne 65/81 to 61/70. A real fan sees the real stars collapse too fast.
3. **Absurd single-game scores early** (minor). Knicks 161 and 156 (labelled franchise records) when the league average is about 115. Brunson at 32.7 PPG after 14 games.
4. **A few ratings and labels out of line** (minor). Tobias Harris 81 (34yo) over Keldon Johnson; Westbrook listed as "SF"; Anunoby 87 over Brunson 86; staff recommends declining Hart's 81-OVR $22.4M option; "Mbeng 77/82" for an undrafted guard.
5. **Flat story beats** (minor). "Sim to end of playoffs" gave no pause for the Knicks sweep, owner letters repeat the same line ("I didn't buy this team to be in the middle"), and Tidwell repeats "nothing the staff measured helps from here".

## Against the genre (FM / BBGM / MyNBA / Hoop Land: where it wins and loses for me)
- **Vs BBGM:** it wins on real names, the in-game tactical layer and the halftime explainer. BBGM wins on a bottomless draft/FA pool, long-run history and faster loops.
- **Vs MyNBA/2K:** it wins on honesty. There is no scripting; shooting luck is surfaced, not hidden. 2K has real faces and a real draft class for years.
- **Vs FM:** it has FM's "why did this happen" voice and a decent opponent scouting screen. It loses on depth of staff, training, contracts and player conversations.
- **Vs Hoop Land:** more realistic and more explained; fewer menus to hide behind.

## Would I pay and keep playing
- **$7.99 phone unlock:** yes. A real-league GM game this clean is rare.
- **$14.99 on Steam:** yes only if the draft class and progression issues get fixed. At the moment I would wait for a sale.
- **Hours I'd put in:** 30-60 on the current build, then I'd burn out on fictional prospects.
- **Recommend to a friend: 8/10.**

## The one change that would most raise my score
Carry the real-world data forward: a real-ish 2027+ draft class (named prospects from real 2027 and 2028 lists, or at least real-style pools) and a market that keeps real stars (LeBron, Curry, Durant, Giannis) on realistic aging curves, so year two still feels like the NBA.
