# 02 — The Rebuilder
## Who I am and what I played
Sacramento Crowns (30th of 30 on the New League rank), real league, GM + Head Coach, job security ON (default 50/100 at start). Phone viewport 390x844, headless. I played 5 full seasons (2026-27 to 2030-31, about 410 regular-season games plus 4 playoff runs and a play-in). I coached 1 game live (the 2029 play-in elimination game at Dallas) and quick-simmed the rest. About 6 hours of wall-clock, a lot of it fighting my own scripts. I did not take the Toronto job offer in year 5.

Records: 21-61, 23-59, 39-43 (8th seed, lost R1), 50-32 (5th seed, won a series 4-3, lost R2), 51-31 (3rd seed, lost R2). Owner grades: +20, +27, +55, +65, +50.

## Play log
1. **Oct 21 2026, setup.** Sacramento is 30th, yet it holds Sabonis 85, LaVine 82 and Murray 80. The owner's goals are "win about 21, give 3,600 minutes to players 23 or under, stay under the tax". The owner asks for exactly what a tank delivers. Sim to 1 month stopped at game 5 for Monk's torn meniscus (31 games) and Simmons (Achilles, 110 games).
2. **Oct 30, trade block.** "Shop a player" is excellent: "5 teams would deal for Sabonis" with each team's best package. The labels were confusing: "Lowball", "Below value" and "Fair value" mean how I'm treated, not how the offer looks. Most offers were old salary filler.
3. **Oct 30, Sabonis trade.** I built it by hand with Memphis: Sabonis for Coward (79, age 23, $6M), Grant (a $34M contract) and UTA's 2027 1st. The GM line moves "They want a lot more", "Close: add a little more", "They'd take it". I felt clever. The confirm sheet says a first-round pick can't be undone and shows the rotation effect ("Acuff to the bench"). Murray went to Detroit for Thompson (79, age 24, cheap to 2031) plus Robinson and two 2nds. At the Feb 6 deadline Hunter went to Memphis for Green, a LAL 1st (top-4 protected) and four 2nds. The AI counter-offered by asking for $3.1M cash. The deadline market for veterans was thin: mostly "salary dump" labels.
4. **Season 1.** I reached 15-35 at the deadline, finished 21-61 and was 14th in the West. Owner mail at 6-22 said "Winning is good business. Keep it up", and 2-14 got "This is what a sound team looks like". Tone-deaf templates. The review was "A good season", +20 and +22, job security 60/100. Tanking is rewarded, not punished.
5. **Jun 2027, the lottery.** It is a real sequential reveal from #14 up, with odds, "You slip to No. 3, the balls didn't bounce your way" and HOU jumping 3rd to 1st. Fun, and the best moment of the offseason. But Today only says "The lottery is in". I found the reveal by wandering into Office > Draft. I got #3 in 2027 and slid again in 2028 (3rd to 6th).
6. **Draft 2027.** #3 Shawn Terry (C, staff read 78/87). The offers sheet for #3 listed 28 offers, for example UTA's Keyonte George plus two 1sts. #19 Ibeh and #24 Griffin were duds: Ibeh's staff read of 62/77 was really 58/70, and Griffin went 68/77 to 54/58 by age 23. Releasing Ibeh cost $12.2M of dead money (the sheet warned me and I clicked through). A bust pick is a real cap cost, which is good.
7. **Summer 2027, free agency.** I had $19M of space. Stars were out of reach: "NYK's offer is ahead. Leading takes $25.1M". I signed Whitmore (74/79, age 22) and Clowney (72/78). Whitmore dropped 4 points the next summer.
8. **Summer 2028, cap space.** I had $62.5M of space, since 2028-29 committed payroll was just $114M. Castle (PG, 23, 81/84) asked $30.7M but "Lead with $43.4M" was the price to beat HOU. I bid 4 years at $43.4M and won on day 3. He rose +5, +6, +1, then -1, and was MIP (86-88).
9. **2028-29, the turn.** 25-29 at the deadline, 39-43 final (+16 wins), 8th seed. Live play-in game at Dallas: won 132-115. Post-game said Tactics -7.7 pts, Shooting luck +12.4 pts. Honest. First round lost to HOU. Castle set a 44-point franchise playoff record. This was the "rebuild turns" moment. It is carried by one free-agent signing, not by the draft.
10. **2029-30.** 50 wins on a 36 goal. First series win 4-3 over New Orleans. Lost R2 to OKC. Terry +5 and +6 (86/90). The staff's deadline card offered "Trade for Nikola Jokić" against "Stand pat". My script chose Stand pat, so I never learned if it was real.
11. **2030-31.** 51 wins, 3rd seed, R2 exit again. Toronto (30-52) offered me their job in an "important" message. Nice touch. The league parity is good: BOS, PHX, CLE, SAS champions in different years, with Wembanyama MVP at 99.
12. **Sims stop a lot.** Almost every week an AI GM offer ("Coward for Porter Jr.", "Kuminga for Raynaud + Riley + pick") interrupted "Sim to deadline" or "Sim to end of regular season". They are mostly "we are rebuilding, X doesn't fit our timeline" templates. I counted 6 stops in one 50-game stretch.

## What hooked me (top 5)
1. **The Castle moment (summer 2028).** $62.5M of cap space after two years of building flexibility, then a "lead with $43.4M" bid that won on day 3 and a +5 summer. That is the rebuild turning, and it came from a decision I made.
2. **The trade desk.** "Shop a player" lists every team's best package and every deal I build gets a graduated reaction. Selling Sabonis and Murray for 79-rated 23-24 year olds plus a first felt like real GM work.
3. **The lottery reveal.** Sequential, with odds, "slip to No. 3", an "Offers (28)" sheet on the pick. It made a dull June a story.
4. **Terry's arc (77 to 86/90).** The Growth tab names which attributes moved (stamina -2, speed -1). Readable.
5. **The post-game luck line.** "Tactics -7.7, Shooting luck +12.4" after the play-in win told me I'd gotten away with one. Honest in a way BBGM never is.

## What pushed me away (top 5)
1. **Draft picks feel like lottery tickets, not development (major).** Of five first-rounders I drafted (#3, #6, #19, #24, plus #17/#22 auto-picked), only Terry became a star. Hunt (#6, "78/81-91") peaked at 78/80. Acuff dropped 75, 74, 70 at age 20-22 with pot 83, 80, 75. Griffin went 68, 54. Meanwhile #60 and #35 picks gained +5 to +7. Aggregate progression looks too random to plan around.
2. **Free-agent bidding forces a ~40% overpay (major).** "Leading takes $25.1M" against a $18.6M ask. Castle at $43.4M a year for 4 years is a burden. Good for story, bad as a repeatable rebuild tool, and there is no real way to compete for FAs while bad (interest "Not keen", "26% wins").
3. **The owner rewards tanking and does not react (minor/major).** Goal "win 21", 21 wins, "A good season". Mail says "Winning is good business. Keep it up" at 6-22. Fans go "Fed up", but the owner stays "Steady". No job pressure at all in years 1-2.
4. **The trade-block labels and "give up more than you get" lines (minor).** "Lowball" and "They'd jump at it: you're overpaying" are about what I'm doing, in language that sounds the other way round. I misread three offers.
5. **Constant AI-offer interruptions (minor).** "Sim to end of regular season" stops about every 7-10 days for a boilerplate offer. Fine for control freaks, annoying for a 5-year loop.

## Bugs (repro steps, screenshot path)
- **Stale rotation after a trade (minor).** After the Sabonis trade the Rotation screen shows the new players at 0 target minutes until the next game is played, even with "Let the assistant manage" ON. Screenshot: players/02-rebuilder/shots/16-rotation.png.
- **Live game reset (minor).** Hash-navigating to /live while a live game is running restarts the game state (score went from 32-22 to 0-0). "Resume live game" on Today works. No screenshot (confirmed by my score logs).
- **Today never points at the lottery reveal (minor).** After "Sim to end of playoffs" the phase is Draft lottery and the Today screen says only "The lottery is in". The reveal lives in Office > Draft. Screenshots: shots/22-lotterytoday.png, shots/24-draft.png.
- **Staff-pick release asks for a second tap (minor, by design?).** "Release Bryce Graves · staff's pick" on the queue needs a second "Confirm: Release..." tap. The first tap looks like a no-op to anyone not reading the button, and the Play bar stayed on "Release 1 player". Screenshot: shots/61-rel.png.
- **"Sim to trade deadline" unavailable on deadline day (minor).** On the day itself only "Sim 1 day" is offered; my loop got `play false` 3 times. Fine if known.
- **Owner mail tone vs. record (minor).** See #3 above; mails 2026-27 Mar 1 and Apr 1.

## Against the genre (FM / BBGM / MyNBA / Hoop Land)
- **vs Basketball GM:** Better trade AI feedback, much better FA decisions, real cap pain (dead money, options, MLE). Worse on draft-class clarity: BBGM's prospect pot/age are more reliable, and its tank is more rewarded and risk-free. This game's staff read vs true gap is a more interesting problem, but progression is noisier.
- **vs Football Manager:** The Growth tab and attribute-level change lines ("Stamina -2, speed -1") are FM-adjacent and I like them. But FM gives you a youth coaching and pathway system. Here I can see "Development coaching 17th of 30" and cannot change it, which is exactly where a rebuilder wants a lever.
- **vs NBA 2K MyGM:** Much deeper, no animation needed, and the lottery and offers are more fun than 2K's.
- **vs Hoop Land:** Hoop Land is simpler; this wins on trade/FA depth and clarity.

## Would I pay and keep playing
$7.99 phone unlock: yes, easily, for the trade desk and the 5-season loop alone. $14.99 on Steam: probably, if the development lever and the draft layer improve. I'd put in about 40 hours before I'd want a second save (the one-save loop is quick: 5 seasons in about 6 hours of clumsy scripted play, probably 3 hours for a human). Recommend to a friend: 8/10.

## The one change that would most raise my score
Make draft and youth development something I can steer and learn from. Show true-vs-staff accuracy by scouting level, let me set development coaching and a youth pathway, and make top-10 picks pay off at a steadier rate than second-rounders do. Right now the rebuild's payoff came from cap space, not from the picks I spent three seasons collecting.
