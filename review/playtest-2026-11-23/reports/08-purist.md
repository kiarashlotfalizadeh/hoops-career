# 08 — The Real-NBA Purist

## Who I am and what I played
- Persona: 22, knows every rotation, contract and second-round rookie. Trust is earned when the real players feel right.
- Career 1 (main): real league, Oklahoma City, GM only. One full 2026-27 season through the Finals (65-17, title, 4-1 over Cleveland), then the 2027 draft/re-sign/free agency, then into 2027-28 preseason to check progression.
- Career 2: fictional league, Boston, GM + Head Coach. Played game 1 live (partly) and simmed the whole 2026-27 season (29-53).
- Career 3: real league, Boston, GM + Head Coach, the "second real season" comparison. Coached about 1 live game partly (mentality, zone/press menus, timeout, Players/Stats tabs), then simmed to the end of the regular season (53-29, 2nd East seed).
- Device: 390x844 phone, 3D off. About 40 sim calls. Roughly 2.5 hours of play.
- Honesty note: I cannot verify anything after about June 2026, so every "wrong" below carries a confidence level.

## Play log
1. **Oct 2026, new career.** Dumped the league ratings table (400 players, 14 skills each) and every team page. First impression: the data is shockingly deep. 2026 rookies (Dybantsa WAS, Peterson UTA, Boozer MEM, Harper SAS, Wagler LAC), Aday Mara, Stirtz, Topic, Sorber and Steinbach are all on rosters. Feels like a real 2026-27 snapshot.
2. **Roster spot-checks.** Giannis MIA, Durant HOU, Harden CLE, Garland LAC, Trae Young WAS (traded to LAL mid-season), Luka and Reaves LAL, LeBron on PHI at 42. Kawhi on TOR at 92 OVR. Ratings look plausible, but I cannot confirm the post-June moves (Kawhi to Toronto, LaMelo to MIN, Morant to POR, Randle to BKN, Dort to ATL). Confidence they are right: low to medium.
3. **Oct 20, opening night.** Owner wants "win about 69 games, reach the Finals". Fair for a 96-OVR SGA team, but 69 wins is a very hard number.
4. **Oct 28 to Feb 11 (sim to deadline).** Sim stopped about every 10 days for a trade offer. The offers are templated ("We're rebuilding, and X doesn't fit our timeline. He can help a team like yours win now.") and every one asks for Kenrich Williams, Stirtz and a pick. OKC was 45-10 at the deadline.
5. **Apr 13.** OKC finished 65-17 (+12.1 per game), 10 games clear of SAS and DEN (55-27). The owner still put me on a "Warm seat" for missing his 68-win line despite 65 wins and a #1 seed. That felt harsh but it is stated openly.
6. **Playoffs.** OKC 4-0 DAL, 4-2 HOU, 4-3 DEN (DEN 3-4, a close one), 4-1 SAS, then 4-1 CLE. Finals MVP SGA, 36.8 PPG. Realistic shape: the 1-seed does not sweep everything, and the Nuggets nearly took the 3rd round.
7. **Awards.** MVP SGA ("his third straight"), DPOY Wembanyama (3.7 BLK), ROY Keaton Wagler (14.5 PPG), MIP Jalen Green, 6MOY Aldama, COY me. Credible. The #1 pick Dybantsa (80 OVR) averaged 7.8 PPG in 29.7 MPG, which looks odd (see below).
8. **Jun 2027 retirements.** LeBron "calls it a career" and enters the Hall of Fame on the spot (44,374 points, 24 seasons). Real-history record book kept him as the career leader. I loved the retirement news and jersey-retirement lines.
9. **2027 draft.** The class is entirely generated names (Ethan Wheeler, Shawn Adams). Fine for a sim, but the 2027 real prospects are not there.
10. **Re-sign and free agency.** Honest and clear. Committed payroll was $254.7M against a $213.1M tax line, and the game says so in plain numbers. FA pool is plausible (VanVleet 80 OVR at $11.6M, Kuminga, Simons, Hart).
11. **2027-28 preseason.** Ratings moved the way a purist expects: Curry 87 to 80 at 39, Durant 87 to 81, Wemby 97 to 98, Dybantsa 80 to 84, Jokic 97 to 95. Giannis had moved to CHA. The world evolves.
12. **Fictional league.** Boston with an 85-OVR guard, owner "win about 26 games". Players have names like J. Mbappe, K. Ford (98 OVR), M. Lyons. Played game 1 live, lost 121-130. The post-game report is the best thing I saw (below). Finished 29-53; Dante Hart then demanded a trade because he "expects 32 minutes and plays 26.8".
13. **Real Boston, GM + HC.** Preseason it made me release Mike Conley (39) and Tucker DeVries. The release button needs a second tap to confirm, easy to miss. 27-9 at Jan 6, 53-29 and the 2 seed at the end, against an owner target of 48 wins.

## Roster fidelity (2026-27), as far as I know
- **Right or plausible (high confidence):** Jokic 31 C DEN 97/97; SGA 28 PG OKC 96; Wembanyama 23 C SAS 97 (BLK 99, INTD 90); Doncic 27 PG LAL 95; Tatum 28 BOS 91 (rehabbed); Brunson NYK 87; Edwards MIN 91; Haliburton IND 91; Curry 38 GSW 87 with 3P 99; Holmgren 91; Durant HOU 38; Harden to CLE and Garland to LAC (the real December 2025 swap); Butler to GSW; Flagg DAL and Knueppel CHA as sophomores; Bam's 83-point game in the record book.
- **Positions and ages.** Positions are one of five letters and mostly fit. Ages are calendar-year (LeBron shows 42, Giannis 32 at season start while both are still one year younger on opening night). Minor.
- **Moves I cannot confirm (low to medium confidence):** Kawhi on TOR (92 OVR, scored 29.5 PPG on 75 games); LaMelo to MIN; Morant to POR; Randle BKN; George BOS; Dort ATL; Towns NYK; Giannis MIA (and HOU/CHA in other careers). If any of these is wrong, a purist will notice immediately.
- **Rating oddities.** Perimeter defence is compressed: the best PERD in the league is about 83. Dort 78, Caruso 75, Thybulle 75, Herb Jones 79 (real elite wing stoppers feel 85-90+). Jokic's 3P is 67 and his rebound output was 10.4 (real about 12-13). Bigs: Gobert 84 OVR at 34, Kessler 85, Clingan 85, Mobley 85 look fair. 8 of 79 centres have 3P of 70 or more, which is realistic.
- **Injuries/rookies:** injuries carried into preseason (Topic back spasms, Sorber knee, Mara ACL out 72 games, Knueppel hamstring). The rookie classes of 2025 and 2026 are largely right.
- **Coaches:** all 30 head coaches are invented ("Steve Coleman", "Marcus Olander"), all "Since 2026-27". No Spoelstra, Kerr, Popovich or Mazzulla. Big gap for me. After the season four more were fired and replaced with invented names.

## Rotations and coaching by the AI
- Real minutes shape: top minutes are Murray 37.1, Cunningham 35.6, Banchero 35.7, Young 35.9. Only 4 players are above 35.5 MPG. SGA 32.3 is a bit low (real ~34) and Jokic 31 too low. The OKC "closers" and starters table with five-man ORtg/DRtg and a 95-lineup list is believable.
- Stars go cold at minutes caps ("Out 2 games, knee soreness") rather than playing 40, so the "managed load" matches the modern league.
- AI coach styles are wildly "Drop": almost every team defends the pick-and-roll in drop, a few Switch, one Blitz (GSW). Real-league coverage variety is much wider (Miami zone, Boston switching). Slight homogenisation.
- Staff calls in the sim are narrated ("Tried Hedge in place of Blitz. Could hurt. Keeping Blitz.") which I liked.

## Record book and history
- Genuinely good. Champions and MVPs back to 1946-47: Finals MVPs match what I know (Brown 2024, SGA 2025, Jokic 2023), 2024-25 OKC def. IND 4-3 is correct. League records (Wilt 100, Skiles 30 assists, Curry 402 threes, 73-9, Charlotte Bobcats 7-59) are real. A purist sees the past, with the new nicknames pasted on ("Twisters def. Racers 4-3").
- Immersion breaks: team names. Hornets are "Wasps", Spurs "Stirrups", Nuggets "Prospectors", Thunder "Twisters", Cavaliers "Lancers", Celtics "Shamrocks", Pacers "Racers". "Stirrups" for San Antonio is the worst; "Prospectors", "Pathfinders", "Boosters" and "Petrels" are at least plausible. Arenas are generic ("Atlanta Arena", "Boston Garden", "Chicago Stadium"). The Lakers are "Lagoons" and the Clippers "Cutters", which reads like parody. I understand the store-safe reason, but in a real-name league it grates: real players, real contracts, fake teams, fake coaches.
- LeBron immediately in the Hall of Fame, and his No. 23 retired by two teams (LAL and CLE), is a nice touch.

## Fictional league: do generated players feel like basketball players?
- Mostly yes, with caveats. Names read like real players (J. Mbappe, K. Ford, M. Lyons, J. Willis) and the league has proper stars: K. Ford SF 98/99, 33.1 PPG, PER 37.9 (too good, a one-man era), D. Hunt SG 70.4 TS%, M. Lyons 25.9/10.4/9.3.
- Position skew: the top 400 hold 109 SGs and 102 Cs but only 54 PGs (the real league: 95 SG, 79 C, 71 PG). Few real point guards, so assist leaders top out at 9.3.
- 19 of 102 centres have 3P of 70 or more (real about 10%), and 3PM leader is a C (N. Carter, 4.7). Reads like a modern league but a bit stretchy.
- Stat shape is credible: league average 11.9 PPG per player, stars about 30. Real-seeming rotation (36 MPG tops).
- Hart's trade request ("expects 32 minutes, plays 26.8") and the staff's "sell Hart" call felt like a real front-office story.

## What hooked me (top 5)
1. **The real past and present are there.** Champions back to 1946, real MVPs, Bam's 83 and Wilt's 100, 2026 rookies on rosters, injuries carried in.
2. **The post-game report.** "What decided it" in points (eFG, turnovers, rebounds, "Boston attacked drop coverage with pull-up jumpers +6.3 pts") plus "Next time" fixes. It explains why I lost the opener.
3. **Real-feeling season arc.** 65-17 team does not sweep; the 3-seed DEN took us to 7; MVP/DPOY/ROY choices are credible; standings spread 55-27 behind a 65-17 leader is right.
4. **Ratings that follow aging.** Curry at 39, Durant at 39, Wemby up, Dybantsa up, Jokic 97 to 95. The long-run world moves.
5. **Cap/CBA clarity.** Resign screen shows committed $254.7M vs tax line $213.1M and tells me exactly what Keep Everyone costs, with option decisions and staff guidance.

## What pushed me away (top 5)
1. **Invented shell breaks immersion (major).** Wasps, Stirrups, Twisters; no real coaches; "Atlanta Arena". For a purist with real rosters this is a constant low hum. Evidence: all 30 coaches are invented and "Since 2026-27"; team page lists "Redtails/Wasps/Stirrups".
2. **Templated, constant trade offers (major).** Every offer is "X + Kenrich Williams + Stirtz + 1 pick" with the same quote, and big sims stop for them every 4-10 days (OKC Nov 3, Nov 18, Nov 20, Dec 21, Feb 2 ... Real BOS: Jan 23, Jan 27, Jan 31). It feels like spam and a rebuilding team "wants" a contender's star for filler.
3. **Perimeter-defence ratings compressed and Jokic's line low (minor to major).** The league's top PERD is about 83; Jokic 45 GP, 31 MPG, 10.4 reb; Haliburton 17.2 ppg at 91. Purists will check these first.
4. **Dybantsa-style rookie production (minor).** An 80-OVR #1 pick scoring 7.8 PPG at 29.7 MPG, while Wagler (14.5) wins ROY. Doesn't match the rating.
5. **Live game does not persist (major for coaches).** Leaving and resuming a live game restarts it at 0-0, 11:47, and tutorial sheets ("Staff makes tactics calls") block play until tapped.

## Against the genre
- **vs FM:** On par for tactics breadth; the staff recommending and then grading a plan later ("After 10 games, Season Analysis says whether it helped") is FM-like. Loses on depth of scouting and the people layer. Wins on pace: a season in about a minute.
- **vs BBGM:** Much richer on real data, tactics, history and story. BBGM still wins on trade AI (this one spams templated offers) and a fully generated league feel. Hoops Career's fictional league feels closer to BBGM, only more modern.
- **vs NBA 2K MyNBA/MyGM:** No licence, so it can never match the rosters/branding, but it matches the numbers better than 2K's sim and is far faster. Real players, invented teams is the awkward middle.
- **vs Hoop Land / Draft Day Sports:** Clear step up in presentation, stories and clarity.

## Would I pay and keep playing
- $7.99 phone unlock: yes, easily, if the real roster data stays correct after launch (I'd buy it on the strength of the history and report screens).
- $14.99 on Steam: only with real-name teams or an editor/roster-file import that lets me restore the real nicknames, coaches and arenas; as is, I'd wait for a sale.
- Hours: 40-60 across three or four careers; I'd chase real-league rebuilds (Wemby, Flagg, Knueppel) and re-run the same season to see how it diverges.
- Recommend to a friend: 8/10 for hardcore fans, 7/10 for casuals.

## The one change that would most raise my score
Ship an optional real-name pack (real team nicknames, arenas and real head coaches) or a one-tap "real names" toggle for the real league. Everything else in the game already treats the real NBA with respect, and the invented shell is the only thing that keeps reminding me it is a stand-in.
