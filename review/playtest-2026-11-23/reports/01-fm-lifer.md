# 01 — The FM Lifer
## Who I am and what I played
Football Manager lifer (15 editions), new to basketball sims. Real league, GM + Head Coach, **Dallas Wranglers** (15th of 30 by roster rank, West). Phone viewport 390x844, 3D court off (2D court only).

- Season 1: started 0-6, was 2-8 after 10, finished **45-37, 6th seed**, lost R1 2-4 at San Antonio (57-25) after taking G1 107-91. Owner target was 41 wins and the playoffs.
- Offseason: draft (no pick), re-sign period, free agency day 1 to day 8, preseason.
- Season 2: **57-25, 2nd in West**, then swept **0-4 by 7th-seed Minnesota** in R1. Owner target was 52 wins and a playoff round, so the season grade was -3 despite +25 on wins. Two job offers (LAC, MIA) arrived afterwards.
- Live games coached: **10 regular season plus playoff G1 (11 in total)**, all with a per-opponent plan set first (SAS, IND, MIA, OKC, HOU, SAC, SAS, UTA, BKN, and the SAS series plan). The rest was simmed in 7 to 30 day chunks. About 8 hours of play, most of it wrestling a scripted headless client through live games (about 6 to 7 minutes each).

## Play log
1. **Oct 20, preseason.** Today asks me to release 2 of 17. The staff's picks were McGee (free) and Caleb Martin ($19.4M dead money), so I released Biberovic ($3.0M dead) and McGee instead. A two-tap confirm shows the dead money. Good, an FM-style cost.
2. **Tactics tour.** System (style cards with a "Staff's read" fit label, for example Five-Out Motion "Similar fit" and Hub Big "Worse fit"), Shape sheet with a plus and a minus per shape, an Advanced drawer with about 25 controls (play-type weights, hunt mismatches, hot hand, transition, glass, press, crunch time, foul rules, timeouts, sub threshold), roster role stars ("Sasser Movement Shooter 5 of 5"), and Season Analysis with 14 grades. Genuinely FM-shaped. I switched to Five-Out Motion and the game said "8 more changes" it had made under the hood (slower, push less).
3. **Opener at HOU (Oct 21), lost 115-122.** Per-game halftime report said "Houston's shots fell above their looks: luck +6.9" and also "Houston attacked drop coverage with pull-up jumpers". I switched to Ice at the break. "Your moves" then logged "Drop → Ice · DAL 35-32 since". Q3 went 37-32 for me. I felt clever.
4. **Timeout play call (Q4, down 5).** Spain: Gafford. The game logged "Ran it: 1 point". Small, visible, and I loved having the result.
5. **Oct 22 to Nov 8: 1-7.** Five close or ugly losses. Ball security was my D grade (21 turnovers by Q3 at OKC, 93-120). Both the HOU and OKC halftime AI lines read "Nothing the staff would change right now" while I was at 14 turnovers to 6. Weak advice at the moment it mattered.
6. **Nov 4, changed defense to Switch Everything.** A one-tap warning said "Opponents will hunt Lively II on switched ball screens. Drop instead." A tally later (10 games, final) said **"Seems to hurt"** with a **Put back** button. I put it back on Jan 27. That is the loop FM players want: change, wait, get a verdict, revert.
7. **Nov 9 trade.** Staff said my need was ball security (F, 30th of 30). I asked ATL for CJ McCollum (PG, 35, $21M expiring). Verdict chip "Not even close", then "What would make this work?" added Cisse. Confirm sheet: "ball security F → D (30th → 25th), starting five: McCollum, minutes: de Larrea 17 → 9". I also dumped Martin's contract. After the trade the team went about **21-14 in the next stretch**.
8. **Oct 25 to Dec: counters can hurt.** Staff-added Hedge vs IND: halftime note "Indiana's roll men found space behind the hedge". The suggested counter cost me. I lost 127-133.
9. **Jan 27, 23-22 and Season Analysis.** "How teams see you" gave league ranks (net +0.9, 11th; ORtg 115.6 20th; DRtg 114.7 10th). The Five-Out Motion tally said "Too close to call" after 20 games. The game didn't claim credit it couldn't prove.
10. **Deadline (Feb 11).** Staff: "buy a player better than your fifth best (78). Nobody the staff asked takes what you can send." Stood pat. 28-25 at that point, finished 45-37.
11. **Apr 18, playoff G1 at SAS (Wemby out).** Coached live, won 107-91. Then simmed. Lost 2-4 with Flagg and Lively injured.
12. **Offseason.** Re-sign screen: three team options with staff's call, Lively asks $35.5M x5 with a years, raises and option slider and "Very interested". Re-signed Lively, McCollum ($4.0M), Risacher ($5.0M after declining his $17.4M option), extended Christie. Josh Hart (80 OVR) chose LAL: "Winning matters most to him", my $14.8M vs their $14.4M. The game explained why I lost.
13. **Season 2 (57-25), swept in R1.** Playoff message "Outscored by 9.0 a game. The box scores point to turnovers", and "Nothing the staff measured beats your plan". I simmed the series. Coach of the month and "beat all 29 teams" moments were fun.

## What hooked me (top 5)
1. **Change, tally, verdict, revert.** Defensive switch at Nov 4 became "Seems to hurt" with a Put back button on Jan 27. Offense: "Too close to call". The game admits when it cannot tell.
2. **Halftime and quarter reports separate luck from looks.** "OKC was +6.9 pts luck" on a 7-point loss told me my plan was fine and the shots were not. I haven't seen this in BBGM or MyNBA.
3. **Trade desk with a GM brain.** "Not even close", then "ATL would do it if you add Moussa Cisse", then a staff impact sheet before confirming. I dumped a contract and fixed a stated need in 4 taps.
4. **Per-opponent plans are visible.** Counter list with "Should help / No clear edge", who-guards-whom with inch and skill mismatch lines, "Double a star", "Target a defender" with the staff's star pick, Hack-a-player conditions. Lines like "Indiana's roll men found space behind the hedge" tied outcomes back to my plan.
5. **Honest FA and re-sign negotiations.** Years, raises, options sliders, bidding teams named, "Winning matters most to him". Losing Hart felt earned, not rigged.

## What pushed me away (top 5)
1. **Dead-air advice at crunch moments (major).** OKC Q3: 21 turnovers to 8 and the AI said "Nothing the staff would change right now"; HOU, UTA the same. The tool that logs "Your moves" did not notice the single biggest problem.
2. **Live game friction (major).** Leaving a live game mid-quarter loses it (resumes from the last break, same outcome). The "Skip" menu only appears when paused. A "Keep the speed" clutch prompt blocks play until tapped. At 4x a quarter still takes about 1.5 minutes of real time. Playoff series are simmed by default, so the best moment (a decisive Game 6) is easy to miss.
3. **Early-season roster chaos (minor/major).** 4 of my top 12 injured at tip-off, Irving and Flagg hurt for several games in the first 10, then another injury mid-game. Realistic but combined with team options and "Not keen" free agents it left no way to respond. Nobody would sign with a 2-8 team.
4. **Tactics screen density on a phone (minor).** The Advanced drawer is long and every row has five chips. "Change" has an invisible commit ("Done: keep the changes") that cost me a failed run. Role stars mean little without explanation.
5. **Some features are shells (minor).** Lineups tab empty until a game, Growth/draft barely engaged, staff "calls" mostly say "no clear edge". Complexity is real in tactics and trades but thin in training and scouting.

## Bugs (repro steps, screenshot path)
- **Cold-load redirect to /start (intermittent).** After relaunching the profile, going straight to `/#/team/tactics` bounced me to `#/start` (showing the title screen with "New league") 3 times, then a later launch was fine. Probably a race between route guard and career load (or my many leftover browser processes). Screenshot: `.../players/01-fm-lifer/shots/44.png`.
- **Final game state isn't finalized until you leave the page properly.** My script stopped on the Final screen once and the game resumed from Q4 3:28 on the next launch with the same score (115-122). Not necessarily a bug, but confusing.
- **Overtime break shows "Start OT" button** and the live loop never advanced past regulation if you only press Resume. Screenshot: `shots/g10-final.png`.
- **A sheet or toast covers "Play options"** ("While you were away", trade offer) and blocks tapping until Escape. Hit this on every sim chunk.
- **Counter "Add" replaces a previous counter silently** (Hedge → Ice) when pressed twice. Fine by design perhaps, but there is no undo cue.
- **Self-inflicted:** I ran `pkill -f headless_shell` once to clear a stuck browser and may have killed other testers' browsers too. Apologies.

## Against the genre (FM / BBGM / MyNBA / Hoop Land)
- **vs FM:** Tactics feedback loops (fit label, verdict, "since you changed it") are as good as FM's analyst feedback and clearer. FM is deeper on roles, training, scouting, set pieces and match-day tweaks. The basketball version lacks per-player instructions beyond a role star and a few sliders. FM's press conference and morale layer are absent by design.
- **vs BBGM:** Much better coaching and cap UX. BBGM sims faster, lets me play 20 seasons in an evening, and has a deeper draft/league loop. Here two seasons took hours of live play.
- **vs MyNBA/MyGM:** Honest odds, advanced explanations and a smarter trade AI. 2K has visuals and licensed players, but its sim tuning is a black box.
- **vs Hoop Land / Draft Day:** More modern and readable, with real reasons attached to results.

## Would I pay and keep playing
- **$7.99 phone unlock: yes**, for the tactics-plus-GM loop and the offline sim.
- **$14.99 on Steam: maybe**, only if the depth below the tactics layer grows (training, scouting, staff roles).
- **Hours:** about 60 to 100 if live coaching moves faster and playoff games are easier to coach.
- **Recommend to a friend: 7/10** (8/10 to an FM fan, 5/10 to a casual).

## The one change that would most raise my score
Make the live assistant call out the biggest problem in the box score and offer a one-tap fix. When I had 21 turnovers to 8 the report still said "Nothing the staff would change right now". The pieces exist (luck versus looks, "your moves" since-scores, counters), so wire the top deciding stat (turnovers, glass, threes) to a suggested tactic with the expected effect, the way the trade desk previews its own impact.
