# 05 — The Win-Now Contender, 24

## Who I am and what I played
Oklahoma City Twisters, real league, GM + Head Coach, job security ON, 390x844 phone viewport, 2D court (kit default). I played four full seasons (2026-27 to 2029-30), 328 regular-season games and four playoff runs, over a long sitting.

Live coaching was thinner than the persona asked for.
- Game 1 of the career was played live, mostly in full, with a Hedge call. Live games take about 8-12 real minutes each on this headless box, so that was the limit.
- In later seasons I drove playoff games live through a script: it took the staff's calls, tapped Hedge at halftime, tapped the "X in" sub prompts and used "Skip ahead". That covered a few R1 and conference-finals games in years 3 and 4.
- I did not hand-coach one complete series. Several game-7 type moments were sim'd because my driver skipped them. Read my coaching verdicts with that in mind.

Zero titles in four years as a 1st seed. League champions were MIN, ORL, ORL and PHI. SGA won MVP in three of four years, and Holmgren was Defensive Player of the Year once.

## Play log
1. **Oct 20, 2026, the opener.** The owner gave goals on day one: 66 wins, conference finals, add nothing to the payroll. The Today card said OKC had only a 50% chance at SAS. I took that as honest, since Wembanyama's team is real, and not as a sign the sim was rigged against me.
2. **Game 1, live.** A 31-21 first quarter. The break board is excellent: shot map, four factors, and a "What's deciding it" list that separates luck (+10.3 pts) from skill (SAS beat my blitz with 4-on-3s). I switched Blitz to Hedge at the half and the sim showed "Changed tonight: Blitz → Hedge" with a Reset. Won comfortably. I felt clever, and I felt like the game was talking to me.
3. **Nov 20.** The "While you were away" sheet said the staff tried Hedge for me and "no clear edge, keeping Blitz". My tactic change had lapsed. It's fair, since I set it for one night, but I didn't expect the staff to override it.
4. **Jan 22, Holmgren asks for a trade.** He wanted 32 minutes and was getting 26.7 under the assistant's rotation. The card offered "Set his minutes / Shop him / He stays". That is a great tension. Fixing it took a fiddly slider (see Bugs), and I briefly put Holmgren at 44 min by accident.
5. **Feb 11, deadline.** The staff offered "Trade for Anthony Davis": JWill (84) + Caruso + Sorber + a 1st for an 86. I declined. I built my own deal for ATL's Jalen Johnson (86, $30M), offering picks plus Caruso and McCain. It came back "Not even close", and "What would make this work?" answered "ATL can't find a version of this they'd take. Ask for less." Win-now trading is walled off for a 45-10 team with a deep, cheap roster.
6. **Apr 2027.** 67-15 and the 1st seed, with MVP, DPOY and Coach of the Year. Yet the owner's Job security read 43, "Warm seat", with the owner trend "falling" (-15 since Mar 17). Winning did not buy trust.
7. **The Cup.** OKC won the In-Season Cup in Vegas (118-91 over DET, SGA Cup MVP) during an 11-game streak. That was a nice moment that did not get any big celebration screen.
8. **Playoffs R1 G1 (live).** The court gets a "PLAYOFFS" paint and a series header ("R1 G1 · Series tied 0-0"). Shipley offered "2-3 Zone, 6 points inside on 5 trips" with an accept chip. I won by 29. It looks and sounds different from the regular season, but only by skin.
9. **May 2027, WCF G1 live** (won by 33). I then sim'd the rest and lost the series 2-4 to the 40-42 MIN team. My sim loop overshot, and the Finals went by without my watching.
10. **Offseason 2027.** Re-signing Wallace (77/82) at $14.8M×5 pushed projected tax to about $234M. Josh Hart (80, MLE $12.75M) would have added **$82.7M** of tax on its own. I skipped him. The tax is brutal.
11. **27-28.** Mitchell (84 OVR) played about 13 minutes a game, because the rotation I had hand-set earlier gave him 0 target after he was re-signed. The game never warned me. Toggling "Let the assistant manage" fixed it, and "Undo" exists.
12. **Apr 2028.** The books said **$201.9M luxury tax paid**. The owner's line was "Make it count in the playoffs". Then I was swept 0-4 by LAL with two stars hurt. This was the low point.
13. **28-29 R1 G1 vs NOP (live).** A real thriller: trailing 105-100 in Q4, SGA fouled out of rotation, a Stirtz sub prompt, and a 128-126 win. The best 10 minutes I had in the game.
14. **29-30 R1 G2 vs HOU (live).** Down 89-83 after Q3, tied 111-111 at the end of regulation, then lost in OT 128-119 ("Houston's shots fell +20.3 pts, luck"). Stakes felt real, and "luck" is stated plainly.
15. **Year 4 West Finals vs SAS.** Won G1 live, 110-103 (SGA was out). I then sim'd the series and lost. The owner's review: "A poor season... One more like it and I stop suggesting and start deciding." A second team (DEN, MIL, TOR, WAS in various years) offers me their coaching job every June, which is a cool poaching hook.

## What hooked me (top 5, each with evidence)
1. **The break board and "What's deciding it".** "OKC +10.3 pts · luck... SAS +3.3 pts, SAS beat the blitz with 4-on-3s." It told me the score was luck, not skill, and it told me what to try. That is better than anything BBGM gives me.
2. **Live playoff games with real swings.** G1 vs NOP (128-126 after trailing 105-100), G2 vs HOU (OT after leading, "+20.3 pts luck"). These were the only moments I felt anything.
3. **Roster tensions that are not just ratings.** Holmgren's trade request over 26.7 vs 32 minutes. The Mitchell zero-minute trap. The staff's "sign cover" cards after injuries to Williams and SGA.
4. **The league is a living world.** Trades happen without me (Booker to MEM, Durant retires, Dončić to LA), league headlines and award news keep coming, and champions are never OKC.
5. **The owner has teeth.** "One more like it and I stop suggesting and start deciding", job security falling every spring, rival teams phoning me with jobs. It all adds real weight.

## What pushed me away (top 5, each marked dealbreaker / major / minor, with evidence)
1. **Major: the luxury tax is absurd.** Projected tax was $234M on a $275M payroll. One $12.75M MLE signing added $82.7M of tax. I paid $201.9M in 2028 and $250.9M of payroll in 2029-30 with the owner still only saying "Spend wisely". For a contender it removes the "keep the core together" fantasy: re-sign asks of $17-20M for 79-84 OVR role players are unaffordable. It also felt like it had no consequence other than the owner's goal score.
2. **Major: the owner's payroll goal is unwinnable for a winner.** "Add nothing to the payroll you inherited" was marked Met in year 1 and Missed every year after, because rookie deals expire and re-sign asks double or triple. A -100 on payroll swamped everything: the 2028 review for 62 wins and a CF game 7 was grade -37, "A poor season". It does not feel like it rewards winning, so I stopped caring about the grade.
3. **Major: a title does not feel earned or even reachable.** Four seasons as the 1st seed gave one Cup and zero rings. I can't tell whether that is variance or the sim holding me back. The champions (MIN at 40-42, ORL twice, PHI) were never the 1-seed. Starting at 96/93/87 and still not winning a title in four years is a lot to ask a fan to accept. The playoffs also use the same match engine and card structure as the regular season, so there is no "playoff intensity" mode (see item 5).
4. **Major: key stars get hurt at the worst times and I can't do anything about it.** Holmgren out about 15 games into the 2028 playoffs, then out again in 2029, and SGA out for WCF G1. The staff said "sign cover", but at the deadline I had no one to sign. I have no way to rest-manage the late season beyond "Rest on back-to-backs".
5. **Minor-to-major: playoffs feel the same as the regular season.** There is a PLAYOFFS floor logo and a series header. There is no series card with a cast, no "clinch" or "elimination" fanfare I could see, and no celebration for the Cup or any series win beyond mail. The one real new thing is the staff's per-opponent scouting plan.

## Bugs (repro steps, screenshot path)
- **Live game stalls at break; "Start Qn" sometimes ignores a programmatic click and works with a real click.** Repro: pause at End of Q3, tap Start Q4 and it works for real taps. (Driver issue on my side: `/players/05-contender/y44-brk1.png`.)
- **"Skip stopped: Williams is hurt and out for the game"** leaves the game paused with a "Resume" button and the subs list. Easy to miss. After leaving and re-entering, the game resumes in a paused state. Screenshot: `players/05-contender/y31-end.png`.
- **Final screen can show but the game stays "Live · Tied" on Today until you tap "Next game".** Leaving from the final frame without that tap left the home card showing a live game. Repro: finish a game, tap Leave, and the Today card still says Live.
- **Manual rotation does not adapt to new roster.** After I hand-edited minutes in Jan 2027, re-signing Ajay Mitchell (85 OVR) left him at **0 target minutes** for 2028-29 with only a "Minutes add up to 190" line (screenshot `s46.png`, `s19.png`). The assistant toggle fixes it.
- **Tactic change on tonight's game did not stick** ("Tried Hedge in place of Blitz. No clear edge. Keeping Blitz." on Nov 20 after I set Hedge). I assume the sheet toggles my one-night override off, but nothing told me.
- **Rotation minutes slider overshoots with keyboard arrows** (Holmgren went 30→44 min when I pressed ArrowRight 40 times). No snapping or sensible cap.
- **Touch targets under the bottom bar.** Many buttons ("Set his minutes", "Decline Jared McCain's option", the trade verdict bar) are overlapped by the bottom nav/Continue bar, so taps don't land until you scroll. A real phone user will notice this.
- **Trade UI.** I got "Not allowed: OKC would have 16 players. The most is 15." while building a trade on deadline day, even with 15/15, since the incoming star made 16. The reaction bar correctly warns, but a one-for-two deal should auto-offer a roster cut.
- **Repeated copy.** "X beat the blitz with quick passes into 4-on-3s" appeared in almost every game against different teams (DAL, MIN, NOP, HOU, SAS), at game 1 and game 2 of a series alike. It reads as boilerplate and becomes noise.

## Against the genre (FM / BBGM / MyNBA / Hoop Land: where it wins and loses for me)
- **Wins over BBGM.** Coaching conversation (staff cards, scouting, "what's deciding it"), a real tactics layer, honest explanations, a living league, and a gorgeous live 2D presentation. BBGM never tells me why I lost.
- **Wins over MyNBA.** Franchise stakes (owner grades, coaching poaches) feel sharper, and the sim is far less script-driven.
- **Loses to FM.** FM's press conferences, dressing room, and long-horizon squad building keep a dynasty alive. Here, after year 2 the roster is static (my top-8 never changed), trades with AI are hard to win, and the tax strangles any moves. FM gives you a reason to rebuild a champion every summer.
- **Loses to Basketball GM on franchise freedom.** In BBGM I can fleece the AI, stack picks and build a super-team. Here the AI won't sell stars to a contender for picks, so "trade picks for help" was my least effective lever.
- **Even with Hoop Land / Draft Day Sports:** a similar depth of pure basketball sim, but this one's UI is much better.

## Would I pay and keep playing
- **$7.99 phone unlock: yes**, on strength of the live games and explanations. I would pay it in a minute.
- **$14.99 on Steam: maybe.** I'd want a title-winning dynasty path, deeper tactical instructions, and a real celebration. Right now I'd wait for a sale.
- **Hours:** about 15 hours in year 1-2, then I'd drop off around year 4 (which is where I stopped). Chasing a title and failing four times in a row is exactly when a contender quits.
- **Recommend to a friend: 7/10.**

## The one change that would most raise my score
Make the luxury tax and the owner's payroll goal fit a winning team. Cap the tax rate, or let me re-sign my own young stars without a -100 on the grade. Then add a visible title celebration and playoff presentation so that the 1-seed's path feels like it ends in a parade that is reachable. Right now the better I build, the more the system fines me.
