# 06 — The Game-Day Coach

## Who I am and what I played
Youth-basketball coach, 37. Real league, East, **Charlotte Wasps**, GM + Head Coach (a mid team: owner wants 37 wins and the play-in). Phone size 390x844 for almost everything, one desktop (1280x800) 3D session and one phone 3D session. I played Oct 21 to Nov 24 of 2026-27: **17 games, 14 coached fully live** (2D court, 4x speed), the rest on staff sims. I did NOT reach 25 live games and never reached the playoffs. A live game takes 9-11 minutes of real time even at 4x, so 25 was not reachable in one sitting. Live record 8-6, staff-simmed 0-3, team 8-8, 6th in the East, +1.1 a game. About 4 hours wall clock, most of it waiting on live games.

## Play log
1. **Oct 21, opener at BKN (L 125-127).** Release-2-players gate was clear and one tap (dead money $10.7M stated). Played with no game plan. Down 20-28 in Q1 on a "BKN 12-2 run" I called a timeout. The huddle card said "Brooklyn's shots fell above their looks. BKN +14.0 pts, luck. That part is luck, with nothing to change." I loved that: a coach is told straight that nothing is fixable. We lost by 2; the report said -11 pts of shooting luck. First time I believed a sim's excuse.
2. The Summary report is the best thing in the game: Tactics -6.8 pts / Shooting luck -9.6 pts, "What decided it" with point values, Four Factors, "Your moves" (every sub and timeout with the score swing since: "Timeout, CHA 16-2 since"), "Next time: Brooklyn hunted switches. Go to Drop: every game" with a one-tap button. This is a real coaching feedback loop.
3. **Staff sub suggestions ping-pong.** The Now card says "Tired or in foul trouble: Kalkbrenner / 2 fouls / Steinbach in". I took it; two possessions later "Steinbach in / Kalkbrenner in". Kalkbrenner/Steinbach swapped four times in one half. Accepting these blindly produced silly rotations (Miller 37 min, 33 pts on 11-28).
4. **Oct 22 to 26, W vs ATL 126-104, W vs MIA 124-112.** Won by 12+ twice. In the ATL game I called a timeout in the 4th at +22 against a "10-1 run": a wasted timeout, because my rule fired on the run label, not the game state. The game happily let me do it.
5. **Oct 30, W 143-122 vs WAS, 134-132 win in a game that went long, 151-143 OT win vs PHI.** Scores of 143 and 151 for a Wasps team are not NBA 2026. League-leading scorer Jamal Murray averages 37.2 ppg at Nov 24, Dončić 32.3, Mitchell 30.7. Pace/scoring feels inflated about 10-15%.
6. **Run calls.** The Now card shows "BKN on a 7-0 run: Momentum is theirs: they shoot better until the run ends. A timeout ends it." with Timeout / Not now / "Could go either way". Direct, game-changing, and it actually was a fun lever. But I could never tell if my timeout stopped anything. Run lengths of 10-12 kept appearing after timeouts in 6 of my 16 timeouts.
7. **Nov 9, L 102-129 vs BOS and L vs CLE (CLE +30 in the paint).** Prescott's line stayed "We have 7 more threes, but they're +30 in the paint. Nothing the staff would change right now." Giving up +30 inside, with a Kalkbrenner-led team, and the assistant has no suggestion. At 4th quarter it gave "Cleveland's zone clogged driving lanes: beat zones with Motion & cuts" which is a real note, but 3 quarters late.
8. **Nov 17, a GSW trade ask (Anderson Jr. for Melton) landed on Today** mid-sim. Fine, turned it down in one tap.
9. **Crunch time.** I only saw the late-game play-call card once, in the CLE game: "Iso: Miller / Hammer: Miller / Elevator: Miller / Floppy: Knueppel", each with a one-line description. This is what I came for. But I only saw it once in 14 games; the others had a "Sim to the end" offer instead (Q4, 5:46), which my script grabbed by accident in game 1 and ended the game early (game 1 was decided by 2 and I never saw the finish: a real "oops" risk for a button that sits first in the list).
10. **Nov 20, 128-125 over LAL (11 team fouls each, ended with 0 timeouts left).** A tight finish; the clock, foul and timeout counts on the scorebug held up. I could not find a hack-a-player or intentional-foul trigger live except pre-game under Special plans.

## What hooked me
1. **The luck line**: "BKN +14.0 pts · luck. That part is luck, with nothing to change." (timeout huddle, game 1) and the postgame "Shooting luck -9.6 pts". A coach finally gets told what is variance.
2. **Postgame "Your moves" with since-score** after each sub/timeout. I started checking whether my changes helped.
3. **3D desktop court**: tip-off still, a half-court set and a shot all read as real arena TV: crowd, bench, refs, a labeled ball-handler, sponsor boards. Faces and uniforms look credible.
4. **Run/momentum cards that say what a timeout does**, plus "Drop on ball screens: The staff thinks this helps from here: Their ball screens 5 points on 5 trips". Evidence-led, one tap, not-now alternative.
5. **Late-game play-call menu** (Iso/Hammer/Elevator/Floppy with plain-English descriptions).

## What pushed me away
1. **Major: the live game is slow and mostly passive.** ~10 real minutes per game at the max 4x; no 8x; no way to jump to just before the next decision except "Key plays"/"Skip". With an 82-game season, I cannot coach every game fully. There is no "coach the last 6 minutes only" jump.
2. **Major: staff sub suggestions flip-flop** (Kalkbrenner/Steinbach in and out four times; "Reid in / Knueppel in" back to back), and a one-tap accept invites silly lineups. A real coach wants substitution *patterns*: a stint plan or minutes target.
3. **Major: the assistant's voice stays quiet when it matters.** "Nothing the staff would change right now" while down +30 in the paint (CLE) or +24 (BOS). The zone advice came in Q4.
4. **Minor: timeouts feel unproven.** 16 timeouts, and opponents often rolled on; the huddle gives text but no number for what the timeout was worth.
5. **Minor: the phone 2D court is a cropped half-court slice** with the ball often off the edge; the live action shifts under you. 3D is clearly better, and portrait 3D's court strip is only ~380px tall.

## Bugs
- **Live clock/period undefined at quarter breaks.** On the break screen the scorebug showed the finished-quarter state; my parser only read it between plays (that is a test-harness issue, not a game bug).
- **Skip menu lists "Sim to the end" as its first button.** Easy to hit (cost me the finish of game 1).
- **Duplicated sub cards.** "Reid in || Reid in", "Salaün in || Salaün in" — two buttons for the same sub (Q2, game 2).
- **Headless-only**: "3D is running slowly on this device" banner shows during 3D play; not a bug for real devices.

## Against the genre
- **vs Football Manager**: FM's match day has shouts, touchline instructions and a quiet assistant; this has similar bones (instruction cards, assistant, post-match analysis) and the *luck vs tactics* breakdown is better than FM's. FM's match day is less passive, though, and its sim speed is far better.
- **vs Basketball GM**: BBGM has no live coaching, so this is far ahead on game day; it is behind on pace (BBGM plays a game in a second).
- **vs NBA 2K MyGM**: 2K's live control is far deeper but is about the stick, not the coach's brain; this is the coach's seat. The 3D court is near 2K quality of stills.
- **vs Hoop Land / DDS**: much more game-day insight than those.

## Would I pay and keep playing
$7.99 on phone: yes, for the report screens and the luck line alone. $14.99 on Steam: only if there is a faster live mode (or I could play only the key minutes). Hours: I'd put 25-40 hours in if games could be coached at 5 minutes. Recommend to a friend: **7/10**.

## The one change that would most raise my score
Let me coach just the decisive stretch: add a "Jump to the last 8 minutes" / "Skip to next call" that stops at runs, foul trouble and crunch time, and have the staff's sub suggestions respect a plan (minutes + fouls) instead of flip-flopping. That turns 10 minutes a game into the 3 minutes a coach cares about.
