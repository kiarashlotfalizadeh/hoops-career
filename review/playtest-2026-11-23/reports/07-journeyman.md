# 07 — The Career Journeyman
## Who I am and what I played
Washington Mages (real league, East), GM + Head Coach, job security on, default owner. I took a weak-to-average roster. The owner's first target was "win about 35". I played **11 full seasons, 2026-27 to 2036-37, about 900 games**, all by sim. I coached 0 live games. Device was a 390x844 phone viewport, with 3D off. It took roughly 5 hours of wall clock, and most of that was the stops inside "sim to end of season". I also ran `season-test` and `star-check` once each.

Final record: **394-508 (.437), 3 playoff trips (all first-round exits), 0 titles**. I was never fired, and I never saw a job offer.

## Play log
1. **2026-27, Oct to Nov.** The opener went to 5-10, which flattened the early hope. By Nov 17 I had 14th in the East and a 70% TOR forecast. The BOS three-team offer for Johnson was clear, and I declined. I finished 36-46 and lost the play-in on Apr 17. Owner grade was +17 against "win 33", so he was happy with a 36-46 team. The Today card was lively all year: Davis scored his 20,000th point, a 19% upset of Orlando, a 140-136 OT win.
2. **Draft 2027.** I held #11. The best available was Keith Palmer, 72 OVR with POT 82-84. The board, "Big board", "Combine", "Mock" and "tap a pick to trade for it" all felt like a real draft room.
3. **2028 re-sign (my own blunder, and a feature).** My script let Sarr, George and Champagnie walk to free agency. The game had warned me twice: "Open free agency? 1 player becomes free agent" and "Re-signing period: you have 5 expiring contracts". I bid on Sarr (78/85) at his ask, then again with a "Lead with" premium, and lost him anyway. The inbox said: "Alex Sarr chose Cleveland over us. Their offer 4 yrs $23.3M. Our offer $24.6M. He liked their situation better. Winning matters most." Every loss came with a reason and both offers. **This is the best part of free agency.**
4. **Seasons 2-3.** 35-47 and 34-48, each ending in the play-in or a miss, with Trae Young fading from 87 to 85 to 81 (age 28 to 31). Owner security stayed 50-59 (the +7 grade). The draft picks developed. Marshall (#2 in 2029) is 84 at his peak, and Hampton (#3 in 2030) is 83-84.
5. **Deliberate tank, 2029-30.** I benched Young, Johnson, Moody, Coulibaly and Dybantsa behind Hunt (52), Palmer and the rest. The game warned me ("Starting five: no real creator, ball handling 72, Young is on the bench, ball handling 98"). Dybantsa then **asked for a trade**: "expects 32 minutes, plays 21.8". Result: **23-59, owner grade -87, security 53 to 35 "Warm seat"**, fans "Fed Up", arena 83% full. I was not fired.
6. **2030-31, the owner's ultimatum moment.** This was a full-screen story card: "Win 34 games, or the summer's big announcement is your replacement." I was 23 of 34 on Feb 5 and finished 35-47. I survived by one win while still tanking. It was the best narrative beat of the career layer, but I could not make it bite.
7. **Career, Jobs tab.** It reads: "Teams changing coaches call after the playoffs, if they rate you above their hire. God mode in Settings puts you anywhere." In 11 seasons, including 51-31 in 2033-34 and three playoff trips, I never got a call. I could not see my own "rating", or why it was below their hire.
8. **2033-34, 51-31** (6th seed, lost in round 1). The record book noted a franchise first: "Team threes WAS 141-123 vs DAL Mar 13, 2033, 30 made".
9. **2035-36, deep tank.** I set the five best players' target minutes to 0 and five scrubs to 44. The game told me "Minutes add up to 220, games scale to 240", and "Johnson isn't getting any minutes". Result: **28-54, grade -69, security 51 to 37**. The owner **re-baselined**: the next preseason he "will take a rebuild this season" with a 31-win goal. A 33-49 season then graded -6 "as expected". I never got below 35.
10. **Season 10-11 world check.** I finished with a snapshot of the league (see the drift table below). A year later it had split into extremes: **NYK 3-79** (net -22.9, ORtg 94), POR 13-69, DET 20-62, against PHX 61-21 (ORtg 131, eFG 61.0%) and CHA 66-16.

- **Top-10 players** went from 90-97 OVR (2026) to 91-99 OVR (2036). Buzelis went 91 at 23, to 95 at 26, to 99 at 28. Top-30 average OVR stayed 89-90 with an average age of 28, and the number of 90+ players stayed 11-14. That is no real inflation, but the ceiling creeps up two points.
- **Aging is believable.** Dončić went 97 at 32, 94, then 91 at 34. SGA went 96 to 93 by 33. Young went 87, 85, 81 by 31.
- **Cap and money.** Cap was $174.9M in 2026-27 and about $313M in 2036-37 (6% a year). Payroll went from $191M to $320M (WAS, the 9th highest). A star asks $42M (Johnson 82 OVR, Dybantsa 87/90). That is 9 different champions in 11 years, with no dynasty, and the star-check agrees (most titles by one franchise OKC 11 of 64). To a fan, it can read as randomness more than a dynasty era. There is no "the Wembanyama Spurs" story beyond one MVP trophy. Note the SAS coach row: "47-199 (.191)" over three seasons.
- **Draft classes:** #11 in 2027 was 72/84. The 2037 board has #1 Mbappé 75/77-91, #2 Daniels 79/85-93, #12 Gibson 78/83-87. That is a slight inflation of rookie ratings, but not broken.

Real legends retire at believable ages, with real names and stats. The record book mixes real history ("Wilt 100", "Since 1946-47") with the league's own.

## What hooked me (top 5)
1. **Reasons for every free-agency loss.** "Chose Cleveland over us. Their offer 4 yrs $23.3M. Ours $24.6M. Winning matters most." That is what FM and BBGM never tell you, and it made me want to bid smarter (inbox, Jul 1 2028).
2. **The world ages like a real league.** Stars rise and fall on believable curves, LeBron retires after 25 seasons and 46,080 points, and the Hall of Fame fills with real names. I recognised the history without being told.
3. **Owner pulse and ultimatum.** The owner changes his goals with me ("he'll take a rebuild this season"), and the ultimatum card put real tension into a 23-of-34 start.
4. **The record book and signature wins.** My own record showed up ("Team threes WAS 141-123, 30 made"). Career signature wins list "Beat 2-1 Orlando as an 8% underdog". Franchise leaders, coaches and Hall of Fame pages make a 10-year save feel lived in.
5. **Stability across 11 years.** Pace 97.4-98.5, 3PA rate 41.5-42.8, ORtg 112.7-115.0 and a top-30 average rating of 89-90 held steady. I never hit the "10 years later it is nonsense" wall I feared.

## What pushed me away (top 5)
1. **Dealbreaker for my persona: I cannot get fired, so there is no job market.** Evidence: grades of -87 (23-59) and -69 (28-54), with a Warm seat of 35-37. The owner then re-baselines ("takes a rebuild") and security recovers. Eleven seasons produced zero job offers, even after 51-31 and three playoff trips. The "Jobs" tab is two sentences. For a Journeyman this is the whole career fantasy missing.
2. **Major: about 15-20 stops per season in "Sim to end of regular season".** Each one is a staff call, injury, offer, Cup or deadline stop. Over 20 seasons that is hundreds of taps. There is no "auto-handle staff calls" setting, and the calls were nearly always "Ride it out".
3. **Major: re-sign and free-agency flow is easy to botch on a long save.** One tap on "Open free agency" can drop your three best young players. The warning is there, but I would want a "you are about to lose Sarr 78/85" blocker. Bids at the ask lose to a better "situation", which is fair, but I only learned that after the fact.
4. **Minor/major: tails widen late.** NYK 3-79 (ORtg 94, net -22.9), DEN 9-73, POR 13-69, SAS coach 47-199 (.191). The real worst ever is 9-73, and a 3-win season would be a deranged one. Meanwhile BKN went 66-16 at 127.2 ppg and PHX had an eFG of 61.0%. Parity at the top, but extremes at both ends in the final years.
5. **Minor: tanking gives little feedback.** When I benched five stars with 0 target minutes, the team still won 28 and later 33. "Minutes add up to 50, scaled to 240" is honest, but "Johnson isn't getting any minutes" is a warning, not a block. Also, deliberate tanking is a very mild penalty: fans "Fed Up", arena 83% full.

## Against the genre (FM / BBGM / MyNBA / Hoop Land)
- **vs Basketball GM:** BBGM lets you get fired and offers jobs with a click. Hoops Career has better texture (real names, owner pulse, reasons for FA losses, the record book), but is weaker on career mobility. BBGM's multi-year trend screens are also better.
- **vs Football Manager:** FM's "sacked, apply for vacancies, board confidence" loop is what I wanted. Here the board patience is too forgiving. FM's pain in a rebuild is real, and Hoops Career softens it.
- **vs NBA 2K MyGM/MyNBA:** this game's league history and Hall of Fame pages are more honest and less random than 2K's. No flashy cutscenes, but the "story moments" are more restrained.
- **vs Hoop Land / Draft Day Sports:** a similar nerd-friendly world. Here the day-to-day is smoother on a phone.

## Would I pay and keep playing
- **$7.99 phone unlock:** yes. A 10-season save held together, and it fits on a phone.
- **$14.99 on Steam:** only if the job market and the firing loop are real; with the current career layer, probably not yet.
- **Hours:** about 40-60 before I would stall, mainly on the draft and rebuild loop.
- **Recommend to a friend:** 7/10.

## The one change that would most raise my score
**Make the owner bite and make the job market real.** If two bad seasons (a -87 and a -69) put me on the street, and then "Jobs" showed named vacancies with a visible reputation score and why each team rates me, a Journeyman save would have a spine. Right now the world is honest for 11 years, but my own career never changes teams.
