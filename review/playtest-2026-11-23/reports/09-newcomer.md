# 09 — The Crossover Newcomer (19, 2K MyNBA yearly, tried Basketball GM, never played FM)

## Who I am and what I played
- Career 1: real league, Utah Swing (default pick), GM + Head Coach, Teaching ON, phone 390x844. One full season (30-52, 13th West, no playoffs, lottery and draft reached, staff pick at #20), about 30 game-steps plus 2 live games (opener at MEM, game 29 at BOS). The 3D court was never turned off in the opener (software render); I used the 2D court after that.
- Career 2: fictional league, Utah Swing, same defaults. First 20 games (7-12 after 19), 1 live game (vs DEN, won 132-130), 1 staff call (Powell broken hand).
- Rough time: about 2.5 hours of real time, but a newcomer's "first hour" fits inside the first 12 in-game steps.

## Play log
1. **Start screen (`/start`).** Clean: "New league", 30 teams, 82 games. "Real 2026-27 rosters / Fictional league" is clear. Team step shows a roster rank ("20th of 30") and the owner's mood ("Owner: 29 wins · rebuild · builder"). I did not know what "builder" or "rebuild" meant. Step 3 then showed "32 wins · rebuild · win-now": the owner changed between steps (29/builder to 32/win-now), which looked like a bug.
2. **Today, preseason.** The owner card ("Win about 32 games / Give about 10,000 minutes to players 23 or under") is clear. The "Your first games" checklist (rotation, playbook, plan, coach live) is the best thing in the first hour: 4 taps, each ticks off. I never wondered what to do next. Tapping the "?" on a rotation header opens a plain-English "How rotations work" sheet. Teaching works.
3. **Playbook picker.** "Staff's read: Similar fit / Worse fit" per style, like a 2K playbook with a coach telling you what fits. I understood this at once. "Hedge / Ice / Blitz / Drop" ball-screen coverage was Greek to me. The "Staff still looking…" on every counter before game 1 felt dead.
4. **Opener, live (Oct 21, @ MEM).** The 2D court is small (top 40% of the screen) and plain next to 2K, but the scorebug, win-chance bar and a coach card ("Trent Novak: Early. Watching how they want to play.") hold my attention. Real time: about 110 s per quarter at 1x, so a full game is about 7 minutes. "Skip ahead" (End of Q1 / Halftime / Clutch / Sim to final) is great. First hook: the quarter-break report with a shot chart, "UTA +10 pts · luck", and at half the opposing coach "ices George's ball screens". Final 127-103, "Won by 24". Hooked for about 20 steps.
5. **Post-game screen.** "Tactics −0.9 pts / Shooting luck +16.5 pts" is clever but deflating: a 24-point win was mostly "luck". It uses eFG%, TOV%, ORB%, FT/FGA with no explanation on the card. For a 2K player this is the first "homework" moment.
6. **Checklist stays on "Coach a game live (next)"** even after I coached the whole game, because I only watched and sim'd. It says "ticks when you make the call", but I felt cheated.
7. **Slide (Oct 22 to Nov 12): 5-7, "Six straight losses" mail.** "While you were away" recap is a mini-newspaper (results, injuries, power-rank 12th) and is the best "week summary" I have seen. The offers sheet and staff Needs-you card are lively.
8. **Trade offer text I could not read (Nov 12, MEM wants Collier for Jackson II).** "Staff's read of your need, three-point d: F → F (28th → 29th in the league), read with the staff's minutes both ways... your setting 'Help defense: Balanced' costs it (D without)". I had no idea. Turned down.
9. **Followed the staff's "Change-up" suggestion (2-3 Zone), then 6-5 in the next stretch.** Season Analysis ("Drop coverage: Cost you", "Zone defense: Helped") is a real feedback loop, and it works. Newcomer verdict: I liked it. After picking Zone, every other style flips to "Worse fit"; a bit abrupt.
10. **Accepted ATL's Green + 2nd for Dort (Dec 4, 11-12).** A two-tap confirm. Then 1-4: Markkanen and Jackson Jr. were both injured and I had no way to "feel" the Dort effect.
11. **Live at BOS (Dec 18).** Up 60-53 at half. The Tactics tab ("What BOS is running; Hurting you: Isolation, Pressure") is a real FM-like moment. I changed Ball pressure to Pressure at the half; lost 111-113. The game report lists "Your moves: −0.6 pts, Half: Ball pressure Balanced → Pressure, UTA 49-60 since". This is a brilliant answer to "did my call matter". But the same report says "Shooting luck +37.3 pts" and Boston "−41.4 pts" on looks. Those magnitudes don't reconcile with a 2-point game (see Bugs).
12. **Deadline (Feb 6, 21-32).** Staff call "sell Markkanen". The Trading block shows CLE giving Evan Mobley (85, age 25) + a 2033 1st for Markkanen (86, 29), both "Fair value"; I made the trade (two taps; "a first-round pick moves, can't be undone"). This was the best 2K-style fantasy moment in the game: I felt like a genius GM.
13. **End of season: 30-52, owner "holding / Steady", Peterson All-Rookie 1st Team.** Sim to end of regular season took about 3 s. The draft lottery has a "Reveal the order / Skip to the order" screen that I (as a script) skipped. At #20 the staff picked "Aaron Long". No face, no scouting excitement, and "who?"
14. **Career 2 (fictional).** The team picker shows "M. Powell 89, T. Lawrence 83, B. Dort 83". All generic names. The starting five reads SG, SF, C, C, C (three centers). Game 1 win: 109-94. After 19 games: 7-12, 12th West. League leaders: Dylan Newton (DEN, C) 42.3 PPG, 12.8 reb, 3.6 blk, PER 50.6, TS 71.5. Houston +15.6 pts/game. Did not hook me (see the genre section).

## What hooked me (top 5)
1. **The "Your first games" checklist** (0/4 to 3/4 in under 10 taps) with "?" sheets such as "How rotations work". I was never lost.
2. **Quarter-break and halftime reports**: shot chart, "What's deciding it", the opposing coach's adjustment ("ices George's ball screens"), and the post-game "Your moves: Ball pressure Balanced → Pressure, UTA 49-60 since". It answers "did my choice matter" better than any 2K game I've played.
3. **Trade market**: Trading block "Best package from every team" gave me Mobley + a 1st for a 29-year-old injured Markkanen in four taps.
4. **"While you were away"** stretch recaps (record, rank drop 1st → 7th, power rank, results with newspaper headlines).
5. **Skip ahead** inside live games, and Resume at quarter breaks, so I could pick my moments and not watch 7 minutes every game.

## What pushed me away (top 5)
1. **Jargon walls (major).** eFG%, TOV%, ORB%, FT/FGA, "on/off −51.1" after one game, "ORtg/DRtg", ball-screen coverages "Hedge/Ice/Blitz/Drop", and the trade text "three-point d: F → F (28th → 29th in the league), read with the staff's minutes both ways". A 2K player taps away from these.
2. **Numbers that don't reconcile (major).** "Shooting luck +37.3 pts" and Boston "−41.4 pts" in a 111-113 game. A newcomer's reaction: "luck is 37 points? Then why did I lose?" I stopped trusting the luck line.
3. **The fictional league (major for a public release).** Generic names, positions like three Cs in one starting five, a 42.3 PPG center with PER 50.6 and a +15.6 pts/game team after 19 games. As a newcomer it looks broken and has no stars to care about.
4. **The 2D court and the draft (minor/major).** The live court is small and plain, and the draft is "The staff took Aaron Long" with no face or scouting thrill. 2K wins on flash; this game has no substitute for it in those two spots.
5. **Resume live game (minor).** Leave mid-quarter, resume, and the game restarts from the last saved break (Q1 tip). The Today pill says "Q1 · Tied 0-0" so it is honest, but I lost 25 s of play twice. Also "Coach a game live" never ticked after I watched a whole game.

## Against the genre (FM / BBGM / MyNBA / Hoop Land)
- **vs MyNBA/2K:** loses on flash (court, faces, draft) and on instant familiarity (no LeBron/Curry on the box, invented nicknames); wins on "why" (halftime, "Your moves") and on how fast a season goes (3 s). The trade block beats 2K's trade AI feel.
- **vs Basketball GM:** BBGM is quicker to read (OVR/POT, one page). Here the first hour is longer but better taught; the checklist beats BBGM's blank slate. BBGM never explains luck; this game does (even if the numbers look wrong).
- **vs FM:** it has the staff cards, the owner pressure, the "staff's read" fit and "Needs you" queue that I imagine FM has, without 40 menus. I would say it is "FM with the homework taken out", and then it puts some homework back in the analytics card.
- **Fictional league vs real:** the real league hooked me (Markkanen/Mobley/Giannis trade news); the fictional one did not.

## Would I pay and keep playing
- **$7.99 phone unlock:** yes, if the real-roster league is what I get; I would pay for the trade block and the halftime reports alone. About 10 to 15 hours before I'd bounce, more if I find a rebuild loop.
- **$14.99 on Steam:** maybe. I would want a bigger court and faces. Hours: 20 if the fictional league was fixed and the draft had some faces.
- **Recommend to a friend, 0-10:** 7 (8 to a BBGM friend, 5 to a pure 2K friend).

## The one change that would most raise my score
Make every number a newcomer sees explain itself in one tap and then fix the luck line: tap "Shooting luck +37.3 pts" and "eFG%" for a one-sentence plain version, and make the magnitudes reconcile with the final score. That is where I felt "homework", and where a wrong-looking number cost the game its trust.
